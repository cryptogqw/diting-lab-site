About

A daily digest of the latest papers in cryptography, crypto and AI, plus regulatory and tax documents from Japan, Mainland China, Hong Kong, Singapore, the US, the UAE, the EU and the UK. Every item keeps its original title, with an explanation and summary in English, Chinese and Japanese.

How it works

Public paper metadata and regulatory documents are collected on a schedule that follows each site's access rules, always linking back to the original. The AI reads only the original text to write each explanation and summary, without speculation or opinion. Papers are grouped by field and regulation by jurisdiction; papers stay on the site for about three months.

Explanations and summaries are generated by AI from the original text. Dates, rates and amounts are checked against the source, but errors are still possible, so always rely on the original. Nothing here is legal, tax or investment advice.

About the stars

Stars are a rough AI assessment based on the title and abstract: each paper is scored for novelty, strength of evidence and breadth of impact, then mapped to 1–5 stars (about 2% get five stars and 10% get four). The site only marks four- and five-star papers as Notable, with a one-line reason; no mark does not mean a paper is weak. The full text is not read, so treat the stars as a reading aid, not a verdict on quality. "Code" means the abstract states that code, data or tools are released.
