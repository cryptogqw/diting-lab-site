Silent Payments for Multiparty Wallets

In briefSilent Payments protocol proposal for multiparty wallets

SummaryA Delving Bitcoin post proposing a protocol that lets multiparty wallets make secure Silent Payments. Each signer's ECDH contribution is combined as a weighted sum, and AggDLEQ aggregates their proofs into a single BIP-374 proof. The author states the security proofs are under internal review and not yet published.
