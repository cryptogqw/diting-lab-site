ERC draft: Account Authority Lifecycle for STVA

In briefERC Draft: Account Authority Lifecycle for STVA

SummaryThis draft proposes a standard model and interface covering the full lifecycle of authority over an account, including issuance, activation, attenuation, review, suspension, revocation, and recovery. It aims to prevent stale owners, modules, or keys from retaining authority beyond their intended purpose. High-risk authority changes must pass independent simulation and be authorized through Scoped Capability Tokens.

Eltoo state chain on signet, third time: TEMPLATEHASH + CSFS + INTERNALKEY

In briefEltoo state chain on signet, third build using TEMPLATEHASH, CSFS and INTERNALKEY

SummaryThe author rebuilt a small Alice/Bob/Carol Eltoo state channel on signet for the third time, this version using TEMPLATEHASH, CSFS and INTERNALKEY. The run deliberately published an old state and then replaced it with a newer update. It compares update-leaf and witness sizes across APO+CTV, CSFS+CTV and TEMPLATEHASH, and argues that TEMPLATEHASH returns the template hash to the script so the update script needs only three opcodes.

PropAMM fills on Ethereum mainnet and PropAMM support for TEMBRI (toolbox): same-block quotes, a small spread, and flow taken from lagging AMMs

In briefPropAMM fills on Ethereum mainnet and TEMBRI toolbox support: same-block quotes and tight spreads

SummaryThe post analyses 92,627 fills across nine Ethereum PropAMMs, on-chain venues priced by a single maker, over 30 days from 10 September to 9 October 2026. Three venues accept quotes only when stamped with the current block's timestamp, so every fill executes against an update in the same block. Takers paid about 0.45 bp at the fill, and prices did not move in their favour afterwards.
