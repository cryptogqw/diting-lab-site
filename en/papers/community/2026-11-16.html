Delving Bitcoin Nov 16, 2026 #

Toward Efficient Post-Quantum Bitcoin Bridges

In briefGarbled STARK verifier for efficient post-quantum Bitcoin bridges

SummaryThis Delving Bitcoin discussion presents a garbled-circuit verifier for hash-based STARK proofs, offered as a post-quantum alternative to garbling a Groth16 verifier in BitVM3-style bridges. The authors measure on-chain and off-chain costs using Ethereum mainnet block 26,206,588. The off-chain garbled verifier is about 15 times smaller than BitVM3's Boolean Groth16 verifier, while publishing the proof input on-chain costs over a thousand times BitVM3's Assert.

Delving Bitcoin Nov 16, 2026 #

Silent Payments for Electrum

In briefspscand: a Silent Payments scanner for Electrum

SummaryThe author previously released a Silent Payments sending plugin for Electrum and now introduces spscand, a companion scanner. It finds payments to a user's sp1 address, tracks their spends and reorgs, and reports them through a local JSON API and a webhook. According to the post, it never queries a server about addresses, outputs or transactions, and blocks can come from a user's own node, a blindbit server or BIP352 filters, with Tor supported.
