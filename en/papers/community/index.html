In briefReveal optionality in encrypted mempools and a post-quantum way to remove it

SummaryThis forum post examines how much value a sender gains from controlling whether to reveal a transaction in an encrypted mempool, and why fees alone and forced opening do not fully remove this option. The author proposes Blind Simultaneous Release (BSR), which requires participants to commit before seeing new information and uses only hash functions, making it post-quantum. The accompanying paper has its theorems machine-checked in Lean 4, but the hardware speed assumption it relies on has not been measured.
