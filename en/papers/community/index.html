Lean verification of EIP-7002: system-call success and FIFO requests

In briefLean verification of EIP-7002: guaranteed system-call success and FIFO withdrawal requests

SummaryThis ethresear.ch post reports Lean 4 proofs about the deployed bytecode of EIP-7002, the execution-layer contract for validator exits and partial withdrawals. The proofs show the end-of-block system call always succeeds regardless of storage, using at most 210,000 gas. They also show requests are emitted exactly in arrival order. The results are stated against the Jaune EVM semantics and cover the Prague, Osaka, BPO1 and BPO2 forks.

Cost-backed Sybil resistance for self-issued identifiers

In briefProposal for cost-backed Sybil resistance in self-issued identifiers

SummaryThis Delving Bitcoin discussion proposes a construction that makes bulk production of self-issued identifiers costly by requiring a memory-intensive process over a 256 MiB terrain, bound to consecutive Bitcoin headers. CLN: Self-Referential Channel Announcement DoS disclosure

In briefCLN: Self-Referential Channel Announcement DoS Disclosure

SummaryCore Lightning v25.02 through v26.04 crash on a channel_announcement whose two node IDs are identical, and the node stays down across restarts until an operator manually removes the stored record. An attacker needs only an unspent 2-of-2 output, so the attack costs nothing beyond on-chain fees. The fix in v26.04.1 rejects announcements where node_id_1 is not lexicographically less than node_id_2.
