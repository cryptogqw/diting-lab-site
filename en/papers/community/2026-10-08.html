The unleashed power of stacking EEZ & FCR seen through an example

In briefCombining FCR and EEZ: faster cross-layer payments on Ethereum without a bridge

SummaryThis ethresear.ch post uses a checkout example to show how the Fast Confirmation Rule (FCR) and the Ethereum Economic Zone (EEZ) work together. FCR shortens the wait before a block can be trusted, while EEZ removes the separate crossing between a rollup and the base layer. Used together, the author says a payment can confirm in about two slots, roughly 25 seconds, without trusting a bridge.

Price of Censorship Resistance in Multiple Proposer Protocols Under Sybils

In briefCost of Censorship Resistance Under Sybil Attacks in Multiple Proposer Protocols

SummaryThis forum post discusses a paper on the cost of censorship in multiple proposer protocols, which derives the price and bounds for effectively censoring transactions under different transaction fee mechanisms. The author examines how these bounds change when Sybil nodes are present. The post also considers what the altered conclusions imply for multiple concurrent proposer (MCP) protocols.

Ethereum PropAMMs: today and tomorrow

In briefEthereum PropAMMs today and tomorrow: how on-chain market makers compare across Ethereum and Solana

SummaryThis Ethereum Research forum post compares PropAMMs, on-chain venues where market makers update quoted prices directly, on Ethereum and Solana.
