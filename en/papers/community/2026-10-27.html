In briefProof of One: an open polling design using encrypted ballots and passport-based identity, seeking critical review

Inventory of PQ P2P Migrations in the Consensus Layer

In briefInventory of Post-Quantum P2P Migration Challenges in Ethereum's Consensus Layer

SummaryThis Ethereum Research forum post takes stock of the challenges facing post-quantum migration for consensus-layer peer-to-peer networking, including Discovery v5, ENR, libp2p, and QUIC/TLS 1.3. It argues that replacing validator BLS signatures alone does not update networking identities or handshakes. The post notes structural limits such as the 300-byte ENR and 1,280-byte Discovery packet size. It maps the required changes without selecting an algorithm, encoding, or activation schedule.

Lean verification of EIP-7002: system-call success and FIFO requests

In briefLean verification of EIP-7002: guaranteed system-call success and FIFO withdrawal requests

SummaryThis ethresear.ch post reports Lean 4 proofs about the deployed bytecode of EIP-7002, the execution-layer contract for validator exits and partial withdrawals. The proofs show the end-of-block system call always succeeds regardless of storage, using at most 210,000 gas. They also show requests are emitted exactly in arrival order. The results are stated against the Jaune EVM semantics and cover the Prague, Osaka, BPO1 and BPO2 forks.

Cost-backed Sybil resistance for self-issued identifiers

In briefProposal for cost-backed Sybil resistance in self-issued identifiers

SummaryThis Delving Bitcoin discussion proposes a construction that makes bulk production of self-issued identifiers costly by requiring a memory-intensive process over a 256 MiB terrain, bound to consecutive Bitcoin headers. The produced object records this evidence, and an independent verifier can measure it against the specification and recompute a random sample of six legs. The author notes that whether the construction imposes a meaningful marginal physical cost under adversarial conditions remains an open question and invites critical review.
