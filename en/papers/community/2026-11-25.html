Optimal Dynamic Fees for L2s: A Vault Approach

In briefOptimal Dynamic Fees for L2s: A Vault Approach

SummaryThis Ethereum Research forum post summarizes a paper on how layer-2 networks should set transaction fees to recover Ethereum posting costs. It compares proportional fee rules with a linear-quadratic regulator rule that keeps part of the previous fee, trading off vault tracking against fee smoothness. The authors also show how to choose the memory coefficient and compare Arbitrum's rule with replayed historical Ethereum prices.

FOCIL and Ethereum's PQ transition

In briefFOCIL and Ethereum's Post-Quantum Transition

A Bitcoin Quantum Roadmap: Combining BIP-361 and PACTs to Preserve Ownership Rights

In briefA Bitcoin quantum roadmap combining BIP-361 and PACTs to protect ownership

SummaryThis Delving Bitcoin forum post proposes a quantum-security roadmap that combines BIP-361 with Dan Robinson's PACT proposal. It outlines introducing quantum-resistant addresses and anonymous PACTs, freezing vulnerable legacy spending after a migration window, and allowing recovery through matured PACTs. The author suggests this approach balances quantum protection with preserving legitimate ownership rights.

676-Byte Knowledge-Asymmetry UTXO Rescue and Stateless Lattice PQ-MPC for Legacy Supply Migration

In briefKnowledge-Asymmetry UTXO Rescue and Stateless Post-Quantum Threshold Signing for Legacy Bitcoin

SummaryThe author posts on Delving Bitcoin about two techniques in the AQMP v2.0 protocol. The first rescues frozen legacy UTXOs using knowledge asymmetry from the unhardened BIP-32 parent chain code, with a total on-chain witness of 676 bytes. The second is a stateless post-quantum threshold signing engine for institutional custody, using Shamir secret sharing and HMAC-SHA3-256 synthetic nonces. The post compares witness sizes against Lifeboat and Dropkick and links to a paper and reference implementation, and the author requests technical critique of the BIP-32 chain-code assumptions and the Dual-Commit XOR mempool window bounds.
