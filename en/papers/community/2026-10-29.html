Secure Transaction Verification and Authorization (STVA)

In briefSecure Transaction Verification and Authorization (STVA): proposed Ethereum standard against blind signing

SummaryThis forum post proposes STVA, a draft ERC for attaching human-readable metadata and semantic intent to transactions and UserOperations. It defines an independent simulation and verification interface, scoped and revocable authorization tokens, and signing requirements for multisigs and smart accounts. The author argues that recent large thefts stemmed from blind signing and compromised frontends, and that the standard aims to make signed content independently verifiable.

Bitcoin Core v32.1 Released

In briefBitcoin Core v32.1 Release (Technical Community Discussion)

SummaryThe Bitcoin Core development team released version 32.1 after version 32.0 was tagged but never fully announced due to last-minute issues. The release includes new features, bug fixes, performance improvements, and updated translations. With this major release, versions 29.x and older have reached end of life and will no longer receive updates.
