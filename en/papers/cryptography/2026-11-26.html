One Algebra to Multiply Them All: Fast Homomorphic Linear Algebra in Every Encoding

In briefA unified algebraic method for fast homomorphic matrix operations across encodings

SummaryThe paper shows how to reduce homomorphic matrix multiplication to plaintext matrix multiplication directly in the slot encoding over the standard power-of-two CKKS ring, without changing the ciphertext ring. It uses the cyclic algebra of a cyclotomic field extension to describe endomorphisms independently of coordinates, so that choosing a basis corresponds to choosing an encoding. The framework also covers products of two encrypted matrices, with a stated count of key switchings.

Jonghoo Lee, Guillaume Hanrot, Seongsu Jeon

(Significantly) Faster Post-Quantum Public-Key PCFs for OT

In briefFaster post-quantum public-key PCFs for oblivious transfer

SummaryThis paper studies post-quantum Public-Key Pseudorandom Correlation Functions (PK-PCFs) for Oblivious Transfer (OT), which let users publish public keys so any pair can non-interactively generate many correlated strings to speed up secure computation. The authors give a new lattice-based construction that relies on roughly the same assumptions as prior work by Agrawal et al. but is built from a new blueprint using public-key algebraic homomorphic MACs. Their implementation reports throughput of about 208k OTs per second for the sender and 189k for the receiver on one thread, substantially faster than previous post-quantum constructions.

Shweta Agrawal, Kaartik Bhushan, Geoffroy Couteau et al.

In briefUsing Redundant PMNS Arithmetic to Reduce Side-Channel Leakage in ML-DSA

SummaryThis paper applies Polynomial Modular Number Systems (PMNS), previously studied for large-prime elliptic curve arithmetic, to the small modulus used by ML-DSA, a lattice-based signature scheme.
