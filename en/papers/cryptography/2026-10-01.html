SAGE: Similarity-Based Cleaning of Poisoned Training Data from Verified Examples

In briefSAGE: Cleaning Poisoned Training Data Using Similarity to a Small Verified Set

SummaryData poisoning attacks insert malicious examples into training data to make models misclassify a chosen target, a growing risk as machine learning relies on untrusted public data. The paper proposes SAGE, which uses a small set of expert-verified clean and poisoned examples, a generic feature extractor trained on a separate dataset, and similarity-weighted non-parametric prediction to flag poisoned training examples. In briefContext-blind, device-bound anonymous credentials built from signatures and proofs

SummaryThis paper presents a modular construction of context-blind device-bound anonymous credentials, where each presentation requires fresh secure element (SE) participation without revealing its context to the SE.
