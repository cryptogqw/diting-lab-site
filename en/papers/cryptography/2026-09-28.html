Zero Knowledge Proofs in Quantum Networks

In briefZero-knowledge proofs for quantum networks, a verification method that reveals no extra information

SummaryThis paper develops a framework for zero-knowledge verification based on the indistinguishability of quantum states under operational constraints. Generic Bounds for Multi-Instance Problems: The Strange Case of Inverse Diffie-Hellman

In briefGeneric-group bounds for multi-instance problems, focusing on Inverse Diffie-Hellman

SummaryThis paper studies how hard multi-instance problems over prime-order groups are in the generic group model, where an adversary must solve at least k of n independent instances. It shows that multi-instance CDH and SDH are as hard as solving k independent discrete logarithms, while multi-instance IDH and LKDH can be significantly easier than multi-instance CDH in certain parameter regimes. All these problems offer 1/2(log(p)+log(k)) bits of security. The lower bounds extend Yun's hyperplane query model and are derived using algebraic geometry techniques.

The authors also evaluate out-of-domain accuracy on SafetyBench-ZH and show that experience replay recovers performance lost to alignment.
