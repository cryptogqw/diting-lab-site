AdaFedXplain: Adaptive Federated Learning with Explainable AI for Ransomware Detection

In briefAdaFedXplain: Explainable Adaptive Federated Learning for Ransomware Detection

SummaryThis paper presents AdaFedXplain, an explainable adaptive federated learning system for detecting ransomware without sharing raw data between clients. It combines progressive XGBoost complexity, a decaying learning rate, and dynamic client grading. Using 62,485 real ransomware samples from four non-IID clients, the authors report 99.59% detection accuracy and outperform FedAvg, FedProx, and SCAFFOLD in energy, latency, and communication overhead. SHAP is used to explain which PE header features influence detection decisions.

Md. Emran Biswas, Md. Delowar Hossain, Mst. Tanbin Yasmin Tanny et al.

The Covering Radius of $\text{RM}(1,9)$ is Precisely $242$

In briefExact covering radius of RM(1,9) proven to be 242

SummaryThis paper studies the covering radius of the first-order Reed-Muller code RM(1,9), which equals the maximum nonlinearity of a 9-variable Boolean function. The authors use a computer-assisted proof to show the value is exactly 242, resolving a long-standing question where only the range 242 to 244 was known. The argument relies on divisibility properties of the Walsh spectrum and an exhaustive computation over cubic functions.

Xiang-dong Hou, Evelyn Sabo, Freeman Slaughter

PRICE: Privacy-Preserving Image Classification in Mobile Edge-Cloud Computing with Homomorphic Encryption

In briefPRICE: Privacy-preserving image classification for mobile edge-cloud systems using homomorphic encryption

SummaryThe paper addresses privacy risks when mobile devices send images to the cloud for classification. It proposes PRICE, a three-tier architecture where the edge extracts features in plaintext and only compact embeddings are CKKS-encrypted for final-layer inference in the cloud. It also introduces RowHyDiag, a row-packed encrypted matrix-vector method that reduces rotations. Experiments on real devices across five datasets report up to 31.8× faster cloud inference and 85.3× fewer rotations than the Halevi-Shoup method.

Duc-Huy Nguyen, Bui Duc Manh, Chi-Hieu Nguyen et al.

Revisiting the Security of VDOO

In briefRe-examining the security of VDOO, a multivariate signature scheme

SummaryVDOO is a multivariate signature scheme submitted to China's NGCC program. The authors treat it as a multilayer Rainbow construction, keep only its last oil space to obtain a generalized UOV instance, and apply existing wedge attacks. For the 512-bit parameter set, they estimate attack costs of 2^432 gates for the reconciliation variant and 2^379 for the intersection variant, below the claimed level.

Siyu Li, Hao Guo, Peigen Li et al.

SemVideoSte: Agentic AI for Cross-Modal Semantic Video Steganography Communication

In briefSemVideoSte: Agentic AI for Hiding Secret Messages in Generated Videos

SummaryThis paper proposes SemVideoSte, an agentic AI-driven scheme for cross-modal semantic video steganography, which hides secret messages inside generated videos for secure transmission. A multi-mask mechanism selects reliable embedding positions, and a private digital token controls the seed and guidance offset so the message is embedded during the final diffusion sampling step. Simulations on open-source datasets report high embedding capacity, reliable recovery, and strong semantic consistency across channel conditions, while eavesdroppers without the token cannot reliably recover the message and the stego videos resist steganalysis detection.

Rui Meng, Song Gao, Tingting Zhu et al.

How Secure are NTRU+ Keys? Analysing the Best Module Attacks

In briefHow Secure Are NTRU+ Keys? Analysing the Best Module Attacks

SummaryThis paper examines lattice reduction attacks against NTRU+, a South Korean key encapsulation standard. The authors introduce relative canonical and coefficient embeddings to analyse module lattices over cyclotomic fields and identify the optimal subfield for attacks. Using simulation-based cost estimates, they find the best module attack saves only 4–10 blocksizes, reducing NTRU+ key security by at most three bits in the Core-SVP model. The overstretched NTRU analysis is also extended to modules, and the authors conclude NTRU+ parameters remain safe.

Paola de Perthuis, Filip Trenkić
