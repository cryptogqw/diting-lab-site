Maximum-Likelihood Decoding of Gabidulin Codes Is NP-Hard Even in Dimension One

SummaryThe paper proves that deciding whether a received word lies within rank distance n−2 of a one-dimensional Gabidulin code is NP-complete, so maximum-likelihood decoding of these codes is NP-hard. The reduction is deterministic, whereas the previously known one was randomized, which makes Rank Syndrome Decoding and Rank Minimum Distance for Fqm-linear codes NP-complete under deterministic polynomial reductions. The proof uses an intermediate problem called Twisted Square Membership, and all constructions are explicit and polynomial.
