An Agentic ENF Framework for Forensic Video Authentication Against Generative AI

In briefENF-based agentic framework for forensic video authentication against generative AI

SummaryThis paper proposes a forensic framework that authenticates video using the electric network frequency (ENF), a mains-driven hum in audio and flicker in video that generative models do not control. It combines a two-channel ENF detector, synthetic-injection training, reference matching to verify or recover recording times, and an agentic orchestrator that fuses authenticity and manipulation evidence. On held-out real recordings, the detector accepts genuine audio and video at 93% and 100%, claimed-time authentication reaches an AUC of 1.0, and the cascade cuts wall-clock time by 54% without losing accuracy.

Ryan Wyton (1), Bacha Rehman (1) ((1) Southampton Solent University)

In briefSmoothness of shortest isogenies between supersingular curves and their Frobenius conjugates

Threshold Post-Quantum E-Voting from Lattice Commitments

In briefT-EVOLVE: A threshold post-quantum e-voting protocol that tallies even if some authorities are absent

SummaryThis paper presents T-EVOLVE, a lattice-commitment-based homomorphic e-voting protocol that Shamir-shares each vote among n authorities so that any t participating authorities can complete the tally. The authors claim privacy against up to t-1 colluding authorities and verifiability against all. Security is proven in the classical random oracle model, with the verifiability reduction not tight and parameters resting on classical hardness estimates. The C implementation reports 109 KB per yes/no ballot for four authorities without signatures, and about three minutes to aggregate 10^4 ballots on one core.
