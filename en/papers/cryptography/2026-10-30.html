Batched Threshold Predicate Encryption from Succinct LWE

In briefBatched threshold predicate encryption built on succinct LWE

SummaryThis paper introduces batched threshold predicate encryption (BT-PE), which combines batch-scoped authorization, predicate-based access control, and threshold decryption. The authors give a lattice-based construction that supports polynomial-size arithmetic-circuit predicates, compact batch digests, and compact per-party key shares. Decryption requires that the encrypted attribute belongs to the authorized batch, satisfies the required predicate, and enough parties participate. Correctness and query-selective one-sided security are proven under the ℓ-succinct LWE assumption.

Shayeef Murshid, Ramprasad Sarkar, Mriganka Mandal
