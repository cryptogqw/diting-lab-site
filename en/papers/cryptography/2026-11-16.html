PQC Migration: End-to-End Performance Profiling of Post Quantum Cryptographic Algorithms in mTLS

In briefPost-Quantum Crypto Migration: Performance Profiling of TLS Algorithms

SummaryThis paper measures end-to-end performance of TLS 1.3 connections using NIST-standardized post-quantum and hybrid key exchange algorithms under various network conditions. It evaluates speed (throughput, connection time) and sustainability (energy, CPU cycles). The authors find that standardized post-quantum algorithms cause no measurable degradation, while hybrid algorithms show noticeable slowdowns that worsen under poor network conditions, so migration should be network-condition aware.

Rahul, Manjesh K. Hanawal, Sameer G. Kulkarni et al.

Exposure and remediation in the French maritime sector: a passive measurement of 129 organisations

In briefInternet-facing exposure and fixes in France's maritime sector: a passive scan of 129 organisations

SummaryThis paper passively measures the internet-facing attack surface of 129 maritime organisations, 103 French companies and 26 international operators active in French waters, using public DNS, email-authentication, TLS certificate and web data. After manual verification, 475 findings were retained, about 3.7 per organisation. 46% of organisations had at least one high or critical finding, and French companies fared worse than international operators. The authors sent individual reports to 43 domains in March 2026, rescanned 123 organisations in November 2026, and compared remediation rates between notified and non-notified organisations.

Edouard Vallet, Colomban Monnier

AudioTrace: File-Level Membership Detection for Audio Large Language Models

In briefAudioTrace: File-Level Membership Detection for Audio Language Models

SummaryThis paper studies whether a given audio recording was used to train an audio large language model, a question raised by privacy and copyright concerns. It introduces AudioTraceBench, a benchmark of 1,285 recordings with fine-tuning membership labels, and shows that existing text- and video-based methods perform near random guessing on audio. The proposed AudioTrace method generates audio-conditioned proxy questions with the target model and aggregates membership evidence per recording, reaching an AUROC of 0.60 and outperforming existing baselines.

Guangzhi Sun, Xiao Zhan, Shuai Wang et al.

Stop Early, Stay Simple: Evidence Acquisition for Power System Fault and Attack Diagnosis

In briefStop Early, Stay Simple: Evidence Acquisition for Power System Attack Diagnosis

SummaryThe paper examines whether a learned policy is needed to decide which telemetry sources to read when diagnosing faults and attacks in cyber-physical power systems. It introduces ActiGrid, a benchmark framework in which a Double Deep Q-Network chooses between requesting another source and stopping to diagnose, and compares it with fixed subsets, entropy stopping, a greedy value-of-information rule, and tree ensembles. On the TU Delft and MSU/ORNL benchmarks, the learned policy is not better than simple controls, while compact subset classifiers perform well while reading fewer sources.

Scenario-Based Risk Certificates for Multi-Property Vulnerability Discovery in Parametric MDPs

In briefRisk certificates for finding multiple vulnerabilities in parametric Markov decision process models

SummaryThis paper studies how to find parameter settings that break several safety or performance requirements at once in parametric Markov decision processes (pMDPs), where transition probabilities depend on uncertain parameters. It builds candidate vulnerability regions for each requirement and analyzes random testing budgets under a given parameter distribution. The method has two layers: a scenario-approximation layer giving PAC guarantees for conservative vulnerability regions, and a testing layer that yields a stopping certificate computed from region masses alone. A composition theorem bounds the expected non-discovery score.00

In briefSTA: A split temporal attack method for testing how well multimodal RL driving policies withstand timing errors

SummaryThe paper argues that multimodal reinforcement learning driving policies depend on sensor observations being temporally aligned, so timing inconsistencies can cause decision failures. Where the Savings Go: The Limits of Hiding Cross-Tenant Cache Hits in LLM Serving

In briefWhere the Saved Time Goes: Limits of Hiding Cross-Tenant Cache Hits in LLM Serving

SummaryLLM serving engines cache prompt prefixes, so a shared cache can reveal whether other tenants have sent a given text through faster responses. The paper shows that common defenses that hide cache hits fall short, because the saved time is passed to requests queued behind the hit and can be detected by an attacker within normal rate limits. On one vLLM instance, a single probe detects cached texts of 256 tokens or more with advantage at least 0.98. The authors also prove that a tenant's gain from other accounts' content is bounded by the allowed leakage, so at zero leakage only the provider keeps the savings.

WhyThe authors show hit-hiding defenses overlook where saved time goes, prove tenant gain bounds leakage, and report a single probe detecting cached prompts on vLLM.

Modular Walsh rigidity for Boolean functions of degree at most four

In briefModular Walsh rigidity for degree-four Boolean functions

SummaryThe paper studies Boolean functions of degree at most four. It proves that such a function on 4m variables (m≥4, M=2^m) is bent if all its Walsh coefficients are congruent to ±M² modulo a single odd integer p≥9M/16. This improves the earlier sufficient bound p>7M/12 and includes the case M=16, p=9.

Filipe Martins Vono, André Oliveira Naufel de Toledo, Valéria Gonzaga Santos
