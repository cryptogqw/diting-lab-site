Information Loss Is All You Need: TC⁰ Pseudorandomness from Worst-Case Lattices

In briefInformation Loss Is All You Need: Post-Quantum TC⁰ Pseudorandomness from Worst-Case Lattices

SummaryThe paper introduces learning with algebraic rounding (LAR), which applies deterministic integer rounding to blocks of secret-dependent linear measurements and encodes the result in a lattice quotient. For a fixed family with polynomially bounded modulus and dimensions, the authors show these deterministic labels on uniform, secret-independent public matrices are computationally indistinguishable from uniform labels given any polynomial number of classical random examples. Security rests on quantum worst-case hardness of GapSVP and SIVP over general lattices at approximation factor Õ(q). The construction yields a post-quantum weak pseudorandom function with joint key-and-input evaluation in TC⁰, and the paper also proves hardness of learning polynomial-size two-hidden-layer ReLU networks from noiseless uniform random examples.
