Classical Oracle Separation of QMA from the QCMA Hierarchy and Quantum Cryptography Even When P = QCMA

In briefClassical oracle separation of QMA from the QCMA hierarchy and quantum cryptography, even when P = QCMA

SummaryThe paper introduces a new problem called Codeword-Hash pairs in Noise (CHN) and proves, in the query complexity model, that it lies in QMA but is hard for the QCMA hierarchy (QCMAH). Using this problem, it constructs a classical oracle under which QMA is strictly more powerful than QCMAH. The paper also shows that, relative to an oracle where P = QCMA, various quantum cryptographic primitives, such as pseudorandom states with classical keys, can still exist.

WhyThe authors introduce CHN, prove a classical-oracle separation of QMA from the QCMA hierarchy, and answer an open question of Kretschmer, Qian, and Tal.
