In briefBarriers to Identity-Based Encryption in Generic Group-Action Models

SummaryThis paper studies whether identity-based encryption (IBE) can be built from the basic interface of generic cryptographic group actions. In the type-safe generic group-action model, the authors prove that an IBE scheme whose public parameters contain n non-origin action-set element-handles can support at most n identities, and they show a matching construction that makes this bound tight.
