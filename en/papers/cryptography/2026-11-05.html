Cloudy Skies: Early Detection of Jamming Against Iridium LEO Satellite-IoT Links

In brief"Cloudy Skies": Early Jamming Detection for Iridium LEO Satellite-IoT Links

SummaryThis paper examines whether jamming of Iridium low-Earth-orbit satellite-IoT links can be detected early, using the physical layer of messages that are still decoded correctly. The authors release a real-world Iridium dataset covering 47 jamming conditions and compare three autoencoder-based anomaly detectors with two signal-quality indicators used in GNSS. They find that autoencoders reach an AUC of at least 0.84 when a noise jammer raises the noise floor by at least 9 dB, while C/N0 and SNR estimators fail, so detection should rely on physical-layer information.

Ingrid Huso, Giulia Fliescher, Savio Sciancalepore et al.

KeHoSu: Keyed Homoglyph Substitution for Authenticated Text Provenance over Copy-Paste Channels

In briefKeHoSu: Keyed Homoglyph Substitution for Authenticated Text Provenance over Copy-Paste

SummaryThis paper presents KeHoSu, a method that uses look-alike characters from homoglyph attacks to embed an authenticated attribution mark in text that survives copy-paste. The chosen variant of each letter encodes one bit of an identifier built from a platform field, a user field, and a truncated authentication tag. The authors tested it on 18 common platforms and report that most excerpts were attributed to the correct account at about 178 letters (two sentences) for LLM-generated stories and 167 letters (one sentence) for English prose.

Andrei Alexandru Dr\u{a}ghici, Sediola Ruko, Briland Hitaj et al.

Stealthy Malicious Interference Detection in UAV Formations: A Power-Domain Signaling Game Approach

In briefDetecting hidden jamming UAVs in formations via a power-domain signaling game

SummaryThis paper addresses detecting stealthy jamming in UAV swarms, where a jammer can adjust its transmit power so its interference looks like benign link fluctuation. The authors propose a belief-driven detection scheme based on a Bayesian security game, in which the defender updates its posterior belief about the interferer's type from observed SINR and chooses between normal operation and defensive power boosting. They characterize pure-strategy perfect Bayesian equilibrium conditions for separating and pooling structures. Numerical results indicate the scheme improves the defender's expected utility over conventional schemes.

Jianle Ba, Shaolong Guo, Yuntao Wang et al.

CANMentor: A Large-Small Model Collaboration Framework for CAN Bus Intrusion Detection

In briefCANMentor: A Framework Where Cloud LLMs Guide Vehicle Intrusion Detection on CAN Bus

SummaryCANMentor is a framework in which a cloud-side large language model acts as a teacher for lightweight intrusion detection models inside vehicles on the CAN bus. Vehicles run local inference first, and a privacy-aware generator creates proxies from original CAN messages for the cloud teacher, whose predictions are used only when they arrive within a set latency bound. Knowledge distillation from the teacher's outputs yields relative Macro F1 gains of up to 25.01% on ROAD and 20.79% on HCRL Car-Hacking under non-IID partitions.

Beyond Instruction Override: Benchmarking Indirect Prompt Injection with Task-Tampering Attacks

In briefBeyond Instruction Override: Benchmarking Indirect Prompt Injection via Task-Tampering Attacks

SummaryThis paper observes that existing indirect prompt-injection benchmarks mostly test planted instructions that steer tool-using agents toward an attacker's chosen task, while attackers can also tamper with the outcome of a task already in progress. The authors define attacks by authority rather than instruction wording and build a benchmark of 90 validated cases across three tool-use environments. Experiments find that attacks targeting decision rules, available actions, and state often succeed more than attacks targeting the agent's objective, and that detection-and-removal defenses are much less effective against observation- and state-targeting attacks.

WhyThe authors define injection by authority and benchmark six decision-tampering targets across 90 cases and five LLMs, showing defenses miss several.

Rongting Zhang, Martin Bertran, Sandeep Avula et al.

Dynamic Watermarking and Action-Conditioned Latent Dynamics for Secure Vision-Based Controllers

In briefDynamic watermarking and latent dynamics for detecting replay attacks on vision-based controllers

SummaryThis paper proposes an adaptive dynamic-watermarking framework for detecting replay attacks against vision-based controllers. A private Gaussian watermark is added to the nominal control input, and an action-conditioned latent dynamics network predicts system evolution in a latent space learned from images, with attacks flagged by discrepancies between predicted and observed latent states. A reinforcement learning policy adjusts the watermark covariance, and a Lagrangian safety penalty is used during training. The framework is evaluated on a vision-guided UR5 robot task involving reaching a target while avoiding a moving human arm.
