Cloudy Skies: Early Detection of Jamming Against Iridium LEO Satellite-IoT Links

In brief"Cloudy Skies": Early Jamming Detection for Iridium LEO Satellite-IoT Links

SummaryThis paper examines whether jamming of Iridium low-Earth-orbit satellite-IoT links can be detected early, using the physical layer of messages that are still decoded correctly. The authors release a real-world Iridium dataset covering 47 jamming conditions and compare three autoencoder-based anomaly detectors with two signal-quality indicators used in GNSS. They find that autoencoders reach an AUC of at least 0.84 when a noise jammer raises the noise floor by at least 9 dB, while C/N0 and SNR estimators fail, so detection should rely on physical-layer information.
