Identity Amortization: Shared Resources and the Cost of Sybil Attacks

In briefIdentity Amortization: How Shared Resources Lower the Cost of Sybil Attacks

SummaryMany Sybil defenses raise attack costs by requiring one resource per identity. This paper shows that when one resource can support several identities, an attacker pays less per identity than the defense assumes, a effect it calls identity amortization.
