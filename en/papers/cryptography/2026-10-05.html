In briefRAISED: Self-Distillation Makes LLM Agents More Resistant to Prompt Injection

Explicit Nonlinear Functions beyond the Fourier bound

In briefExplicit nonlinear functions that beat the Fourier bound on agreement

SummaryThe paper studies constructing vectorial maps F from F_2^n to F_2^m that agree with any affine map on as few inputs as possible. Classical bent-function and Fourier methods give agreement bounds no smaller than 2^{n/2}. The authors construct, for any γ>0, functions with m=O_γ(n) whose agreement with affine maps is at most (1+γ)^n. They obtain the same behavior for agreement with m-tuples of degree-d polynomials, improving prior Gowers-norm-based bounds. The approach connects to algebraic counting of polynomial system solutions and to combinatorial graphs and hypergraphs.
