Fast ℓ_∞-Reduction of Rank-2 Lattices in ℝⁿ via a New Reduced-Basis Notion

In briefFast ℓ∞ reduction of rank-2 lattices in ℝⁿ using a new reduced-basis notion

SummaryThis paper studies rank-2 lattices in ℝⁿ under the infinity norm and introduces a new definition of a reduced basis for this setting. Based on it, the authors propose a lattice basis reduction algorithm that performs a multidimensional analogue of Euclidean division using a dominant coordinate. Each non-terminal iteration halves the maximum infinity norm of the basis vectors, and the algorithm is proven to terminate with bit complexity O(nT²), matching classical Lagrange–Gauss reduction. Experiments report significant runtime gains, especially for high-dimensional lattices.

Bo Pang, Shaojing Zhang, Chengliang Tian

Superspecial points on Humbert surfaces and the probability of (N,N)-splitting

In briefSuperspecial points on Humbert surfaces and the probability of (N,N)-splitting

SummaryThe paper studies superspecial principally polarized abelian surfaces over the algebraic closure of a finite field and determines the proportion that are optimally (N,N)-split. It shows this proportion is 5NJ₂(N)/p + O_N(p⁻²), where J₂ is Jordan's totient. The constants are computed explicitly for 2 ≤ N ≤ 7, and the result gives the stationary density relevant to splitting-detection attacks on two-dimensional superspecial isogeny problems.

WhyLinks superspecial surfaces to genus averages via Siegel weights, giving a rigorous first-order formula for (N,N)-splitting proportion, verified exactly at twelve primes.

Tanush Shaska

Eﬃcient Fixed-Point Implementation of Mitaka

In briefEfficient fixed-point implementation of the Mitaka signature scheme

SummaryMitaka is a variant of Falcon (FN-DSA) with a simpler, parallelizable signing procedure. This paper implements the MitakaZ signing algorithm in fixed-point arithmetic, replacing the wide integral Gram root decomposition with a fixed-point Cholesky decomposition to reduce the cost of integer Gaussian sampling. Bounds on intermediate values and representation errors are proven. On an Intel Core i7-1360P, signing averages 361 kcycles for Mitaka-512 and 707 kcycles for Mitaka-1024; on an ARM Cortex-M4F, 4.0 and 7.8 Mcycles.

Shiduo Zhang, Paul Kirchner, Xiuhan Lin et al.

In briefNarratives Mislead: How LLMs Misjudge CVSS Severity in Bug Bounty Reports

SummaryThe study builds a benchmark of 240 Huntr bug bounty reports with vendor-validated CVSS vectors and evaluates eight LLMs under four prompting strategies and fine-tuning. The best prompting setup reaches only 49.58% level accuracy, and a fine-tuned 7B model reaches at most 56.25%. Models overestimate severity far more often than they underestimate it and rarely recognize low-severity vulnerabilities. The authors attribute these errors to three semantic boundaries where the CVSS specification and narrative reports diverge.

Private Evaluation of High-Dimensional Functions

In briefPrivately evaluating arbitrary high-dimensional functions using only black-box queries, with outputs guaranteed to lie in the convex hull of nearby results.

SummaryThis paper studies differentially private black-box evaluation of arbitrary high-dimensional functions. The evaluator can only query the function over subsets obtained by removing a bounded number of elements from a dataset, and aims to privately output an approximation of the function's value. The authors reduce the problem to high-dimensional private quasi-concave optimization, yielding generic evaluators for both pure and approximate differential privacy whose outputs lie in the convex hull of nearby vectors with high probability. The work also covers applications to user-level optimization and shows the formulation is equivalent to private quasi-concave optimization up to a dimension factor.

WhyGeneralizes private real-valued evaluation to high dimensions, proves equivalence with private QCO, and improves user-level optimization overhead to additive, a strong theoretical contribution.

Mohammad Afzali, Hassan Ashtiani, Adam Smith
