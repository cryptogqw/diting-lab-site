In briefPassGPT+: Using Language Priors to Model Passwords

In briefQuantum Attacks Break Two Post-Quantum Key Exchange and OT Compilers

In briefPost-quantum security limits of (O)EKE and Masny-Rindal OT protocols

SummaryThis paper examines whether encrypted key exchange (EKE), its OEKE variant, and Masny-Rindal oblivious transfer remain secure against quantum adversaries. These compilers build password-authenticated key exchange and oblivious transfer from any KEM using only idealized symmetric-key primitives. The authors prove that a general family of these protocols is not UC-secure against quantum polynomial-time adversaries, even with a post-quantum KEM. They also show both compilers satisfy certain game-based security notions and introduce an advantage-tight one-way to hiding lemma.

Path-Finding, Orbit State Preparation, and the Security of Invariant Quantum Money

In briefPath-Finding and State Preparation in the Security of Invariant Quantum Money

SummaryThis paper examines the assumptions underlying the security of quantum money based on knots and invariant money. Existing proofs rely on additional knowledge-of-path assumptions whose relation to security is unclear. When equivalence classes are orbits of efficiently computable group actions, average-case hardness of path-finding is shown to be necessary for security. For knots, forgery reduces only to an equally hard state-preparation problem, and the authors propose a falsifiable preparation assumption equivalent to security.

Security Properties of Neural Networks as Decision Problems

In briefAnalysing neural network security properties as decision problems

SummaryThe paper formalises eight security questions for certifying deployed neural networks, such as backdoor detection, fault-induced unsafe states and input leakage, as decision problems. It shows that piecewise linear networks can be encoded as quantifier-free real-addition formulas of linear size, so network properties become quantifier-alternation sentences placed in the polynomial hierarchy. Most problems are shown co-NP-complete over ReLU, backdoor trigger detection Sigma_2^P-complete, and parameter-fault verification exists-R-complete.

In briefRate 1/5 Non-Malleable Codes Against Entangled Split-State Tampering
