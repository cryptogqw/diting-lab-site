Security Authentication as Protective Friction: Understanding the Dual Effects of Authentication Measures in e-Government Platform

In briefSecurity Authentication as Protective Friction in e-Government Platforms

SummaryThis paper examines security authentication measures, such as facial recognition and multi-factor verification, on e-government platforms like government credit platforms. The authors reconceptualize these measures as protective friction and propose a dual-pathway model in which it reduces privacy-security risk perception while increasing effort cost, with self-efficacy moderating only the risk pathway. The research-in-progress outlines a mixed-methods design combining a user survey and staff interviews at a Chinese municipal credit center to test the model.

Jianshan Pan, Zongyi Chen, Qiyou Lin et al.

Memorization Lives in a Few Attention Heads: Mitigating Memorization in Diffusion Models via Projection-Based Suppression

In briefMemorization in Diffusion Models Traced to a Few Attention Heads, with a Projection-Based Fix

SummaryThis paper studies how diffusion models memorize and reproduce training samples during inference. The authors find that memorization is mainly driven by a small set of shallow-layer cross-attention heads rather than the whole mechanism. They propose Projection-based Top-k Attention Head Suppression, which uses a semantic-control score to pick the most relevant heads and removes their condition-specific components via directional projection. Experiments across diffusion models and datasets show reduced memorization with better image quality than existing methods.

Puwei Lian, Yujun Cai, Songze Li et al.

BMuSig-L: Blind Multi-Signature from Lattice

In briefBMuSig-L: a lattice-based blind multi-signature scheme

SummaryThis paper presents BMuSig-L, a two-round, concurrently secure blind multi-signature scheme based on lattices. A blind multi-signature lets a user obtain a joint signature from a group of signers while keeping the message hidden from them and preventing colluding signers from linking the signature to its signing session. The construction builds on MuSig-L, a concurrently secure lattice blind signature scheme, and a knowledge-sound non-interactive argument system. The authors prove one-more unforgeability under stated assumptions and present a prototype evaluating signature size and verification time.

Anirban Chakraborty, Mriganka Dey, Nikita Dey et al.

In briefCirculant Diffusion Layers: Cost Bounds and Rectangle Reuse

SummaryThis paper studies the addition cost of sparse invertible circulant matrices over F_2 and odd prime fields, measuring diffusion by branch numbers. A Quasipolynomial Quantum Algorithm for the Vector Dihedral Coset Problem with Smooth Modulus

In briefQuasipolynomial quantum algorithm for a vector dihedral coset problem with smooth modulus

SummaryThis paper presents a quantum algorithm that recovers a hidden vector s in Z_q^n from samples of the form (|0,x⟩+|1,x+s⟩)/√2, the vector dihedral coset problem. When the modulus q is B-smooth, the algorithm runs with n^{O(B^{1+o(1)} log q)} B^{O(B^{2+o(1)} log q)} samples and time, which is quasipolynomial when B is polylogarithmic and q is polynomial in n. The method reduces the modulus one prime factor at a time and uses an Ivanyos–Santha sieving procedure to clear non-linear phase terms. The authors state that the result does not yield a new attack on LWE in any regime.

Aparna Gupte
