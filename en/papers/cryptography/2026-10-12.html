Revisiting Fast Tendermint: An Agreement Violation and Two Repairs

In briefRevisiting Fast Tendermint: a consensus violation and two fixes

SummaryThis paper shows that the published round-change rule of Fast Tendermint violates Agreement: one Byzantine process can cause a violation for every f≥1, and two can do so for f≥2 without double signing. The problem arises when a process skips a round without recording the value that may have been decided there. The accompanying Quint specification does not permit such skipping and is unaffected; the flaw is in the published pseudocode. Two repairs are proposed: Walk requires leaving a round only after a precommit quorum and precommitting before leaving, and Justify places the evidence needed to accept a proposal in the proposal itself. Both satisfy Agreement and Validity under asynchrony and Termination under partial synchrony.

Alejandro Ranchal-Pedrosa

SummaryThis paper presents a compiler that builds a dynamic SNARK for Plonkish constraints using a static SNARK for NP and a vector commitment scheme as black boxes.
