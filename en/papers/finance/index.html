In briefAn axiomatic foundation for optimized certainty equivalents

SummaryThis paper provides economic axioms that characterize Optimized Certainty Equivalents (OCEs), a major class of risk measures that includes Mean-Variance, Expected Shortfall, and the Entropic Risk Measure.
