EverMine: Dissecting the Self-Evolution of Research Capabilities in Long-Horizon Alpha Research

In briefEverMine: Studying Self-Evolving Research Skills in Long-Term Alpha Discovery

SummaryThis paper introduces EverMine, a framework for testing whether self-evolving agents keep improving their accumulated research skills during long-horizon alpha factor discovery. It splits the research state into history, the current factor portfolio, and reusable capabilities, and compares runs with fixed or evolving capabilities under matched resource limits. Across 18 long trajectories and 48 continuation branches, capability evolution showed no consistent gain, while tuning existing factor structures still helped. The authors also find that a candidate's value depends on the evolving portfolio and the order in which candidates are submitted.

Siyuan Li, Jiangfeng Zhang, Rui Yao et al.

LiveOption: Evaluating LLM Agents in Structured Option Trading with Nonlinear Payoffs

In briefLiveOption: Testing AI Agents on Structured Option Trading with Nonlinear Payoffs

SummaryThis paper introduces LiveOption, a framework for evaluating large language model agents in option trading, a market more complex than simple stock trading. It provides a reproducible environment under realistic execution and capital constraints, covering portfolio overlays, earnings-event trading, and 0DTE intraday trading. The authors also propose a hierarchical metric suite assessing action validity, decision quality, risk, and outcomes. Experiments show current agents often fail to achieve competitive returns in most scenarios.

Haochen Luo, Yifan Li, Binh Minh An et al.
