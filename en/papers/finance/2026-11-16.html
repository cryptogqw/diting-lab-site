A Second Order Transport Scheme for Dynamic Portfolio Subspaces under a Varying Risk Metric

In briefA second-order method for moving portfolio subspaces under changing risk weights

SummaryThis paper studies how portfolio exposures can be transported between moving subspaces when the risk metric changes over time. It derives the local error of risk-weighted Procrustes alignment, showing that a term coupling metric change with motion normal to the subspace can reduce global accuracy to first order. Geometric mean alignment and a symmetric Procrustes construction cancel this term, yielding reversible updates with second-order global accuracy and uniform error bounds on nonuniform partitions.

Alejandro Rodriguez Dominguez

Mollification Regularization and Asymptotic Correction for Analytical Expansion of BSDEs with Non-Smooth Terminal Conditions. I. Theory

In briefSmoothing and Asymptotic Correction for Analytical Expansion of BSDEs with Non-Smooth Terminal Conditions (Theory)

SummaryThis paper addresses the failure of Zhang's (2017) analytical expansion method for backward stochastic differential equations when the terminal condition has kinks or discontinuities. The terminal condition is smoothed by a mollifier, and a joint error decomposition is derived. Under an explicitly stated uniform local-to-global Taylor-propagation assumption, balanced convergence rates are obtained. Asymptotic corrections that cancel the weak bias, and the conditions under which their faster exponents hold, are also discussed. Numerical evidence is reported in the companion paper.

Liangliang Zhang

EWMA Principal Eigenportfolios of Equity Returns: Market Representation and Beta-Hedging Performance

In briefEWMA principal eigenportfolio: market representation and beta-hedging performance of equity returns

SummaryThis paper tests whether an adaptively estimated principal eigenportfolio improves equity beta hedging compared with capitalisation-weighted and equal-weighted market benchmarks. Using S&P 500 stock returns with a changing constituent universe, it builds EWMA-PC1 from the leading principal component of an exponentially weighted second-moment matrix of volatility-standardised returns. Compared with SPY and RSP under a common hedging procedure, EWMA-PC1 achieves greater mean variance reduction and lower average residual correlation than SPY across tested configurations, while its advantage over RSP is smaller and mixed.

Andrew Kangmin Kim, Aurora Neyan Zhou

Markets for Compute and Inference: Structure, Financing, and Risk Transfer

In briefMarkets for Compute and Inference: Structure, Financing, and Risk Transfer

SummaryThis paper examines markets for renting compute to run large language models and for selling inference priced per million tokens, asking what risks can be hedged, by whom, and at what price. It finds that the short-dated rental rate that exchange contracts settle on is not the price driving operators' revenue, which mostly comes from private multi-year contracts with largely undisclosed prices. The authors design a reverse convertible note that would let leveraged operators hedge falling rental rates, with a coupon that depends steeply on the rate's annualised volatility.

Dr Gabriel Borrageiro
