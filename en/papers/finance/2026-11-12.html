Price co-movement and price information diverge across Nordic electricity zones after the energy crisis

In briefNordic electricity price links and price information diverge after the energy crisis

SummaryAfter the 2021-2022 energy crisis, day-ahead electricity prices in Nordic bidding zones stopped moving together. The study measures how Norwegian and Swedish zones co-moved with Germany-Luxembourg from 2018 to 2025 and forecasts 2025 prices with one model. Southern zones stayed strongly linked to Germany-Luxembourg, while northern zones linked weakly. However, the usefulness of price information did not follow this split, and it differed mainly between national generation systems.

My Thi Diem Phan, Tuyen Trung Truong, Hoai Phuong Ha et al.

Dynamic Scenario-Based Portfolio Optimization for Multi-Asset ETFs

In briefDynamic scenario-based portfolio optimization for multi-asset ETFs: how trading constraints shape outcomes

SummaryThis paper compares historical moving-window returns with conditional scenarios generated by ARMA-GARCH models and a Student-t copula for 30 U.S.-listed ETFs. Mean-variance and conditional value-at-risk portfolios are built under long-only and limited long-short constraints and evaluated over 946 sessions. Historical portfolios showed higher observed reward-risk ratios in most comparisons, but no paired difference stayed significant after multiple-testing adjustment, and conditional scenarios did not consistently reduce realized downside risk. The authors separate target-weight changes from rebalancing trades and find that relaxing the target-adjustment limit affected exposures, turnover, and some returns more than changing scenario count or random seed.

Abigail A. Mensah, Ali M. Jaffri, Nicholas Appiah W. Brent Lindquist et al.

TAPES: Terminal Adjoint-Propagated Error Scheduling for Financial Diffusion Samplers

In briefTAPES: Offline Time-Grid Scheduling to Reduce Numerical Error in Financial Diffusion Samplers

SummaryFinancial diffusion samplers used with few function evaluations can have time-grid numerical error that distorts terminal quantities such as covariances and tails. The paper proposes TAPES, which estimates local errors via Richardson comparisons, propagates them to the terminal report using an adjoint, and optimizes the time grid by minimizing the squared norm of their sum. Across a regime-switching simulation and Mag7 data, TAPES gives the lowest design and audit report errors in all 24 tested rows. It changes only the offline schedule and keeps the deployment number of function evaluations unchanged.

Yilie Huang

In briefHuman Capital in Context: Employment Survival and Worker-Firm Match Quality

Global Seasonality Factor Investing

In briefGlobal Seasonality Factor Investing: a study using calendar patterns to predict risk-adjusted returns in global futures markets

SummaryThis paper treats seasonality as a factor for predicting risk-adjusted returns across global futures in bond, commodity and equity markets. The authors fit nonlinear seasonality models for each individual market and test them with walk-forward out-of-sample evaluation. Fast-moving patterns such as day-of-week and day-of-month deliver strong gross returns but turn negative after transaction costs due to very high turnover. Slower patterns such as day-of-quarter, day-of-half-year and day-of-year remain positive net of costs, except for day-of-quarter. Adding seasonality to a global factor portfolio raises its out-of-sample Sharpe ratio by about 30%.

Andrew Butler, Adam Butler, Maciej Zawadzki

Idiosyncratic Market Effects in Global Time Series Momentum

In briefMarket-specific effects in global time series momentum

SummaryThis paper studies time series momentum, the tendency for a market's recent average return to positively predict future returns. Building on a global nonlinear factor model, the authors fit models to individual markets and find their response curves differ from the global model. They propose a framework that lets each feature-market pair express its own predictive behaviour. Empirically, nonlinear models fitted to single markets, despite much smaller samples, are robust and can marginally improve out-of-sample performance.

Andrew Butler, Maciej Zawadzki, Adam Butler

Parsimonious and Flexible Parameterizations for Realized Covariance Matrix Models

In briefCompact yet flexible parameter designs for models of realized covariance matrices

SummaryThis paper proposes a new class of parameterizations for covariance models that describe how conditional covariance matrices of financial asset returns evolve over time. Each variance and covariance receives its own time-varying coefficient while the total number of parameters stays limited. The authors derive sufficient conditions that guarantee the resulting covariance matrices are positive definite. The approach is assessed through Monte Carlo simulations and an empirical application, showing it can capture complex dependence structures while remaining computationally tractable.

Edoardo Otranto
