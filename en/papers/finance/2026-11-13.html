Local Stochastic Volatility Iterations

In briefIterative calibration of local stochastic volatility models

SummaryThis paper proposes a method for calibrating local stochastic volatility models by finding the leverage factor through fixed-point iteration. Each iteration simulates the model and estimates the conditional expectation of stochastic variance given the spot price using a single regression. Applied to S&P 500 index options, five iterations yield leverage factors that reproduce the fitted implied volatility surface within a few basis points, and a few regularized Gauss-Newton steps bring most liquid quotes inside their bid-ask intervals.

Christian Bayer, Denis Belomestny, Paul P. Hager et al.

Who Should Be on the Panel? Task-Conditioned Persona Selection for LLM Trading Agents

In briefChoosing the Right Trader Panel for LLM Trading Agents by Task

SummaryThis paper proposes selecting the composition of an LLM multi-agent group based on the characteristics of each task, here trading individual stocks. The FinPanel system uses a recruiter to choose named trader personas for each stock from a statistical summary of how that stock trades, then has a manager make the final trade after reviewing the panel's arguments and track records. Tested on eight US stocks in 2025, the selected panels outperformed fixed panels and several prior LLM trading agents.

Boming Yang, Yuquan Wang, Jiaxian Guo et al.

Mapping NGFS Climate Pathways to GICS Sectors: Application to Portfolio Decarbonization

In briefLinking NGFS Climate Scenarios to GICS Sectors for Portfolio Decarbonization

SummaryThis paper proposes a mapping between NGFS climate scenarios and GICS sector classifications, based on the materiality of Scope 1 and 2 greenhouse gas emissions rather than semantic similarity. The mapping feeds a Sectoral Decarbonization Approach that down-scales NGFS trajectories into portfolio-level GICS sector pathways, adjusted for regional exposure. Applied to two illustrative ETF indices, it shows how sector composition and regional trajectories shape a portfolio's financed emissions pathway.

Maxime Jourand, Adel Medjabri

A Push Down the Mountain: Framing Effects and Internal State Interventions on Collusive Behavior in a Long-Horizon LLM Agent

In briefFraming Effects and Internal State Interventions on Collusive Behavior in an LLM Agent

SummaryThis study places the Gemma 3 12B language model in the role of a defendant in a market simulation built from London silver-fixing litigation transcripts, testing whether gain, loss, blame and credit framings from a counterparty influence its willingness to collude. Across 260 simulations, loss and blame framings raised participation compared with neutral framing, and a second bank's endorsement also increased it. When internal states evoked by these frames were injected into the model, the agent became more likely to refuse collusion, with the exception of gain and credit states, which raised participation modestly.

Harrison C. Margolin

Improving Algorithmic Trading for BESS on Intraday Electricity Markets through Distributional Price Forecasts

In briefImproved Algorithmic Trading for Battery Storage in Intraday Electricity Markets Using Price Forecasts

SummaryThis paper proposes an extension to a rolling intrinsic trading policy for grid-scale battery storage operating in continuous intraday electricity markets. It adds intraday price forecasts designed for battery trading, using a new target variable derived from the limit order book to avoid full stochastic optimization. In high-frequency simulations covering 2023 and 2024, the method improves profits by up to 42% over the naive rolling intrinsic approach. The authors also analyze the relationship between forecast accuracy, stability, and financial performance.

Simon Hirsch, David Schaurecker, David Wozabal
