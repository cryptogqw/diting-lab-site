Dynamic Spatio-Temporal Graph Attention Networks with Nested Clustered Optimization for Multi-Asset Portfolios

In briefMulti-asset portfolio allocation using dynamic spatio-temporal graph attention and nested clustered optimization

SummaryThis paper proposes ST-GAT-NCO, a deep graph learning framework for tactical allocation across multiple assets. It uses causal dilated 1D convolutions to extract temporal momentum and downside risk, and a multi-head Graph Attention Network to learn spatial asset representations that define a distance space for two-stage downside nested clustered optimization. Tested over a 73-month non-anticipative walk-forward out-of-sample window from January 2020 to January 2026 across 13 global asset classes, net of 10 bps transaction costs, it reports a 5.32% CAGR.

NoLBERT-Calls: Reading Earnings Calls Without Hindsight

In briefNoLBERT-Calls: Reading Earnings Calls Without Hindsight

SummaryThis paper adapts NoLBERT, a language model pretrained only on 1976–1995 text, to earnings calls without exposing it to anything dated after 31 December 2023. Training uses the calls themselves: the model learns to pick each analyst question's management answer from the other answers in the same call. On US calls from January 2025 to October 2026, it ranks the true answer first for 78.0% of questions, up from 26.3% before adaptation.

WhyThe authors adapt a 1976-1995-pretrained encoder to earnings-call Q&A without look-ahead, reporting 78.0% top-ranked accuracy over about 254,575 questions, ahead of the baselines.

Kevin Foley, Jonathan Hartadi, Shivesh Prakash et al.

PIRanNN: Random-Feature Pricing and Calibration under Jump-Diffusion and Stochastic-Volatility Models

In briefPIRanNN: Fast Option Pricing and Calibration with Random Features under Jump and Volatility Models

SummaryThis paper proposes a random-feature method for pricing and calibrating European options under jump-diffusion (Merton, Kou) and stochastic-volatility (Heston, SABR) models. The one-hidden-layer network with fixed random weights reduces fitting to a single regularised least-squares problem, with no iterative training. For jump models, subtracting the Black-Scholes price reduces pricing error by about two orders of magnitude. The method matches the accuracy of a reference physics-informed neural network in seconds, and calibration recovers jump parameters to within 3% when pricing error is small.

Saurabh Bansal, Srikanth K. Iyer, Shashi Jain
