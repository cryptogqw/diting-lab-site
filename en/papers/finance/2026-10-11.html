From Economic Explanation to Performance Improvement in Machine-Learning Portfolios

In briefFrom Economic Explanation to Better Machine-Learning Portfolio Performance

SummaryThis paper proposes a local first-order attribution-propagation framework that links feature exposures in an allocation function to local sensitivities of portfolio performance metrics. The authors relate these attributions to Shapley values defined for portfolio objectives and derive statistical inference for Sharpe-ratio attributions. An attribution-guided exposure intervention can be applied after training without retraining the model. Empirically, the interventions yield statistically significant improvements in net Sharpe ratios across different model classes.

Yunqi Liang, Stefan Zohren, Mihai Cucuringu

Too Late to Prune: Generative AI, the Screen, and Team Creative Decisions

In briefToo Late to Prune: How Timing of Generative AI Access Affects Team Creative Choices

SummaryThis paper examines when generative AI's options are most valuable to creative teams. In a randomized experiment, 148 three-person teams used the same language model at different stages of a timed product-design task. Moving the same 15 minutes of access from the search stage to the selection stage lowered expert-rated creativity by 0.62 standard deviations, with the difference located at the screening step.

Difang Huang, Shumin Yu, Fadong Chen et al.
