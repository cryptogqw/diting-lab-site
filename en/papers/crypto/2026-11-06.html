Replication Certification at Publication: Checkable by Anyone, Anywhere, in Seconds, Forever

In briefReplication certificates: a signed, publication-time record that anyone can verify quickly and later

SummaryThe paper introduces a replication certificate, a cryptographically signed record of deposited data and code files and their logged output. The author reruns the analysis once under a random seed the archive derives from the deposit's hash and a blockchain block, and the archive signs and timestamps the record without rerunning anything. Journals only need to check for the certificate, and anyone can verify or replay each step decades later.

Gary King, Yiqing Xu, Leo Yang Yang

The Promise of Strategic Agentic Scambaiting

In briefStrategic Analysis of Autonomous Scambaiting Agents in Fraud Countermeasures

SummaryThis paper presents a formal model of agent-to-agent fraud, in which autonomous scamming agents and defensive scambaiting agents compete over each other's resources. It analyzes how engagement, resource consumption, belief adaptation, and disruption outcomes evolve over time. The authors report that scammer profitability becomes negative once baiting-agent prevalence exceeds certain thresholds. Under their baseline parameters, when both sides improve at the same rate, the defender's investment in mimicry quality gains more than equivalent scammer investment in detection, so the arms race favors the defender.

Abhishek Satyam, Hossein Siadati, Haadi Jafarian et al.
