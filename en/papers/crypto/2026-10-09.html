Graphene double quantum dot as a solid-state two-level system in the THz range

In briefGraphene double quantum dot as a terahertz-range solid-state two-level system

SummaryThe study uses an hBN-encapsulated graphene double quantum dot to form an artificial molecule, with bonding and antibonding states separated by up to 0.6 meV. This corresponds to a resonance frequency of 0.145 THz. Low-temperature transport measurements combined with nonequilibrium Green's function modeling provide direct access to parameters such as tunneling rates. The authors propose the device as a terahertz two-level system compatible with on-chip integration.

Alexis Wietzke, Solen Coeymans, Benjamin Hue et al.

Understanding Verification Vulnerabilities in Cross-chain Bridges: An Empirical Study

In briefA study of verification flaws in cross-chain bridge smart contracts

SummaryThis paper presents the first systematic empirical study of vulnerabilities in the verification logic of cross-chain bridge contracts, which validate external-chain data such as state proofs and signatures. The authors analyzed 588 security audit reports from 36 real-world bridge projects and identified 11 vulnerability patterns grouped into 5 categories. They built a dataset of 830 vulnerability instances across 786 contract files. Evaluation shows existing tools cover these patterns only in limited ways, and the best of four LLMs reaches 51.2% Macro-F1, motivating detection methods tailored to cross-chain verification.

Yuying Wang (Institute of Computing Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences, China et al.

SwingGuard: Risk-Conditioned Multi-Agent Swing Trading with Dual-Timescale Governance

In briefSwingGuard: Risk-Conditioned Multi-Agent Crypto Swing Trading with Two-Speed Oversight

SummaryThis paper presents SwingGuard, a long-only multi-agent framework for crypto swing trading that operates on two timescales. A strategy layer has analysts weigh technical, sentiment, on-chain and macro evidence, then a portfolio architect sets a target position conditioned on current risk. A five-minute risk controller monitors risk and constrains execution until the next strategy revision. Tests on Bitcoin and Ether report positive returns after transaction costs and shallow drawdowns, with a joint portfolio Sharpe ratio of 2.98 versus 2.79 for a post-hoc risk variant.

Junzhou Su, Yichen Luo, Yebo Feng et al.

AgentValve: Sybil-Resilient Payment Authorization for Permissionless AI Markets

In briefAgentValve: A payment authorization protocol resistant to Sybil attacks in open AI markets

SummaryThis paper presents AgentValve, a protocol that authorizes AI agent payments in permissionless markets while resisting Sybil attacks, where adversaries fabricate identities and feedback to inflate provider reputation. It models reputation as finite capacity in a directed trust graph and uses a smart contract to reserve capacity for each admitted payment. Experiments using six months of Ethereum data and synthetic workloads report that payment exposure and capacity depletion stay bounded with up to 10,000 synthetic Sybil identities. The witness-commit mechanism reduces admission cost by up to 40.8%, with common payments costing about $0.04 to $0.21.

Jianting Zhang, Zhongtang Luo, and Aniket Kate

In briefFrom Imbalance to Evidence: A Deep Classifier Test for Very Uneven Sample Sizes

SummaryThis paper studies how to compare two data distributions when one sample is far smaller than the other, a setting where classifier-based two-sample tests lose power. The authors propose EIAD-C2ST, a classifier-agnostic framework that turns the surplus majority-sample observations into useful testing evidence, with a scalable implementation for deep classifiers. Efficient Unbiasable Verifiable Random Functions via (Non-)Black-Box Reductions

In briefEfficient Unbiasable Verifiable Random Functions via Reductions

SummaryThis paper presents simple, practical constructions of verifiable random functions (VRFs) that meet strong unbiasability notions introduced by Giunta and Stewart at Eurocrypt 2024. A compiler turns any VRF into an unbiasable VRF in the standard model, without setup, using only symmetric-key primitives and negligible extra computation. The authors also give a non-black-box reduction with polynomial loss under a mild condition, enabling a provably secure "Hash-then-VRF" construction in place of the heuristic "VUF-then-Hash" approach at almost no cost.

WhyGives a symmetric-key compiler making any VRF unbiasable in the standard model, plus a novel polynomial-loss non-black-box reduction; proofs-focused with no experiments.

In briefDiffusionART: an efficient, stable RL method for fine-tuning diffusion models

SummaryThis paper introduces DiffusionART, an online reinforcement learning method that fine-tunes diffusion models using black-box rewards. The authors report efficient, stable training across image and video generation, including fewer GPU hours than DiffusionNFT and Flow-GRPO-Fast to reach comparable late-stage mean rewards on HunyuanVideo-13B.

In briefOne Node, Two Roles: Competing Rewards for Verification and Attention in Rollups
