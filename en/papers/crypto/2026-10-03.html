Priority Gas Auction Cadence and Searcher Competition: Evidence from Flashblocks on Base

In briefShorter Auction Windows and Arbitrage Competition: Base Flashblocks Evidence

SummaryThe paper studies how Base's July 2025 switch from one 2-second priority gas auction to ten 200 ms auctions changed bidding by automated arbitrageurs (searchers). Using on-chain data and a difference-in-differences design, it estimates a 0.187 gwei fall in the effective priority fee, which it identifies as the causal effect. The revert-rate response is reported only descriptively.

WhyUses Base's switch from one 2s auction to ten 200ms auctions as a natural experiment, with difference-in-differences identifying fee compression and a new auction model.

Pranay Anchuri, Edward W. Felten, Akaki Mamageishvili

Quantifying Ethereum Energy Consumption via Network Mapping

In briefEstimating Ethereum's Energy Use by Mapping Its Network

SummaryEthereum's electricity use fell about 99.95% after switching from proof of work to proof of stake. The authors crawl consensus and execution layer nodes, assign each peer a wattage based on publicly advertised attributes such as client software, hardware architecture, hosting provider and validator role, and use a Random Forest to estimate incomplete peers. Across 6,934 peers from two June 2026 crawls, reachable nodes sum to about 415 kW, or 3.63 GWh per year if sustained.

Yahn Costa Hackspacher, Cornelius Ihle, Vasundhara Shaw et al.
