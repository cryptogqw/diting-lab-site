In briefCorrectable Enrollment and One-Transaction Seed Proofs for Ethereum's Post-Quantum Transition

SummaryThis paper proposes a migration design for Ethereum to post-quantum cryptography. Owners can cheaply enroll post-quantum keys using their existing signatures before ECDSA is scheduled for retirement, and can correct the enrollment with a zero-knowledge proof of their BIP-39 seed until a common deadline. The authors prove account guarantees, model-check the rules, implement the design in go-ethereum and a Lean consensus client, and report a proof of about 190 KB that verifies in 38 ms.
