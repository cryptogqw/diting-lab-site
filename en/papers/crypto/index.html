In briefNeon-HTLC: Collateral-free hash time-locked contracts with logarithmic timeouts

SummaryThis paper addresses the vulnerability of hashed time-lock contracts (HTLCs) to bribery attacks, where miners can be incentivized to censor honest transactions. It shows that existing incentive-compatible HTLCs must trade off between collateral and timeout, and proves this tradeoff holds for a generalized class called two-phase HTLCs, along with identifying optimal parameters.
