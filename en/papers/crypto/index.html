In briefUsing quantum annealing to detect and locate gamma-ray sources in AGILE data

Tight Characterizations of AMM Mechanism Design under Weak Efficiency

In briefTight limits on fair AMM mechanism design under weak efficiency

SummaryThis paper studies miner extractable value (MEV) in automated market makers (AMMs), where block builders can profit by controlling transaction order. It proves that no deterministic, anonymous batch AMM can be both incentive compatible and weakly locally efficient under plain consensus without fair sequencing, even with censorship resilience and unlimited block space.
