High-sensitivity sensing technology of 10 MHz radio frequency electric field based on Rydberg atoms

In briefHigh-sensitivity 10 MHz radio field sensing using Rydberg atoms

SummaryThis paper proposes and demonstrates a quantum sensing scheme for radio frequency electric fields in the 3-30 MHz shortwave band, using cesium Rydberg states and electromagnetically induced transparency. It uses an all-infrared optical setup with 852 nm, 1470 nm, and 780 nm lasers to prepare the 49P3/2 Rydberg state in a room-temperature cesium vapor cell. The state is then probed non-destructively through ladder-type four-level EIT spectroscopy.

Solution Size Growth as an Obstacle to Iterative Refinement for Linear Optimization

In briefSolution size growth in iterative refinement for linear optimization

SummaryIterative refinement boosts solution precision by solving a sequence of related problems. This paper studies how the solution sizes produced by this process grow when applied to linear programs. It exhibits a class of problems where solution size can grow exponentially with the number of refinement iterations. The authors also show that avoiding this requires a black-box solver to be "lucky", and that simplex with Bland's rule exhibits the exponential growth.

Parametric Proof-Carrying Compilation for Quantum Error-Correction

In briefParametric proof-carrying compilation for quantum error correction

SummaryThe paper presents PC-QEC, a proof-carrying compilation framework for quantum error correction parameterized by code distance and syndrome-extraction scheme, covering Standard, Flag, Shor and Knill schemes. For each family and scheme, code-algebra proofs and fault-aware Hoare proofs combine into a finite symbolic certificate whose size is independent of code distance. The authors demonstrate family-wide guarantees for five code families, and for the rotated Surface code one Standard Scheme certificate proves a distance lower bound d for every odd d≥3 in about 214 seconds on average. Lean 4 mechanizes the metatheory and exact circuit-level distances for canonical Surface, hypergraph-product and Toric code families.

Adaptive Quantum Metrology of Gravitational Waves

In briefAdaptive quantum sensing scheme for detecting gravitational waves

SummaryThis paper proposes a feedback-controlled quantum metrology scheme for detecting weak gravitational waves through their direct energy exchange with light. The method adaptively adjusts the SU(2) control direction based on current estimates of the wave's frequency and phase, so frequency shifts add up across intervals and are read out as an optical phase difference. It extends to elliptically polarized waves by using two sensing channels at 45 degrees to measure the plus and cross components separately. In the ideal coherent regime, the quantum Fisher information scales as T_int²T_ph², and for fixed photon number the ideal sensitivity shows Heisenberg-like 1/n scaling.

Wan-Ting He, Shuang-Quan Ma, and Ke-Mi Xu

In briefQubit encoding of the mass observable in quantized Schwarzschild interior

SummaryThis paper presents a quantum-computing framework for the mass observable of the Wheeler–DeWitt quantized Schwarzschild black-hole interior. The authors build finite-dimensional representations of the mass operator in the physical sector of correlated two-mode oscillator states and map them to qubit operators via Pauli decomposition. BP-based qLDPC Decoding under Long-range Correlated Noise: Trapping Configurations and Prior Mismatch

In briefBP-based quantum LDPC decoding under long-range correlated noise and prior mismatch

SummaryThe paper tests two BP-based quantum LDPC decoders, Relay-BP and beam search, which were designed for independent noise, on spatially correlated noise with unchanged single-error probabilities. Correlated errors are generated from a hidden Gaussian Markov random field that controls correlation strength and range. The authors find that correlation can change logical error rates by orders of magnitude and alter how the decoders rank against BP-OSD. They also report that correlation mainly raises the chance of known trapping configurations, and that supplying marginals conditioned on the hidden field recovers most lost performance, pointing to prior mismatch as a major cause.

In briefTrade-off between trainability and classical simulability in IQP-based RL policies

SummaryThis paper studies reinforcement learning policies built from parameterized IQP quantum circuits, whose measurement outcomes are mapped to a smaller action space by a classical decoder.
