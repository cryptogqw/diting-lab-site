Gaussian and Qubit Semigroup Dynamics beyond GKSL

In briefSemigroup Quantum Dynamics Beyond the Standard GKSL Master Equation

SummaryThis paper studies semigroup dynamics for open quantum systems that do not take the standard GKSL master equation form. Such dynamics can arise in physical systems describing long-time asymptotic behavior and apply only to a restricted set of initial states.
