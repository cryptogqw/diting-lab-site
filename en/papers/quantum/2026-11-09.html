Symanzik Effective Hamiltonian for Lattice Quantum Field Theories

In briefSymanzik effective Hamiltonian for lattice quantum field theories

SummaryThis paper proposes a framework for constructing the Symanzik effective Hamiltonian of lattice quantum field theories directly from dimensional analysis and symmetries in the Hamiltonian formulation. It enables classification of all operators that can contribute at a given order in the spatial lattice spacing, and this list can be reduced using equations of motion and other identities. The framework is demonstrated for φ⁴ theory and pure gauge theory, with results consistent with earlier work for classical improvement. It is intended as a basis for a future non-perturbative improvement programme in the Hamiltonian formulation.

Fernanda Steffens, Carsten Urbach

Attainable and unattainable quantum precision limits in ultrastrong light--matter coupling

In briefReachable and unreachable quantum precision limits in ultrastrong light-matter coupling

SummaryThis paper studies how quantum precision depends on the gauge choice in ultrastrongly coupled light-matter systems. The transformation between gauges involves the coupling strength g, so each truncation gives a different family of results. The quantum Fisher information follows an exact parabola across gauges, and for harmonic matter it is minimized at a universal value set by the matter and cavity frequencies. Only the minimal-coupling bound can be reached, and the gauges differ by a factor of 9.6 with opposite trends.

Wangjun Lu, Qing Yu

Discrete time crystals stabilized by bounded measurement noise

In briefDiscrete time crystals kept stable by limiting measurement noise

SummaryThis paper asks whether continuous weak measurement on a periodically driven, long-range interacting spin chain can sustain discrete time-crystalline period doubling. Typical quantum trajectories reach a state without subharmonic response, while the most-probable trajectory, with measurement fluctuations suppressed, shows robust period doubling. The authors introduce a bounded-noise protocol restricting measurement fluctuations to a finite window, finding that narrow windows preserve the order, intermediate windows give coexisting ordered and disordered trajectories, and wide windows destroy it.

Pallabi Chatterjee, Tanay Nag, and Ranjan Modak

Parameterized equivalence checking of quantum circuit generators via weighted automata

In briefChecking quantum circuit generators for equivalence across all parameters

SummaryThis paper presents a method to check whether a quantum circuit generator matches a reference for all parameter values, under six equivalence relations. The generator builds circuits from a qubit count and a bit string. For ladder programs, the authors prove that above a computable threshold the circuit matrices form a uniform matrix product operator, a weighted automaton whose dimension does not depend on qubit count. Equivalence reduces to a zeroness check on a difference automaton, performed modulo primes. Experiments show the method finds errors missed by fixed-size checks and gave no incorrect verdicts.

Semiclassical Asymptotics of Entanglement in Torus-Link States

In briefSemiclassical asymptotics of entanglement in torus-link quantum states

SummaryThis paper studies the entanglement entropy of topological quantum states built from T_{d,dn} torus links in SU(2) Chern-Simons theory at level k. In the semiclassical limit k→∞, the authors derive the asymptotic behavior of moments of the reduced density matrix, finding that the limiting entropy is governed by the two edge regions while the bulk contribution is suppressed. The result is expressed using Hurwitz zeta functions and depends on k modulo n. For large d, the entropy reduces to a binary entropy set by left and right edge weights, and the two limits k→∞ and d→∞ are shown to commute.

Vikash Ganwariya, Simran Sain, Siddharth Dwivedi
