Exact quantum Hamming-weight computation at optimal depth and minimum width

In briefExact quantum circuit counting how many qubits are 1, at optimal depth and width

SummaryThe paper builds an exact quantum circuit that computes the Hamming weight (the number of 1s) of an n-qubit register with depth O(log n) and size O(n log n). It uses only the n input qubits plus ceil(log2(n+1)) binary output qubits, and both depth and total width are stated to be optimal. The authors also give exact circuits for all symmetric Boolean functions and apply the counter to prepare balanced Dicke states for even n.

In briefDe Finetti Structures in Quantum Networks: how reduced states of subsystems in large quantum networks converge to a standard form

SummaryThis paper studies N identical quantum systems on a graph's vertices, coupled pairwise along edges, with K vertices designated as the system of interest and the rest as a reservoir. Upper Bound on the Correlation Energy of a Dense Fermi Gas in the Thermodynamic Limit

In briefUpper bound on correlation energy of dense Fermi gases

SummaryThis paper proves that the random phase approximation gives an upper bound on the correlation energy of a three-dimensional, weakly interacting Fermi gas in the thermodynamic limit at high density. It uses a mean-field scaling and a correlated trial state built from collective particle-hole excitations on Fermi surface patches via a quasi-bosonic Bogoliubov transformation. The key step controls the position-space localization of these excitations, and an iterated Duhamel expansion reduces the problem to an effective quadratic Hamiltonian that is approximately diagonalized.
