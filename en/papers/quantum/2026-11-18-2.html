Entanglement cost and classical capacity of multimode Gaussian states and channels

In briefEntanglement cost and classical capacity of multimode Gaussian states and channels

SummaryThe paper studies the entanglement cost and classical capacity of multimode Gaussian states and channels in continuous-variable quantum information. The authors prove that entanglement of formation, Gaussian entanglement of formation, and entanglement cost coincide for finite-energy bipartite Gaussian states, and that this value lower-bounds these quantities for non-Gaussian states with the same covariance matrix. They determine the entanglement cost and the energy-constrained classical capacity of every multimode Gaussian channel, both reducing to Gaussian covariance optimizations. The capacity is achieved by pure Gaussian signals with Gaussian displacement modulation.

WhyThe authors prove equality of three entanglement measures for Gaussian states and determine multimode Gaussian channel capacity and entanglement cost via proofs.

Yuan Gao, Gerardo Adesso

Development of a Discrete Variable Representation of a Field-Adaptive Hermite Basis for Accurate Modeling of Magnetically Confined Systems

In briefA Method for More Accurate Modeling of Particles Trapped in Magnetic Fields

SummaryThis paper develops a magnetic-field-dependent Hermite-function discrete variable representation (Hermite-B DVR) that adapts to magnetic confinement. It efficiently represents wavefunctions across a wide range of field strengths using fewer basis functions. The method is benchmarked on two-dimensional quantum dots in perpendicular magnetic fields, showing excellent agreement with analytical spectra and wavefunctions. It is also applied to arbitrary confinement potentials and tilted fields.

Athul Tom, Pratyush Bhattacharjya, Sangita Sen

Exact classical bounds of bipartite correlation Bell inequalities using improved branch-and-bound

In briefExact classical bounds for bipartite Bell inequalities via improved branch-and-bound

SummaryThis paper presents an improved branch-and-bound algorithm for computing the exact classical (local) bounds of bipartite correlation Bell inequalities. It uses a see-saw-based heuristic to obtain a strong initial lower bound and greedy row reordering to tighten pruning, with an OpenMP parallel implementation. Benchmarks show large runtime reductions on random matrices, and exact computations are reported for square matrices up to 130×130 and rectangular matrices up to 81×321. Applications include improved lower bounds on finite-order Grothendieck constants for d=3,4,5,7 and XOR games with quantum-to-classical ratios exceeding √2.

Istv\'an M\'arton, Erika Bene, P\'eter Divi\'anszky et al.
