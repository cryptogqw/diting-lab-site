Experimental demonstration of phase-conjugated twin-wave coherent reception over 100 km with injection locking assisted phase stabilization

In briefPhase-conjugate twin-wave coherent reception over 100 km of fiber using injection locking

SummaryThis paper experimentally demonstrates a coherent receiver that recovers 200-MBaud signals with modulation formats up to 256-QAM after 100 km of optical fiber. Optical injection locking regenerates the local oscillator from a reference sent through the link, and pilot-assisted optical feedback stabilizes the signal-to-local-oscillator phase so that phase-conjugated twin waves combine at one balanced photodetector. The reported signal-to-noise ratio exceeds the ideal single-mode homodyne standard quantum limit benchmark and conventional heterodyne results by about 3 dB and 6 dB, respectively.
