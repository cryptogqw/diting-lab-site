Multipartite entanglement structure of crystal quenches

In briefMultipartite entanglement in crystal quenches: how tripartite entanglement arises and spreads after a quantum quench

SummaryThis paper studies how multipartite entanglement emerges and spreads after a quantum quench, focusing on quasiparticle multiplets generated by crystal quenches in a tight-binding chain. SummaryThis paper studies XYZ codes, which extend the CSS framework by adding Y-type stabilizers.
