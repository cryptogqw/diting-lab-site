Provable circuit-level thresholds for non-abelian topological phases

In briefProven circuit-level thresholds for non-abelian topological phases, a method for building error-protected quantum memory

SummaryThis paper presents an explicit scheme for realizing a quantum memory in any two-dimensional non-chiral topological order. The authors construct syndrome extraction circuits based on the Turaev-Viro path integral, along with a classical algorithm for decoding on the fly. They prove that the scheme has a threshold under a local stochastic circuit-level noise model and discuss how it could be generalized to implement topological logical gates.

WhyThe authors build a quantum memory for any 2D non-chiral topological order via Turaev-Viro-based syndrome circuits and an on-the-fly decoder, proving a circuit-level threshold.

Quantum Knowledge Distillation: State-of-the-art, Applications and Open Problems

In briefSurvey of knowledge distillation in quantum machine learning, covering methods, applications, and open problems.

SummaryThis survey reviews knowledge distillation, a technique for transferring predictions or representations between learning models, as applied in quantum machine learning. It classifies methods by teacher-student configuration and by the role quantum resources play, treating quantum-inspired methods separately from classical simulation or quantum hardware execution. The authors find that distillation can improve some quantum student models, but gains depend on task and architecture, and hardware evaluations remain limited. They conclude by identifying priorities such as controlled benchmarking, noise-aware learning, and reproducible evaluation.

Aldana Al-Sulaiti, Kuan-Cheng Chen, Muhammad Bilal Akram Dastagir et al.
