In briefNonergodicity from Hilbert-subspace cloning: a partial breakdown of ergodicity in quantum many-body systems.

SummaryThe paper studies a class of nonintegrable quantum many-body systems built from single Pauli strings. These systems have many noncommuting local conserved quantities and partially break ergodicity because of exponentially degenerate energy spectra. The authors propose a mechanism called Hilbert-subspace cloning, where the same chaotic Hamiltonian acts on exponentially many disconnected Hilbert subspaces. Dynamics within each clone thermalizes, but quantum coherence between clones persists and can be detected by local observables. As a result, long-time local observables are described by a coherence-preserving partial Gibbs ensemble.
