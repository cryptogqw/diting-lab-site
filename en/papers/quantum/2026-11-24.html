It uses cascaded standard single-mode fiber segments, with an optical switch selecting a short or long path in each segment, and Faraday mirrors at both ends that automatically compensate random polarization fluctuations from the fibers. Numerical experiments show a mean output polarization fidelity better than 99% over a 50-nm range around the central wavelength.

Salem F. Hegazy, Salah S. A. Obayya, Bahaa E. A. Saleh

Noise-Immune Attractors in Stochastically Driven Non-Hermitian Systems

In briefNoise-resistant attractor states in randomly driven non-Hermitian systems

SummaryThis paper studies non-Hermitian quantum systems subject to random time-dependent fluctuations. The authors derive an exact noise master equation for Gaussian fluctuations and identify a noise-invariance mechanism driven by T-symmetry-broken Liouvillian attractors. This mechanism yields stable defect-density plateaus and an exact classification into noise-independent, fully polarized, and continuously tunable regimes. The results are examined in an imbalanced-pairing non-Hermitian Kitaev model.

Hanieh Najafzadeh, Radmehr Jafari, Abdollah Langari
