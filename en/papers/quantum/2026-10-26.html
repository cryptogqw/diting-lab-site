Bargmann-invariant witness of the graviton commutator

In briefA Bargmann-invariant test for quantized gravity via the graviton commutator

SummaryThe paper proposes that the phase of a Bargmann invariant from two matter-wave interferometers can signal quantum gravity, since no branchwise-definite classical gravitational field can reproduce it. For matter linearly coupled to linearized gravity, the interference coherence factorizes into a Newtonian phase, the graviton state's characteristic functional, and a commutator phase. The authors argue that decoherence alone cannot prove gravity is quantized, because the first two terms are classically reproducible for Wigner-positive graviton states. The Bargmann phase isolates the commutator term, which is independent of the graviton state. For gravity this effect is far below experimental reach, but trapped-ion analogs could test it at order unity.
