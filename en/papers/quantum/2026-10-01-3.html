An exponential query advantage from access to Stinespring dilation unitaries in quantum channel learning

In briefExponential query advantage from Stinespring dilation unitary access in quantum channel learning

SummaryThis paper studies learning how close an unknown quantum channel is to a unitary channel, measured by the optimal average gate fidelity (OAGF). For constant Kraus rank and environment dimension polylog(d), an algorithm estimates OAGF using polylog(d) queries to the Stinespring dilation unitary and its inverse. In briefSingle-shot state preparation threshold: a rigorous theorem and statistical mechanical mapping

SummaryThis paper studies the threshold for preparing logical codewords using one round of noisy syndrome measurements, called single-shot state preparation. The authors prove that for CSS quantum LDPC codes with at least logarithmic distance, linear confinement alone guarantees a nonzero threshold, and under linear soundness the residual data error is local stochastic. Fast and Sure-ious Quantum Process Tomography

In briefFast, reliable quantum process tomography via a closed-form projection that extends quantum state tomography methods to channel estimation

SummaryThis paper studies quantum process tomography (QPT), which estimates quantum channels from measurements, typically by lifting quantum state tomography (QST) methods through the Choi isomorphism while enforcing trace preservation. The authors show that the widely used partial normalization is the exact fidelity projection onto Choi states of quantum channels. As a result, any QST algorithm with fidelity guarantees yields a QPT algorithm with the same sample complexity via a closed-form congruence. The non-adaptive Fidelity Projected Least Squares protocol recovers the Choi rank and reaches infidelity ε using channel uses proportional to 1/ε. Numerical results show improved accuracy over projected least squares for low-Choi-rank channels, with orders-of-magnitude gains in the time and memory of the trace-preservation step and the estimate.

WhyShows partial normalization is the exact fidelity projection onto Choi states, transferring QST guarantees to QPT with sample-complexity bounds and numerical validation.

A. Afham, Sayantan Sen, and Marco Tomamichel

SummaryThis paper presents an entanglement distillation protocol that uses an arbitrary [[n,k,d]] stabilizer code to turn noisy N-qubit GHZ states into k logical copies.
