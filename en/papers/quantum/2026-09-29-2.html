Multilevel Plaquette-Space Sampling for Lattice Gauge Theories with Local Constraint Solves

In briefSampling gauge field plaquettes exactly for lattice gauge theories via local constraint solves

SummaryThis paper proposes a multilevel normalizing-flow method that samples lattice gauge theory configurations directly in plaquette space while enforcing Bianchi constraints exactly. A coarse-to-fine factorization turns a globally coupled constraint problem into a sequence of local solves. The method is validated for U(1) in two and four dimensions and for SU(2) in two dimensions, where it outperforms link-space baselines, with the advantage growing toward weak coupling.

Ankur Singha, Jacob Finkenrath, Karl Jansen et al.

In briefA silicon-on-SiC chip platform for coupling to telecom-band vanadium defects

SummaryThe paper presents a silicon-on-SiC photonic platform in which silicon nanocavities evanescently couple to shallow vanadium dopants in commercial 4H-SiC, improving photon collection at the zero-phonon line. Speed-Fisher Information: Chaos and Irreversibility in Classical and Quantum Dynamics

In briefSpeed-Fisher Information: A Unified Measure of Chaos and Irreversibility

SummaryThe paper proposes a unified way to study classical and quantum chaos by measuring how a system's stationary states respond to slow driving. This sensitivity to average protocol speed is called speed-Fisher information and is linked to irreversible entropy production. The authors show that chaos appears as divergence of this information with protocol time, controlled by low-frequency spectral weight of the perturbation. The approach applies to Hamiltonian quantum and classical systems and extends to non-Hamiltonian classical flows, illustrated with simple examples.

Nachiket Karve, Nathan Rose, David Campbell et al.

In briefThe Power of Oracle Access: Optimal Complexity for the Abelian State Hidden Subgroup Problem

SummaryThis paper studies the state hidden subgroup problem, which asks for the symmetries of an unknown quantum state. For finite abelian groups, it gives a query-model algorithm using O(log(|G/H|)/√ε) forward and inverse queries together with a matching lower bound. Optimal performance in a chaotic Floquet quantum battery

In briefOptimal performance of a chaotic Floquet quantum battery, explained as a quantum device that stores and releases energy.

SummaryThe paper studies a battery of N uniformly interacting qubits charged by periodic transverse kicks and disorder in the interaction direction. Disorder takes the dynamics out of the permutation-symmetric subspace, increasing stored energy; in the large-disorder limit, random matrix theory gives exact results and energy fluctuations become negligible. In briefDiscrete scale invariance in Ising mesons: how kink motion in a magnet gives log-periodic energy levels

SummaryThe paper studies composite excitations in a transverse-field Ising chain with long-range bonds decaying as 1/r^4. SummaryThis paper proposes candidate lower bounds on the real Grothendieck constants K_G(d) and their infinite-dimensional limit K_G using rotationally invariant cubic kernels.
