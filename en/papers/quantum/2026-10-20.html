Separability does not certify zero-noise extrapolation: Factorial diagnostics in dynamic quantum circuits

In briefSeparability does not validate zero-noise extrapolation: factorial diagnostics on dynamic quantum circuits

SummaryThe paper runs a pre-specified 2^3 factorial experiment on three IBM dynamic quantum circuits to test whether separately amplifying gate, latency, and mid-circuit measurement errors yields a reliable zero-noise limit. It separates observable separability, response-model adequacy, and calibration, finding that weak interactions do not guarantee an accurate limit. Multi-knob extrapolations show smaller median errors, yet most original strategies still exclude the known ideal value for several observables. The authors present this as a diagnostic of the implemented response surface, not a certification of channel independence or mitigation accuracy.

In briefTwo bosons with density-assisted tunnelling on a tilted 1D lattice

SummaryThis paper studies how two bosons move in a tilted one-dimensional optical lattice when both standard and density-assisted tunnelling are present.
