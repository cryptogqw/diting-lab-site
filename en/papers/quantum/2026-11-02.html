Quantum Path Finder: Navigating Constraint Space via Non-Abelian Gauge Dynamics

In briefQuantum Path Finder: a new quantum algorithm design based on gauge dynamics in constraint space

SummaryThis paper introduces Quantum Path Finder (QPF), a quantum algorithm design paradigm that uses non-Abelian gauge dynamics within a constraint-protected degenerate manifold, with each Boolean variable encoded as a qutrit that includes an unassigned state. The authors illustrate the framework on unstructured search, complete 3-XORSAT, and satisfiable 3-SAT instances.
