In briefDetuned cavity boosts spectroscopy precision for dipole moment estimation

In briefNon-Bloch dynamical quantum phase transitions in chains with memory

SummaryDynamical quantum phase transitions in periodic chains are governed by real Bloch momenta. This paper identifies non-Bloch transitions that appear only in open chains and are governed by complex momenta, arising in a Hermitian chain coupled to a simple substrate with exactly treated memory. The effect stems from the point-gap winding number W of the projected propagator.
