Quantum Submodular Maximization

In briefQuantum Submodular Maximization: quantum queries for approximately maximizing non-negative submodular functions

SummaryThis paper studies how many quantum queries are needed to maximize a non-negative submodular function, both without constraints and, for monotone functions, under a cardinality limit k. In the exact reversible digital value-oracle model, the unconstrained algorithm reaches an expected (1/2-ε) approximation with O(log n) queries. This is exponentially fewer than the Ω(n/log n) queries that classical randomized algorithms need for ratios above 1/4. For the cardinality-constrained case, the algorithm achieves a (1-1/e-ε) approximation and gives at least a quadratic speedup over classical methods when k=o(n), up to logarithmic factors.

In briefCollapse time of weak measurement across N entangled sites
