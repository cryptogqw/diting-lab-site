Detection of genuine multipartite entanglement by means of entanglement monotones

In briefDetecting genuine multipartite entanglement with entanglement monotones

SummaryThis paper seeks pure-state entanglement monotones that can certify a stronger form of genuine multipartite entanglement called irreducible entanglement, where a state cannot be written as a product of states with at most (D-1)-partite entanglement. The authors first show that no reasonable genuine multipartite entanglement entropy can satisfy both LOCC-monotonicity and subadditivity. After relaxing this to LO-monotonicity, they present a simple method for generating monotones with the desired properties. They further show that a subfamily of these monotones detects any form of genuine tripartite entanglement in three-qubit systems.
