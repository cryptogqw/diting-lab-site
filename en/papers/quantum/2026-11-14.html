Dressed higher-form symmetries in mixed state quantum matter

In briefDressed higher-form symmetries in mixed quantum matter

SummaryThis paper shows how to build emergent strong higher-form symmetries in mixed quantum states that still act unitarily on Hilbert space. The construction uses quasi-local recovery channels from quantum error correction to map the system to a nearby simple stabilizer model. The authors prove that with an appropriate deterministic recovery, the resulting operators remain unitary. Unruh-induced decoherence and entanglement degradation in Ramsey interferometry

In briefUnruh-induced decoherence and entanglement degradation in Ramsey interferometry

SummaryThis paper proposes a modified Ramsey interferometry protocol that uses fringe visibility to probe decoherence linked to the Unruh effect. Unruh-DeWitt detectors coupled to a massless scalar field produce pure dephasing without transitions between internal energy levels. For a uniformly accelerated detector of finite spatial extent, an analytical dephasing function separates inertial vacuum fluctuations from the thermal Unruh contribution. When the thermal part dominates, fringe visibility decays exponentially at a rate proportional to proper acceleration. In the bipartite extension, the joint fringe visibility exactly matches concurrence once the cross-term contribution vanishes, so entanglement degradation can be read directly from interference contrast.
