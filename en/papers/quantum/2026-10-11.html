In briefNon-stationary quantum systems linked to one-dimensional stationary scattering

SummaryThis paper shows that stationary scattering in one dimension can be reformulated as the evolution of an effective non-stationary, non-unitary two-level quantum system, from which reflection and transmission amplitudes are extracted. In briefQuantum circuit model of Compton scattering and incomplete Bell measurements

SummaryThe paper treats massless tree-level Compton scattering as an effective two-qubit operation on electron and photon helicities. In the Bell basis the scattering map splits into two parity sectors and can be represented by a simple quantum circuit containing one nonunitary filter.
