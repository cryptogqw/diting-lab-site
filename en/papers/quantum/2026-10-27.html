Network nonlocality with tripartite entangled sources

In briefNetwork nonlocality from three-qubit entangled sources

SummaryQuantum networks are built from independent sources, and most nonlocality studies have used sources emitting two-party entangled states. This paper studies sources emitting three entangled qubits and asks how network nonlocality relates to the single-source Mermin and Svetlichny inequalities. The answer depends on the wiring: when two input-free central nodes share every source, any Svetlichny violation yields a network violation, while sharing a single central node requires the source to violate the Mermin inequality.

In briefε-smoothed Entanglement of Formation and Its Link to Moreau-Yosida Approximation

SummaryThe paper defines a family of convex continuous functions E_{F,ε} on bipartite quantum states by applying the Mora-Piani-Briegel ε-measure construction, using trace norm distance, to the Entanglement of Formation (EoF). Quantum computation of energy level degeneracy and thermodynamical properties of spin systems

In briefQuantum computing method for energy degeneracy and thermodynamics of spin systems

SummaryThe paper proposes a hybrid quantum-classical method that uses one ancilla qubit to measure the partition function at imaginary inverse temperatures, reconstructing energy-level degeneracies and thermodynamic properties of spin systems. For spectra on a known equally spaced energy grid, the inverse discrete Fourier transform gives exact degeneracies without noise. The authors demonstrate the method on frustrated Ising antiferromagnets on periodic Kagome graphs with up to 36 spins using the ibm_kingston processor. Despite deep circuits and hardware noise, the reconstructed spectra keep their dominant features and the heat capacities reproduce the main temperature dependence of exact results.
