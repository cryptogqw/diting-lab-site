In briefUsing topology to classify all mutually unbiased bases in six dimensions

SummaryThe paper studies mutually unbiased bases (MUBs), which are complementary quantum measurements in which certainty in one basis gives no information about the others. Using the topology of the space of complex Hadamard matrices, which describe transitions between MUBs, the authors classify all sets of MUBs in dimension six.
