In briefVacuum correlation measures expressed as CFT partition functions

SummaryThe paper shows that in any d-dimensional CFT, the vacuum sandwiched Rényi mutual information of integer order n between two disjoint balls maps to a partition function on a manifold with topology #^{n-1}(S^1×S^{d-1}).
