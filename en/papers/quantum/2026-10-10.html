Partial-coherence weak measurement of polarization qubits with a temporal pointer: theory and experiment

In briefWeak measurement of polarization qubits using a partially coherent temporal pointer

SummaryThis paper studies weak measurement of polarization qubits using a temporal pointer, aiming to characterize weak values even when the pointer is only partially coherent.
