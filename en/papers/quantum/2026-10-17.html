Collective Strong Quantum Mpemba Effect in Accelerated Quantum Detectors

In briefCollective strong quantum Mpemba effect in accelerated quantum detectors

SummaryThe paper studies uniformly accelerated quantum detectors that couple collectively to the Minkowski vacuum. For small transverse separation, a strong quantum Mpemba effect occurs, where a state farther from equilibrium relaxes faster than a closer one. The effect arises from two reflection sectors whose populations are conserved, with weak intersector transfer producing a slow relaxation mode. The same mechanism is said to extend to detectors coupled to a common thermal field.

Ergotropy of nonequilibrium steady states beyond the full secular approximation

In briefWork extractable from steady states beyond full secular approximation

SummaryThis paper studies the ergotropy, the maximum work extractable by cyclic unitary operations, of the nonequilibrium steady state of two exchange-coupled qubits, each connected to an independent thermal reservoir. It uses a Bloch-Redfield master equation that goes beyond the full secular approximation. For qubits of equal frequency, the steady-state ergotropy is entirely coherent and grows quadratically with temperature bias near equilibrium. For detuned qubits, incoherent ergotropy can appear, and the paper derives an exact analytical criterion for its activation.

Exact Quantum Query Complexity of the Five-Bit Symmetric Boolean Function $\Exact^5_{3,4}$

In briefExact quantum query complexity of the five-bit function EXACT^5_{3,4}

SummaryThis paper studies the Boolean function that outputs 1 exactly when the input Hamming weight is 3 or 4, and proves its exact quantum query complexity is 4. The lower bound uses a fixed rational dual certificate that rules out any exact algorithm with at most three queries. The upper bound follows from a known four-query algorithm for a seven-bit function combined with a padding reduction. The same certificate also gives a positive lower bound on the worst-case error of three-query algorithms, consistent with earlier near-unit success numerical results.

SummaryThis paper studies a holographic model of a logarithmic conformal field theory, where two scaling dimensions coalesce at an exceptional point in a PT-symmetric system. The authors deform the dipole model so the dimensions split into real or complex-conjugate pairs, and derive renormalized two-point functions in closed form.
