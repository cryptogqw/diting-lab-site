Fer expansion converges almost twice as far as Magnus for qubits

In briefFer expansion's convergence range for qubits nearly doubles Magnus's

SummaryThe paper studies two series expansions for the propagator of a time-dependent Hamiltonian: the Fer and Magnus expansions. The Fer expansion's error decreases doubly exponentially with the number of factors, but its convergence was previously guaranteed only below a radius of about 2.6058, smaller than the Magnus radius π. For a qubit, the authors establish a guaranteed convergence threshold of about 6.2106, or 1.98π, almost twice the optimal universal Magnus threshold π.

An Incomplete-Preference Quantum Anscombe--Aumann Representation

In briefA quantum Anscombe–Aumann representation for incomplete preferences

SummaryThis paper develops a generalised Anscombe–Aumann framework in which acts map finitely many states of the world into the effect interval of a finite-dimensional Hilbert space, without assuming complete preferences. Maximally Entangled Mutually Unbiased Bases in Bipartite Systems of Unequal Prime Dimensions

In briefMaximally entangled mutually unbiased bases for unequal prime dimensions

SummaryThe paper studies mutually unbiased bases with prescribed entanglement properties in a bipartite system of unequal prime dimensions p and q (p<q). The standard solution gives p+1 product bases. For dimension d=pq, the authors construct p+1 bases, p of which consist of maximally entangled vectors and are represented by complex Hadamard matrices equivalent to the Fourier matrix F_d. Using topological entanglement entropy as a probe of nonlocal correlations in quantum state diffusion simulations, the authors identify a transition between a critical phase and an area-law phase, with no transition in the clean limit. Deep in the Zeno regime, they derive an effective non-Hermitian Bogoliubov-de Gennes description and a closed-form localization length that agrees with numerics.

Pinaki Singha, Marcin Szyniszewski, Nilanjan Roy et al.
