Long-lived light-driven bimagnons at finite momentum in NiO

In briefLong-lived light-driven bimagnon pairs at finite momentum in NiO

SummaryResearchers pumped NiO with mid-infrared pulses far below its bandgap to excite phonon-assisted two-magnon absorption, then probed the resulting dynamics with time-resolved resonant inelastic X-ray scattering. The one-magnon linewidth and energy stayed essentially unchanged, indicating preserved long-range magnetic order. A pump-induced two-magnon signal peaks about 4 ps after excitation and decays with a time constant of 28±11 ps, far longer than previously reported two-magnon dephasing times.

Xian P. Yang, Christopher T. Parzyck, Martin Cross et al.

In briefDiscrete time crystals stabilized by anti-unitary periodic driving and dynamical freezing

SummaryThe paper proposes stabilizing a discrete time crystal using anti-unitary periodic driving, through a mechanism called dynamical freezing. In briefStatistics of correlation lengths in the many-body localised phase's Fock space

SummaryThis paper studies long-range many-body resonances in the many-body localised phase of disordered quantum systems, which appear as strong inhomogeneities in eigenstate amplitudes on Fock space. Near-Optimal Multiparameter Pure-State Metrology with a Constant Number of Bases

In briefNear-optimal multiparameter quantum metrology using a fixed small number of measurement bases

SummaryThis paper studies multiparameter pure-state quantum metrology, where measurements optimized for different parameters are incompatible. It introduces the Fisher floor, the smallest fraction of quantum Fisher information retained across all pure states and parameter directions, as a measure of uniform sensitivity. The authors prove that a constant number of fixed orthonormal bases, independent of the Hilbert-space dimension, achieves a nearly optimal Fisher floor. They also show that d+1 mutually unbiased bases, when they exist, have a Fisher floor of at most 1/(d+1), so optimal average balance does not ensure uniform sensitivity.

WhyThe authors show a constant number of fixed orthonormal bases yields a dimension-independent near-optimal Fisher floor, with tight bounds and a contrast to MUBs.

Bowen Xiao, Zhiyao Yang, and Huangjun Zhu

Quantum Boltzmann Equation for Static Correlation with Cumulant Kernel Closure

In briefQuantum Boltzmann equation for static electron correlation with cumulant closure

SummaryThis paper proposes a Quantum Boltzmann Equation for static electron correlation, mapping the BBGKY hierarchy onto discrete atomic-orbital matrix mechanics. The two-body cumulant is projected onto its lowest unitary invariant and relaxed toward a hyperbolic-tangent attractor via Markovian collisional relaxation, giving a parameter-free cumulant kernel. The method is tested on the H3 exchange reaction, H4 dissociation, BeH2, H2O and N2 bond cleavage, an 11-molecule dissociation set, and benzyne diradical isomers. The authors report that the kinetic relaxation resolves strong static correlation at zero electronic temperature.
