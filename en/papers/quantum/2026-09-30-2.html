A Separation Between Types of Quantum Oracle Separations

In briefSeparating Types of Quantum Oracles: How Different Quantum Oracle Models Differ in Separating Complexity Classes

SummaryThe paper introduces the notion of meta-complexity of quantum relativization, studying which types of quantum oracles, such as unitary versus state oracles or those closed under inverse, can separate two quantum complexity classes. Using PostBQP and PreciseBQP as a test case, the authors give a complete classification, within their defined oracle families, of which models can separate these classes. The results show that some oracle types leave the classes equal while others, such as superpolynomial-dimensional forward-only diagonal unitaries, produce separations.

SummaryResearchers used widefield nitrogen-vacancy (NV) magnetometry to image the stray magnetic fields of individual LaCrO3 microcrystals made by molten salt synthesis, resolving the antiferromagnetic Néel transition in single particles.
