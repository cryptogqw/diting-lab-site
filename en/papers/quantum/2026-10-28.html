Recovering classical gauge geometry from quantum master constraints

In briefRecovering classical gauge geometry from quantum master constraints

SummaryThe paper asks how the degenerate directions of classical first-class constraints can be recovered when quantum theory is taken as fundamental and the constraints are imposed through a single positive master operator. For a nonnegative master function whose zero set is a Morse–Bott critical manifold, the image of the linearized Hamiltonian vector field is shown to be the symplectic orthogonal of the zero set, with coisotropy equivalent to the linearization having square zero. Three realizations are discussed, including the spectral bottom for quadratic masters and results for the non-Abelian SL(2,R) model.

Alejandro Corichi

Adaptive Information Mining: Approaching the Quantum Limit of Super-Resolution Imaging

In briefAdaptive Information Mining: Reaching the Quantum Limit of Super-Resolution Imaging

SummaryThis paper presents Adaptive Information Mining (AIM), a framework that achieves super-resolution by refining measurements of incoming photons in sequence. A reconfigurable imaging system reshapes its measurement responses to maximize Fisher information. When imaging point sources, the authors report estimation errors 2 to 5 times closer to the fundamental quantum limit than traditional direct imaging.

Seungman Choi, Peter Menart, Andrew Schramka et al.

Homodyne Unravelling and Parameter Estimation in Squeezed Thermal Baths

In briefPaper on homodyne-based tracking and parameter estimation for quantum systems in squeezed thermal baths.

SummaryThe paper derives an alternative stochastic master equation for quantum systems continuously measured by homodyne detection in a squeezed-thermal environment. This form identifies two collapse operators for monitored and unmonitored channels and expresses state evolution through Kraus-like operators, aiding numerical methods and Cramér-Rao bound evaluation. For qubit frequency estimation, the authors compute the Fisher information of continuous homodyne monitoring and find that its steady-state rate still scales exponentially with squeezing at nonzero temperature.

Eoin O'Connor, Francesco Albarelli, Marco G. Genoni
