Steering the Fidelity in State Preparation on Noisy Bosonic Systems with Risk-Seeking Optimization

In briefSteering fidelity in noisy bosonic state preparation using risk-seeking optimization

SummaryState preparation encodes inputs into a target quantum state, which is needed to run quantum algorithms on bosonic quantum computers. Instead of optimizing only the average fidelity over the noisy ensemble, this work aims to maximize the fidelity of a subset of sampled pure states, accepting a lower overall average. Adapting objectives from risk-sensitive optimization, the authors report that risk-seeking objectives reduce the infidelities of some pure states by up to four orders of magnitude compared with standard average-fidelity optimization. They also show the trade-off between the probability of high-fidelity states and the lowest reachable infidelity can be controlled.

Riccardo Piantoni, Riccardo Pellini, Maurizio Ferrari Dacrema et al.
