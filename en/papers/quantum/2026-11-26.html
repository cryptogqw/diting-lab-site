Vacuum correlation measures as CFT partition functions

In briefVacuum correlation measures expressed as CFT partition functions

SummaryThe paper shows that in any d-dimensional CFT, the vacuum sandwiched Rényi mutual information of integer order n between two disjoint balls maps to a partition function on a manifold with topology #^{n-1}(S^1×S^{d-1}). At n=2 it equals the thermal partition function on S^{d-1}, which upper-bounds the von Neumann mutual information. For n>2 the quantity diverges at finite separation due to a hot-spot limit, and explicit CFT evaluations agree well with density matrix computations.

Hewei Frederic Jia

Bound Entanglement with Negative Partial Transpose Exists

In briefBound entanglement with negative partial transpose exists

SummaryThe paper examines a Werner state on C^8 ⊗ C^8 with density matrix (23I − 3SWAP)/1448. It shows that this state has a negative partial transpose and is bound entangled, so no LOCC protocol can distill entanglement from any number of copies. Even with separable operations, the fidelity of a two-qubit output with a Bell state stays at or below about 0.604. The authors conclude that distillable entanglement is neither additive nor convex.

Complementary mechanisms of critical quantum metrology

In briefTwo complementary ways critical quantum systems can sharpen parameter estimation

SummaryThis paper proposes a second mechanism for critical quantum metrology, based on suppressed quantum noise rather than enhanced response to a parameter. It studies coherently radiating spin states (CRSS) of N spin-1/2 particles, which show a second-order transition at r=1. Near criticality, estimating r benefits from enhanced susceptibility, while estimating the phase φ benefits from suppressed noise, and both reach an error scaling of about N^(-2/3), better than the standard quantum limit of N^(-1/2).

The thermodynamic budget of objectivity

In briefThe energy cost of objectivity: how much work it takes to make an event a shared fact

SummaryThis paper examines when an event becomes an objective fact, meaning enough independent physical records exist that no operation can leave every observer ignorant of it. The authors compute the thermodynamic costs of creating, keeping, and destroying such records exactly, using a minimal model in which a driven central qubit writes records into N initially thermal registers. They report that the work needed to create a record grows linearly with redundancy at the Landauer coefficient, and that recording in a basis misaligned beyond a critical angle makes objectivity unreachable at any work.

Wonyong Chung

Schrödinger's Legacy: The Wave Function and Quantum Reality

In briefSchrödinger's Legacy: The Wave Function and Quantum Reality

SummaryThis paper examines how Schrödinger developed wave mechanics and how his work shaped the philosophy of quantum mechanics, with a focus on the metaphysics of the wave function. He interpreted the wave function as electron charge density, an idea later replaced by Born's statistical interpretation but revived in modern realist approaches such as the mass-density and multi-field interpretations. The paper also notes its usefulness in quantum chemistry for computing the shapes of atoms and molecules. It argues that this realist tradition paved the way for ongoing attempts to define a clear ontology of the microscopic world.

Davide Romano, Massimiliano Badino

Rényi multi-entropy from Riemann surfaces

In briefRényi multi-entropy computed from Riemann surfaces

SummaryThis paper computes vacuum tripartite Rényi multi-entropy in a two-dimensional compact-boson conformal field theory. For each integer Rényi index, the partition function is evaluated on the replica Riemann surface for disjoint intervals, giving a twist four-point function written as a ratio of theta functions. The adjacent three-point coefficient is obtained independently from a Fermat curve. The predictions agree with lattice results for critical XXZ and Ising spin chains and a harmonic chain, and the paper also presents a unitary CFT where the canonical replica construction gives complex Rényi multi-entropy.
