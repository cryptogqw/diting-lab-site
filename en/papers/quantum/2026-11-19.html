CAVQS: Context-Aware Variational Quantum Circuit Synthesis via Unitary Redistribution and Dynamic Reconstruction

In briefCAVQS: Context-aware variational quantum circuit synthesis using unitary redistribution and dynamic reconstruction

SummaryThis paper presents CAVQS, a framework for variational quantum circuit synthesis that aims to reduce costly two-qubit gates. It uses QR decomposition to move unitary factors between overlapping blocks, enabling reductions across block boundaries, and builds ancilla-free dynamic implementations for blocks preceding permitted terminal measurements. On random-circuit targets, mean two-qubit gate counts fall by 28.79%-42.52% versus QFAST, 2.05%-52.62% versus SQUANDER, and 30.51%-74.64% versus Fast-USYN. On 4-6 qubit quantum algorithm benchmarks, the reductions are 27.27%-36.77%, 20.65%-69.10%, and 36.31%-71.88%, respectively.
