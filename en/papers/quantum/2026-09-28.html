Distributional Variants of the Aaronson-Ambainis Conjecture

In briefDistributional variants of a quantum complexity conjecture

SummaryThis paper studies a conjecture asking whether quantum query algorithms under uniform random inputs can be simulated by classical ones with only polynomial overhead.
