Dynamics to decision: A mathematical theory of Lyapunov spectra and decision boundaries in deep classifiers

In briefDynamics to decision: a math theory linking Lyapunov spectra to classifier decision boundaries

SummaryThe paper models a deep classifier as a finite, nonautonomous discrete dynamical system, with layers as time steps, and studies the Finite-Time Maximum Lyapunov Exponent (FTMLE) of data trajectories. For Gaussian classes, the authors prove that the probability-level FTMLE aligns with the decision boundary normal, while the link weakens at the logit and hidden-representation levels, depending on the loss function and structural conditions. They propose geometry-aware fine-tuning and conditions for concentrating high hidden FTMLE near the boundary. Numerical results are presented to support the theoretical findings.

In briefMEND: Label-free detection, localisation and correction of hallucinated predictions in latent world models
