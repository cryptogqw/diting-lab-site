From Data to Program: Fast & Direct Generative Program Inference from Empirical Data

In briefFrom Data to Program: Fast, Direct Inference of Generative Programs from Empirical Data

SummaryThe paper introduces PRODiGI, a pretrained model that infers an explicit, executable generative program from a finite set of samples in a single forward pass. The inferred programs support direct sampling, density and score evaluation, and inspection without relying on the pretrained model. The authors also propose program-space fine-tuning, which adjusts differentiable program parameters to match generated and empirical samples while keeping model parameters fixed.

Simon Kl\"uttermann, Xueying Ding, Leman Akoglu

Beyond Teacher Assignment: Domain-Normalized Multi-Teacher On-Policy Distillation

In briefBalancing Multiple AI Teachers When Merging Skills into One Model

SummaryReinforcement learning can produce separate language models that each excel at one skill, such as mathematics or instruction following, but users want a single model with all of them. Multi-teacher on-policy distillation (MOPD) combines these specialists by having each one give feedback to a shared student model on prompts from its domain. The authors find that instruction-following feedback is much more variable than mathematics feedback and dominates training, so the student gains little from the math specialist. They propose Domain-Normalized MOPD (DN-MOPD), which rescales each domain's feedback by its measured spread, and report improved average scores over MOPD on six public benchmarks across three model sizes.

Xin Li, Hao Jiang, Xin Gao et al.
