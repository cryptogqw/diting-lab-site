ReAnchor: Reusing Rejected Verifier States for Diffusion Drafting

In briefReAnchor: Reusing Discarded Verifier States to Speed Up Diffusion-Based Drafting

SummaryThis paper addresses speeding up speculative decoding, where a draft model proposes tokens that a larger target model verifies. Block diffusion drafters produce multiple draft tokens in one pass but only from a single anchor, and the target's verification hidden states beyond the first rejection are usually thrown away. ReAnchor is a training-free method that reuses these discarded states to reanchor a pretrained drafter at shifted positions, producing parallel draft blocks. At temperature T=0, it improves acceptance length by 19.4% and decoding speed by 17.9% on Gemma 4-12B across eight benchmarks.
