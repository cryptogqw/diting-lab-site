Generalized Adaptive Boosting and the Geometry of Mistakes

In briefGeneralized adaptive boosting and the geometry of mistakes

SummaryThis paper extends classical boosting to treat false positives and false negatives separately, introducing a "Z-learner" that guarantees a joint pair of error rates lying in a set Z.
