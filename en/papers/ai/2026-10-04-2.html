Communication Shapes Collective Inference in Self-Adapting LLM Societies: Evidence from Mafia

In briefHow Communication Affects Group Detection of Hidden Adversaries in Self-Adapting LLM Societies

SummaryThe study examines the game Mafia, where an informed minority hides among an uninformed majority, to test whether communication helps groups identify adversaries. Using 7,416 games with societies of 8 to 100 claude-haiku-4-5 agents, it compares communication protocols against silence.
