Few-Shot Visual Concept Extraction for Steering Diffusion Transformers

In briefFew-shot extraction of visual concepts to steer diffusion transformers

SummaryThis paper proposes a training-free method for controlling generated images by extracting visual concept directions directly from the image-side activations of Diffusion Transformers. Using a simple difference-of-means estimator over a few positive and negative prompt pairs, it obtains reusable concept directions that can be injected at inference for continuous steering. Global difference-of-means is used for scene-level concepts such as lighting, weather, and style, while masked difference-of-means handles local concepts such as colors, textures, and facial attributes. The authors report that the method outperforms text-encoder steering and training-free activation-editing baselines, is competitive with specialized editing models, and supports manipulating multiple concepts in one image.
