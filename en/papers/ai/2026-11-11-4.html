In briefUncertainty-aware offline reinforcement learning with Gaussian-process critics for choosing reactor operating actions

SummaryThis paper addresses reactor start-ups and grade changes, which are currently mostly done manually by operators. It builds on the HOFLON hybrid offline-online method and replaces its tree-ensemble critic and adversarial-autoencoder penalty with a sparse variational Gaussian-process critic, selecting actions that maximise the mean minus κ times the standard deviation. Forward-Value Discretization: Faster Stochastic Diffusion Sampling Without High-Order Approximation

In briefForward-Value Discretization: a training-free method for faster stochastic diffusion sampling.

SummaryStochastic diffusion samplers usually need many more neural function evaluations than deterministic ones to reach similar sample quality. The paper shows that even with exact score functions, stochastic samplers with higher-order approximations remain first-order convergent, so higher approximation order does not generally help. It introduces forward-value discretization, which evaluates the model at a lookahead point built from earlier predictions, and derives the stochastic variant F-DDPM. Across CIFAR-10, ImageNet, FFHQ, and LSUN, F-DDPM achieves lower FID than the evaluated stochastic baselines at matched NFE budgets.

WhyThe authors propose forward-value discretization, prove matching bounds that higher-order stochastic samplers stay first-order, and report FID gains on several datasets.
