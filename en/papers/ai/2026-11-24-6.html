In briefWhich ellipses can be the outline of a given ellipsoid? A ratio test when focal length is unknown

SummaryThe paper asks when an ellipse in an image is the outline of an ellipsoid with known semi-axes a≤b≤c. For a camera with square pixels, zero skew and known principal point, an ellipse is an outline at focal length f exactly when a cone ratio computed from two eigenvalues is at most a/c, with the middle axis b playing no role.
