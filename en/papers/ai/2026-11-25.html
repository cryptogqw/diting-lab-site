In briefBackpropagation reframed as Dirac-bracket dynamics for dissipative training

SummaryThe paper treats gradient-based training of a parametric model as a damped particle moving in the potential set by the loss. Using Bateman-Galley doubling of degrees of freedom, it writes gradient flow, the heavy-ball method and Nesterov's accelerated gradient method as Hamiltonian systems.
