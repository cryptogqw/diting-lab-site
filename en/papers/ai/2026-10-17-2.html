Write Once, Check Many: Reusable Ledgers for Post-Hoc Policy Compliance Auditing of Agent Trajectories

In briefWrite Once, Check Many: Reusable Ledgers for Auditing Agent Trajectories Against Policies

SummaryThis paper proposes a framework in which a writer model converts each LLM agent trajectory into a policy-independent ledger of actions and outcomes, and a reader model then checks new policies using only that ledger. SummaryThe paper studies metric distortion in voting, where voters and candidates sit in an unknown metric space and the rule uses only ordinal preferences.
