In briefAccuracy-Information Tradeoff for Loss-Difference Conditional Mutual Information

SummaryThis paper studies loss-difference conditional mutual information (ld-CMI), which measures how much a learner's loss differences reveal about which candidate in each pair it was trained on. The authors show that, for linear predictors with smooth convex losses and suitable regularizers on specified distributions, any proper learner achieving small excess risk at the optimal sample size must have ld-CMI of order n bits. As a result, range-scaled ld-CMI bounds cannot vanish there, even though these learners' generalization gap is O(n^{-1/2}).
