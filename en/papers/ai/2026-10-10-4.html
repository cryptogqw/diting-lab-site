ULPack: Reference-Conditioned Lossless Compression of Checkpoint Trajectories

In briefULPack: Lossless compression of model checkpoint sequences using a reference weight

SummaryThis paper addresses the high storage cost of saving many model checkpoints during training, which limits how finely training can be studied over time. It proposes ULPack, a lossless codec that conditions entropy models for integer residuals on the reference weight's exponent, which predicts the displacement bit length without extra side information. Across bfloat16, float16, and float32 trajectories, ULPack reduces storage by up to 27% versus ZipNN, 21% versus zstd-19, and 11.5% versus FM-Delta.
