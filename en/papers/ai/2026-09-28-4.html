PDE-JEPA: Predictive Representation Learning of Latent Dynamics Modeling for Parametric PDEs

In briefPDE-JEPA: Predictive learning of latent dynamics for parametric PDEs

SummaryThis paper studies predictive representation pretraining as an alternative to reconstruction-based learning for parametric partial differential equations (PDEs). It introduces PDE-JEPA, which trains an encoder with masked-latent prediction, aligns latent trajectory geometry with physical field evolution via a geometry projector, and uses a physics-structured predictor that separates parameter-independent evolution from parameter-dependent response.
