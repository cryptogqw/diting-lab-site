In briefLearning Source Structure: Bayesian Prediction with Transformers

SummaryThis paper studies transformers trained on sequences whose source parameters are redrawn each time, showing they can learn only what the sequences share: the source family and its prior. In briefCollusion-free teaching dimension for planar graph neighborhoods

SummaryThis paper studies non-clashing teaching dimension (NCTD), a measure of how many examples a teacher needs under a collusion-avoidance rule, and compares it with VC dimension, an open question in learning theory. For closed neighborhoods in planar graphs, the authors prove a tight upper bound of two on NCTD, supporting the conjectured inequality for this class. For the positive-only variant NCTD+, they give a tight bound of four. They also show that deciding whether NCTD+ or NCTD is at most one is NP-complete.

In briefTwo Ways an Agent Prioritises Bodily Needs: Precision Versus Preference in Active Inference

SummaryThe paper compares two ways an embodied agent can respond to an urgent bodily need in active inference: increasing the precision of information about the need, or increasing preference for outcomes that relieve it.
