VagueBench: Benchmarking the Robustness of Embodied Agents on Ambient Context Illusions

In briefVagueBench: Testing Embodied Agents' Reliance on Surrounding Context

SummaryThis paper identifies the Ambient Context Illusion, where embodied agents fail when non-essential contextual details are removed from instructions while the task meaning stays the same. The authors built VagueBench, a benchmark of 1,303 instruction pairs in the AI2-THOR simulator, organized into a taxonomy of four categories and nine subtypes. Testing six state-of-the-art agents, they found task success rates dropped by a relative 19.65% and empty plan rates rose by a relative 54.10%.
