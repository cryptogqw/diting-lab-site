CTXAgent - Self-Controlled Context Management of LLM Agents

In briefCTXAgent: Language Model Agents That Manage Their Own Context

SummaryThis paper studies letting the model itself manage its context, deciding when to compact history, what to keep, and what to write as a hand-off, instead of compacting at a fixed token threshold. The authors compare seven frontier models with and without these context tools, and compare their harness with three common harnesses on two models. Most strong models manage context well and reduce cost, and with Claude Opus 5.5 the self-managed harness matches Claude Code's task success at 34% and 49% lower cost. The agent, benchmark and all results are released.

Matthias Busch, Marius Tacke, Roland C. Aydin et al.

In briefMigrating ML Projects from GPUs to TPUs with Coding Agents: Successes, Failures, and Costs

Controlled Block Proximal Linearized Method for Nonconvex and Nonsmooth Optimization

In briefControlled block proximal linearized method for nonconvex nonsmooth optimization

SummaryThis paper studies multiblock nonconvex and nonsmooth optimization without assuming bounded iterates or coercivity of the original objective. The authors propose the Controlled Block Proximal Linearized (CBPL) method, which adds a control potential to block updates while keeping the original objective, variables, and constraints. Under explicit control conditions and the KL property, the method is shown to yield bounded iterates, finite length, whole-sequence convergence of the original variables, and convergence of objective values. The approach is applied to graph-regularized nonnegative matrix factorization, CP decomposition, and matrix tri-factorization with l0 sparsity, with experiments demonstrating convergence.
