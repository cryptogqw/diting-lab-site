DynaFlow: Learning Free-Form Multi-Agent Orchestration

In briefDynaFlow: A method for learning free-form multi-agent orchestration

SummaryThis paper introduces DynaFlow, in which a main agent generates task-specific executable workflows that coordinate sub-agents through sequential or parallel execution, information exchange, and verification. Only the main agent is trained, while sub-agents remain fixed. Across six benchmarks covering mathematical reasoning, scientific question answering, and multi-hop retrieval, DynaFlow reaches 88.08% overall accuracy in Forced mode, 10.90 percentage points above the strongest evaluated baseline with the same Qwen3.5-27B backbone.

End-to-End Neural Modeling of EM Response and Design Performance for Free-Form RFIC Passives

In briefNeural modeling of electromagnetic response and design performance for free-form RFIC passives

SummaryThis paper proposes an end-to-end neural framework that predicts the electromagnetic response and downstream design performance of free-form passive matching networks in RFICs. It combines a frequency-conditioned neural simulator with a circuit-conditioned neural solver, and uses a dependence regularizer so the two response components capture complementary information. Trained on a large dataset of layouts built from expert contours and random paths, the 21M-parameter model reaches R² above 0.98 for almost all targets and evaluates orders of magnitude faster than traditional EM simulation.

Task Relevance-Driven Prompt Expansion for Federated Continual Learning

In briefTask-relevance-driven prompt expansion for federated continual learning

SummaryThis paper proposes TRPE, a model for federated continual learning that does not require prior knowledge of new tasks' arrival times or category counts. It uses an out-of-distribution detector on local clients and a radius-adaptive DBSCAN on the proxy server to identify new tasks and their categories. Task relevance is measured by the affinity between gradients, and prompts from highly related old tasks are concatenated with new-task prompts for training, with a soft orthogonality constraint balancing learning and forgetting. Experiments across diverse datasets and backbones are reported to confirm its efficacy.

Jiahua Dong, Wenqi Liang, Yang Cong et al.

In briefFrom Explaining to Enabling: Actionable Transparency in Algorithmic Insurance Pricing

SummaryThis paper examines how to explain to policyholders the premiums set by machine learning models. The authors define three requirements for explanations (validity, actionability, and reliability), formalize them as axioms, and propose a method that uses a Conformal Prediction constraint to screen out recommendations the model is uncertain about. Applying four Counterfactual Explanation techniques to 57,832 Florida flood-insurance claims and 98,058 synthetic telematics policies, the authors find that constrained recommendations for high-risk policyholders are more reliable and linked to larger predicted risk reductions.

In briefControlled block proximal linearized method for nonconvex nonsmooth optimization

SummaryThis paper studies multiblock nonconvex and nonsmooth optimization without assuming bounded iterates or coercivity of the original objective. The authors propose the Controlled Block Proximal Linearized (CBPL) method, which adds a control potential to block updates while keeping the original objective, variables, and constraints. Under explicit control conditions and the KL property, the method is shown to yield bounded iterates, finite length, whole-sequence convergence of the original variables, and convergence of objective values.
