RLSplat: Resource-Linear Budget Control for Feed-forward 3D Gaussian Splatting

In briefRLSplat: Resource-linear budget control for fast 3D scene reconstruction

SummaryFeed-forward 3D Gaussian Splatting builds 3D scenes from images in one forward pass. Earlier budget-controllable methods predict many Gaussians and then prune them, so inference cost stays nearly constant regardless of budget.
