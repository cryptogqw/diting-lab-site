Do Neural Models Prefer Their Submodels?

In briefDo neural models favor their submodels? A geometric study of whether loss minima lie in submodels.

SummaryThe paper studies the geometry of neural models, the sets of functions expressible by a given architecture, and asks whether local minima of the loss lie within their submodels, such as those with inactive neurons. It introduces a geometric framework and analyzes shallow and deep multilayer perceptrons as well as lightning self-attention. The authors find that, apart from a few exceptions, preference for submodels cannot be explained by model geometry and must arise from other sources such as training dynamics.

WhyGeometric framework proves shallow and some deep MLPs avoid submodels; novel and rigorous, with limited practical impact.
