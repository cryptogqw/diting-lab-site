DemoFoundry: Toward Scalable Photorealistic Robot Data with Coding Agents and Generative Rendering

In briefDemoFoundry: Automatically Generating Realistic Robot Training Demonstrations

SummaryDemoFoundry is an automated framework that uses coding agents and generative rendering to create photorealistic, physically grounded robot demonstrations. Agents build paired real and proxy videos to train a video renderer, and write control programs that run in a physics simulator to produce proxy videos with aligned robot states and actions. A renderer then converts these proxies into scene-consistent realistic observations, and automated checks keep only successful trajectories. Experiments report better visual quality and action-video consistency, and policies trained on the generated demonstrations outperform raw-simulation training and approach the performance of training on an equal number of real demonstrations.
