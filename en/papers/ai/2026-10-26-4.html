Beyond Deduplication: Sharp Retrieval Limits for Attention with Correlated Distractors

In briefBeyond Deduplication: Exact Retrieval Limits for Attention Under Correlated Distractors

SummaryThe paper studies how redundant context affects attention, separating the effect of repeated items, which add normalization mass, from score variation within correlated groups, which can create extreme individual scores. Using a planted retrieval model with Gaussian distractors arranged in large known clusters, the authors derive exact retrieval thresholds. They show that subtracting log cluster size from pre-softmax scores removes multiplicity but keeps within-cluster extremes, so the required signal stays at least √(2α₁); averaging scores before exponentiation achieves √(2ρα₁). A controlled experiment with frozen sentence embeddings shows averaging helps for singleton targets but can discard evidence when relevant groups contain multiple matches.
