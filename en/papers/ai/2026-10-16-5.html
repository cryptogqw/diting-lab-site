In briefSoftCache: Adaptive Step-Size Caching for Faster Diffusion Transformers

SummaryDiffusion and flow models create images or videos through many iterative steps, each requiring a full pass through a large network, which is costly. SoftCache is a training-free method that replaces the usual yes-or-no decision of whether to recompute with a graded one, using how far a caching signal stays below its threshold to set how far each cached step advances.
