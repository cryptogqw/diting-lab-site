In briefThinkWorld: Using World Imagination to Teach Robots Action Reasoning from Human Videos

SummaryThis paper introduces ThinkWorld, a learning framework for Vision-Language-Action robot models. It represents action reasoning as a Chain-of-Dynamics, a sequence of discrete motion tokens linking the current world state to a future state, and reinforces those chains whose predicted futures better match observed outcomes. This lets videos without action labels supply supervision from observed state transitions, so human and robot experience refine the same reasoning space. Experiments in simulation and on real robots show effective human-to-robot transfer and generalization to unseen tasks and environments.

Chaehyun Kim, Chi-Pin Huang, Jisoo Kim et al.
