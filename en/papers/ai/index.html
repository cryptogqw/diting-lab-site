In briefA framework for adding context to AI social agents while keeping their behavior stable

SummaryResearchers use large language models as agents in social simulations, but these agents often change behavior when prompts are reworded without changing their meaning. The paper introduces controlled context enrichment, which systematically adds neutral padding, generic social background, descriptive decision grounding, or normative decision grounding to prompts. It also proposes selective robustness as a validation criterion: insensitivity to meaning-preserving prompt changes combined with a directional response to decision-relevant social changes. In briefFlowReconBench: Benchmark Comparing Learned and Classical Methods for Rebuilding Flow Fields from Sparse Sensors
