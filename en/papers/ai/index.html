Posterior Matching Distillation

In briefPosterior Matching Distillation: faster diffusion generation that keeps sample diversity

SummaryThis paper proposes Posterior Matching Distillation (PMD), a method that compresses a pretrained diffusion teacher's iterative sampling into a few network evaluations. PMD matches exponentially reweighted averages of student samples to teacher posterior means at fixed noisy states, which recovers the teacher's full clean-image posterior at each trained noise level and supports any-step sampling within the trained timestep grid. Experiments on SDXL and FLUX.2 [klein] 4B report that PMD achieves the best FID among 4-step methods on both models, with competitive or leading prompt-alignment and perceptual-quality scores.

Hidir Yesiltepe, Julius Berner, Arash Vahdat et al.

In briefRL Post-Training in Diffusion Transformers Is Activation Steering

SummaryThis paper examines where the gains from reinforcement learning post-training of diffusion transformers come from. The authors add a fixed set of input-independent steering vectors to the block outputs of a frozen base model, with 65 times fewer parameters than the RL-tuned LoRA on SD3.5-M.
