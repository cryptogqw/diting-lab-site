In briefPropose-Filter-Release: a private filtering method that scales noise to typical record sensitivity

SummaryThis paper introduces propose-filter-release (PFR), a general differential privacy primitive. It privately filters out a few records that fit poorly with the rest of the data, so noise is scaled to the influence of typical records rather than worst-case ones. PFR needs only a concave friendliness score and a friendly-DP release mechanism, and it guarantees standard differential privacy under public conditions on the number of records. The authors illustrate it on scalar statistics, principal subspace estimation, KL divergence of user histograms, effective resistances of graphs, and treatment-rule selection.

Moshe Shenfeld, Eliad Tsfadia, Katrina Ligett

In briefAdaptive scenario generation for KL distributionally robust optimization around a learned density model

SummaryThe paper studies distributionally robust optimization for two-stage stochastic programs when the estimated distribution is subject to error from a finite data record.
