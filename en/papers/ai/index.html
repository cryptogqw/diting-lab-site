Strategyproof Deep Reinforcement Learning for Online Resource Allocation

In briefStrategyproof deep reinforcement learning for allocating online resources

SummaryThis paper addresses two linked problems in online resource allocation: distributing scarce resources efficiently under uncertain future demand, and ensuring agents report task values and requirements truthfully. The authors introduce the first strategyproof deep reinforcement learning mechanism for this setting, combining a monotonic neural network, a generalised critical payment, and training by either RL or imitation of an existing rule. In experiments on a four-resource stream, the neural rule achieves 97.8% of an offline welfare upper bound, and imitation reaches 97.5% in 261 seconds, about 89 times faster than RL.

Regression Functions as Fields on Covariate Space: Evolution Equations, the Hessian, and Identification

In briefRegression functions as fields over covariates: evolution equations and identification

SummaryThe paper treats the conditional mean of an outcome given covariates at each time as a field over covariate space and studies how it evolves when covariates and outcomes change over time. It shows this field satisfies a second-order evolution equation whose diffusion term is the trace of the regression Hessian weighted by the covariate covariance. The authors also connect the Hessian to quadratic regression and kernel smoother bias, and show that for binary outcomes the equation reduces to a Nagumo-type equation with logistic travelling fronts.
