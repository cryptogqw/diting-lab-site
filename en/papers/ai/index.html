In briefFFMT: Frequency-Factorized Multi-Rate Tokenization for Generating Dance from Music

In briefWhen Preferences Clash with Scores: Diagnosing and Correcting Constraint Drift in DPO

SummaryThis paper studies Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) when pairwise preferences disagree with pointwise criteria such as length, correctness, or safety scores. The authors show that conflict pairs, where the preferred output scores worse, tend to increase constraint cost, while matching pairs tend to repair it, so the net effect depends on their balance. They introduce a closed-form pre-alignment diagnostic, ρ*_eq, computed from dataset statistics, and propose Conflict-aware DPO (CoDPO), which down-weights conflict pairs and adds an adaptive margin shift. Across four domains, CoDPO reduces drift while largely preserving vanilla DPO's preference learning.
