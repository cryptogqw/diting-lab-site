In briefBeyond Expected Values: Distributional Tree Search for Risk-Sensitive Planning

SummaryThe paper notes that standard Monte-Carlo Tree Search keeps only mean estimates, which limits its use for tail-risk goals such as conditional value-at-risk (CVaR). It introduces two distributional variants, CATSO and PATSO, which store finite-support empirical return distributions on action edges and select actions by CVaR.
