Mesh2Shell Neural Operator for Inverse Shape Optimization

In briefMesh2Shell Neural Operator for Inverse Shape Optimization

SummaryThis paper introduces the Mesh-to-Shell Neural Operator (M2S-NO), a component of a category-agnostic framework for inverse shape optimization. It separates the explicit mesh used for design from the volumetric representation used for physics prediction, mapping surface samples to a shell representation that a separate PDE neural operator uses to predict physical fields. Objective gradients update mesh vertices directly, without a latent shape decoder. Evaluated on geometric matching, jet-engine brackets, and neutron moderators, the method reduces maximum bracket displacement by 14.3% and increases neutron-sensor current by 13.8% relative to the best training designs.
