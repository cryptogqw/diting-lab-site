In briefMuon optimizer on convolutions: theoretically misaligned yet empirically effective

SummaryMuon is an efficient optimizer with a clear theory for matrix updates, but convolutional kernels are four-dimensional tensors that standard implementations reshape into matrices, breaking that theory. The authors introduce Convolutional Newton-Schulz (Conv-NS), which approximates the polar factor in convolutional operator geometry while preserving kernel support. On CIFAR-10 and ImageNet classification, Conv-NS and reshape-based Muon are similarly efficient and achieve comparable accuracy. Despite better theoretical alignment, Conv-NS does not outperform, and the authors hypothesize that exact convolutional orthogonalization may overconstrain updates.
