BAM: Bivariate Additive Models for Interpretable Tabular Prediction

In briefBAM: Bivariate Additive Models for Readable Tabular Prediction

SummaryThis paper proposes Bivariate Additive Models (BAMs), a type of GA²M in which each pairwise shape function is limited to a simple parametric form, an asymmetric bivariate Gaussian. Each component can be shown as a single-peaked contour map, and its feature pair is chosen automatically during training. Experiments on six tabular benchmarks show that BAM's predictive accuracy is comparable to standard GAMs and close to GA²Ms. The paper also examines the trade-off between accuracy and the number of components a user must inspect.

In briefSplit-KV: Efficient Context Parallelism for Training Diffusion Drafters
