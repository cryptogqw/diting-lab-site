SEPAL: Separated Expert Pairs with Answer-Level Fusion for Reliable LLM Collaboration

In briefSEPAL: A Method That Keeps Separate AI Teams Apart Until Their Final Answers Are Voted On

SummaryThis paper introduces SEPAL, a method for making collaboration among large language models more reliable when answering questions. It assigns three private Actor-Critic teams to direct reasoning, evidence grounding, and verification, with each team revising answers internally. Only the final answers are combined by majority voting. Across five open-weight backbones and five question-answering benchmarks, the authors report a 1.81 percentage point average accuracy gain over a matched single Actor-Critic pair.

RiboUnmix: Learning Shared Translational Dynamics from Biased and Noisy Ribo-seq Measurements

In briefRiboUnmix: Separating Shared Ribosome Translation Patterns from Experimental Bias in Ribo-seq Data

SummaryRibosome profiling (Ribo-seq) measures where ribosomes sit along mRNAs, but the measurements include experiment-specific distortions and random noise. The authors propose RiboUnmix, a probabilistic framework that models each measured profile as a shared sequence-dependent signal scaled by a dataset-specific factor, with a negative-binomial model for replicate variability. On a controlled synthetic benchmark where the true components are known, both recovered components correlate strongly with their targets. RiboUnmix also outperforms sequence-to-profile baselines on four organism-specific real datasets, and shared profiles learned from subsets of 114 HEK-derived datasets remain concordant.

Marginal Response Surface Elicitation for Zero-Label Tabular Learning

In briefMARS: Zero-label tabular learning via LLM-elicited feature response surfaces

SummaryThis paper proposes Marginal Response Surface Elicitation (MARS), a method that uses large language models to supply domain priors from task descriptions and feature semantics, building a reusable zero-shot tabular classifier without labeled data. MARS selects representative feature values from unlabeled data, obtains class support scores and feature weights from the LLM, aggregates responses by median, and predicts via weighted sums without further queries. Across eight tabular benchmarks, MARS achieves the highest average AUC and AP, outperforming direct prompting by 1.97 and 6.21 percentage points while substantially reducing end-to-end costs.

SummaryThis paper examines the standard information-theoretic method that splits machine learning uncertainty into aleatoric (AU) and epistemic (EU) components.
