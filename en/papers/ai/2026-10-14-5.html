Why don't Graph Transformers attend to the same token?

In briefWhy graph transformers do not all attend to the same node

SummaryLarge language models often concentrate attention on the first token, a pattern called an attention sink. This paper finds that the graph transformer GRIT rarely forms such sinks on molecular graphs, and forms fewer when trained on larger graphs. The authors show the main cause is the attention scoring function: replacing GRIT's gated logit with a dot product raises the sink rate from 0.09 to 0.68 on identical graphs. They conclude that attention sinks are a property of dot-product attention rather than of Transformers in general.
