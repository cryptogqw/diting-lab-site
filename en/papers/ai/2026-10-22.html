Convex Equilibrium Potentials for Monge--Amp\`ere: Curvature Bounds and A Posteriori Evaluation

In briefConvex equilibrium potentials for Monge–Ampère equations: curvature bounds and after-the-fact evaluation

SummaryThe paper studies equilibrium neural networks, whose hidden state is the fixed point of a contraction, for solving the fully nonlinear Monge–Ampère equation. It shows that the Hessian of nonnegative softplus equilibrium networks grows at most like (1-q)^{-2}, a sharp exponent confirmed by a one-neuron example, and derives a sharp (1-q)^{-3} bound for third derivatives. Combining residual-based a posteriori estimates with interval arithmetic gives rigorous uniform bounds on the residual and solution error of a trained network. On two manufactured Dirichlet problems on the unit disk, all six trained models were certified.
