Dancing Diffusion: Learning Continuous Programs for Image Generation and Editing

In briefDancing Diffusion: learning continuous programs to generate and edit images

SummaryThis paper introduces Dancing Diffusion, which gives each span of denoising timesteps its own continuous condition block, turning conditioning into a latent program that a fixed pretrained generator executes. Executed prefixes can be reused while later operations are rewritten, preserving earlier content. On SANA, reusing a prefix kept more of the scene than regenerating from noise, and the learned program improved generation quality over native SANA. The authors note that later branches weaken the edit, so semantic control over individual operations remains limited.

Beyond the Evidence Boundary: Behavioral Mirage Under Progressive Degradation in Medical VLMs

In briefBeyond the Evidence Boundary: Behavioral Mirage in Medical VLMs

SummaryThis paper studies how medical vision-language models behave when image evidence is progressively degraded, a condition not captured by standard clean-input evaluation. The authors trace baseline-correct cases through increasing degradation to find an empirical evidence boundary, and define a mirage as continued commitment to an answer at that boundary regardless of correctness. Across three models and three medical datasets, post-boundary behavior varied widely, and residual-stream probes decoded commit-versus-abstain behavior well, while output entropy and abstention did not consistently reflect the persistent commitment.

In briefNoise sensitivity of deep neural networks with biases: annealed correlation and quenched sensitivity

SummaryThis paper studies how fully connected deep neural networks with added independent Gaussian biases respond to input noise. Earlier work showed noise sensitivity for networks without biases and sign activations. The authors show that quenched noise sensitivity persists over a broad range of depths under a positive-correlation condition and the weaker assumption that the perturbed input is asymptotically unlikely to equal the original. The annealed covariance stays bounded away from zero and comes entirely from fluctuations in network-dependent output means.
