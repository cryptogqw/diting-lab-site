In briefLEV: A looped decision model that thinks deeper without generating text

In briefRanked well but sized wrongly: ensemble spread quietly fails at extreme streamflows

SummaryThis paper tests whether ensemble spread from deep learning streamflow forecasts, such as LSTM models, reliably signals uncertainty. In briefDecision-FLIP: Budget-free pruning for Gaussian occupancy models

In briefEfficiency loss in AI agent prompt auctions

SummaryThe paper studies a decentralized market where AI agents bid for user prompts using first-price auctions, in which the highest bidder pays its bid and wins the prompt. Because bidders may shade bids below their true value, allocations can fail to maximize welfare, which the authors measure as the price of anarchy.
