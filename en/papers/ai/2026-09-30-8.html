Decode-Latency Feedback Prefill: A Model-Free Controller and Its Generalization Limits

In briefDecode-Latency Feedback Prefill: A Model-Free Controller and Its Limits

SummaryThe paper proposes DLFP, a model-free controller that resizes prefill chunks overlapping active decodes, using observed decode latency as feedback, to reduce interference in concurrent LLM inference. On Qwen3-0.6B on one A100, P99 inter-token latency fell about 27.7% on average, while mean P99 time to first token rose 34.8%. The gains did not generalize to Qwen3-8B, Qwen3-32B, or a two-GPU tensor-parallel setup, which the authors attribute to scheduler-call intervals being a poor proxy for completed GPU iteration time.

Fine-Tuning Diffusion Language Models with Context Selection and Target Weighting

In briefFine-tuning diffusion language models by choosing which tokens to reveal and which to predict

SummaryThis paper studies supervised fine-tuning of discrete diffusion language models, where masking determines both visible context and prediction targets. The authors propose GoldiMask, which selects revealed context tokens by approximately maximizing a submodular objective and weights the remaining targets by their benefit from that context and learning potential. Across three backbones and three datasets, it achieves the highest average accuracy in most settings, with gains on reasoning and code generation. Ablations show both components contribute, and it reduces decoding iterations on GSM8K and MATH-500 under confidence-threshold parallel decoding.

In briefCan an AI Agent Rediscover a Blaschke-Curve Invariant?

SummaryThis paper studies whether an AI agent can rediscover a mathematical invariant of generalized Blaschke curves for one fixed degree-four Blaschke product, without being told the target theorem. In briefMulti-agent LLM discussion helps less when agents withhold dissent

SummaryThis paper examines conflicting reports on whether discussion among LLM agents improves accuracy or produces incorrect consensus.
