Fixing a Model That Learned Worse Cancer Means Lower Risk: Monotonic Constraints in Bladder Cancer Recurrence Prediction

In briefFixing a Model That Learned Reversed Cancer Risk: Monotonic Constraints for Bladder Cancer Recurrence

SummaryUsing data from 435 patients in a UK multicentre bladder cancer trial, the authors found that an unconstrained XGBoost model learned that higher tumour stage and carcinoma in situ predicted lower recurrence, contrary to clinical expectation. SummaryThis paper presents Lang3DSeg, which uses a point transformer, trained from scratch without geometric pre-training, for annotation-free open-vocabulary semantic segmentation of outdoor LiDAR point clouds. In briefPhysics-based evaluation of AI models for Earth science, beyond forecast accuracy

SummaryAI foundation models pretrained on weather and climate data are increasingly fine-tuned for Earth science tasks beyond weather forecasting, faster than the community can evaluate them.
