GPA-VGGT: Geometry-Preserving Acceleration of VGGT

In briefGPA-VGGT: A Training-Free Way to Speed Up VGGT While Keeping Geometric Accuracy

SummaryVGGT is a feed-forward model that recovers camera parameters, depth maps, and point maps from one to hundreds of views in a single pass. Its global attention cost grows quadratically with the number of frames, which makes long sequences slow.
