In briefKhyontekAI's CheckThat! 2026 Study on Multilingual Retrieval of Scientific Sources for Web Claims

SummaryThis paper addresses CLEF 2026 CheckThat! Task 1, which aims to automatically find the scientific publications behind claims shared on social media that mention research without linking to it. In briefGuarded block transfers for SVM training: linear convergence with explicit constants and gap-certified screening

SummaryThe paper addresses hard-margin SVM training, which is framed as finding the distance between two convex hulls. It proposes guarded block transfers that combine several MDM transfers into one exact line search, falling back to an away step when needed. The authors prove global linear convergence with explicit constants on the Minkowski-difference polytope and bound the iterations needed to screen out zero-weight points. Experiments show matching accuracy with baselines, but the solver is slower than LIBSVM or LIBLINEAR in several settings, so its main advantage is the certified duality gap.

Inference on the Value of Policy Improvement in Adaptive Experiments

In briefEstimating the Value of Policy Improvement in Adaptive Experiments

SummaryThis paper proposes an asymptotically normal estimator for the value of policy improvement (VPI), the gap between the best treatment policy in a class and a baseline policy, under adaptively collected data. The estimator sequentially fits a weighted empirical welfare maximizing policy on historical data and evaluates it with inverse propensity weighting on future samples. The authors test coverage on synthetic and semi-synthetic data and apply it to an adaptive experiment on Jordanian job assistance programs. Their estimates are more conservative than the original hierarchical Bayesian analysis, yet still support benefits of personalization among Syrian refugees.

SummaryLinear attention and recurrent models store past context in a fixed-size state that changes at every step, and keeping this state in high precision is costly at large batch sizes.
