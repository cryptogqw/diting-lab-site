Atoms, Not Knowledge: What Production-Scale Addressed-Memory Writes Deposit

In briefAtoms, Not Knowledge: What Large-Scale Addressed-Memory Writes Store

SummaryThis paper tests whether deterministic hash-addressed conditional memory can inject knowledge after training, using models from 124M to 7B parameters and a large FP8 model.
