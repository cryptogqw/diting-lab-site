SummaryThis paper studies weight perturbation (WP), a learning method that estimates update directions from random parameter perturbations and the resulting scalar loss change, without backpropagating derivatives. In briefThe Self-Improvement Problem: Can Automated Gates Reliably Judge Real Agent Improvements?

SummaryThis paper examines whether automated gates, such as reward models or automated reviewers, can reliably decide if a proposed update to a self-improving agent is a genuine improvement. The authors model agents as programs and show that in the fully general setting, no terminating procedure can both never accept a non-improvement and never miss an available one. They identify a bounded, decidable regime with exhaustive simulation that recovers these guarantees, but show that a chain of accepted updates can still lower the true score.

SummaryThis paper proposes a training-free method to refine an already-distilled 3D Gaussian language map.
