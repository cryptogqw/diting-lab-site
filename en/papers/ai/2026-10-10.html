In briefCounterfactual rollouts for offline contrastive goal-conditioned reinforcement learning under confounding

SummaryContrastive reinforcement learning assumes no hidden variable jointly affects recorded actions and their outcomes, an assumption that fails when demonstrations come from a demonstrator with information the learner cannot see.
