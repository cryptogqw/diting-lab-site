In briefLearning when to trust and re-observe sparse-frame video QA

SummaryThe paper shows that sparse-frame video-language models can change their answers when only the sampling timestamps shift. In briefMaking Tabular AI Explanations Easier to Understand by選ぶ Features That People Find Clear

SummaryThis paper argues that human-centred explainable AI research usually evaluates explanations only after they are generated, offering little guidance on improving accessibility.
