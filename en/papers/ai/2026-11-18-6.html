Variance Restoration over Covariance Rank: Memory-Budgeted Analytic Continual Learning

In briefRestoring Variance Instead of Full Covariance Rank: Memory-Budgeted Analytic Continual Learning

SummaryThis paper studies the trade-off between memory and accuracy in analytic continual learning under a fixed memory budget. The proposed method keeps exact diagonal variances and class statistics, approximates off-diagonal covariances with a streaming rank-q factor, and solves the normal equations via the Woodbury identity. Across five class-incremental benchmarks, it nearly matches dense covariance readouts while cutting persistent state by 90.7%, and favors wider feature codes under equal memory.

In briefAdjusting diffusion model reinforcement learning so that stabilizing anchors do not distort positive feedback targets
