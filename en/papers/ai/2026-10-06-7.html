MoonGS: High-quality Representation of the Lunar Surface via Gaussian Splatting Using Robust Depth Features from Image Pairs

In briefMoonGS: High-Quality Lunar Surface Representation via Gaussian Splatting from Image Pairs

SummaryThis paper addresses the difficulty of reconstructing high-quality 3D lunar terrain from sparse rover images, which have limited overlap and weak textures. It proposes MoonGS, the first feed-forward 3D Gaussian Splatting framework designed for lunar scenes, which predicts Gaussian primitives from only two input images in a single pass without per-scene optimization. The method outperforms state-of-the-art feed-forward baselines on the LuSNAR and synthetic MoonBlender benchmarks and shows the best visual quality on Chang'e mission imagery.

Yun Jiang, Bo Zheng, Yingying Zhang et al.

Signature-Based Feature Learning for Human Activity Recognition: A Reproducible Machine Learning Study of Representation, Depth, and Model Choice

In briefSignature Features for Human Activity Recognition: A Reproducible Study

SummaryThis paper examines whether signature transforms, which encode temporal order and cross-channel interactions, improve human activity recognition from sensor data. Using the UCI HAR dataset with a leakage-aware, reproducible pipeline, the authors compare raw signals, time-augmented paths, and lead-lag paths across truncation depths and classifiers. With Random Forest, time-augmented six-channel signatures at depth 6 reached 0.858 accuracy, beating the strongest raw baseline at 0.816. MLP models did not exceed the raw baselines.

Kamal Jarrar (LMAP), Jacky Cresson (LMAP), Christian Paroissin (LMAP)

JudgeMoE: Distributional Aggregation for LLM-as-a-Judge

In briefJudgeMoE: Distribution-Based Aggregation for LLM Judges

SummaryThis paper introduces JudgeMoE, a lightweight aggregator that assigns example-specific weights to cached score distributions from LLM judges and fuses them before computing a final score, preserving uncertainty and disagreement information. On the original 10-cell benchmark, it improves mean Spearman correlation over uniform log pooling by +0.079. Extended to 16 cells, it gains +0.0393 over the strongest local single judge, with positive differences in 12 of 16 cells and a one-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank p-value of 0.0091. The authors also find that the preferred aggregation method depends on the task and judge pool.

Yiqi Liu, Joseph James, Yang Wang et al.

Empirical Variational Autoencoder

In briefEmpirical Variational Autoencoder: a generative framework for continuous-valued sequences

SummaryThis paper proposes Empirical Variational Autoencoder (EVA), a general generative framework for continuous-valued, non-vector-quantized sequences. EVA builds on the VAE evidence lower bound but learns autoregressive latent priors empirically from training data, requiring only one additional linear layer on top of a VAE. Replacing the standard Gaussian constraint with self-predicted priors reduces the gap between prior and posterior latent distributions. Experiments on image and sound synthesis report generation quality competitive with autoregressive diffusion baselines, with much faster inference.

Kaede Shiohara

WaveGSSM: Graph Wave State Space Models for Propagating Spatio-Temporal Patterns

In briefWaveGSSM: A graph wave model that tracks how spatio-temporal patterns move

SummaryThis paper introduces WaveGSSM, a second-order graph state-space model that keeps two linked states per node: one for the current pattern and one for its rate of change. A graph-wave transition uses these to advance the pattern while capturing spatial propagation and temporal evolution together. The authors evaluate it on four temporal-graph benchmarks and global weather forecasting, reporting the best mean performance on the benchmarks and a 20.2% average reduction in geopotential RMSE for 1- to 5-day forecasts versus a backbone-matched snapshot model.

In briefAnatomy of LLM Sycophancy: What a Flip Rate Hides

SummaryThe paper introduces SycoLens, a modular replay protocol that measures how user pushback and evaluation settings affect answer flip rates in language models. Conditional Flow Matching for Single-Neuron Electrophysiology: Capturing Multimodal Responses Across Stimuli

In briefGenerative AI model reproduces varied neuron voltage responses to the same stimulus

SummaryThis paper introduces a conditional generative model, built with flow matching, that learns the range of voltage responses a single neuron can produce to one input current. Tested on biophysically detailed models of two human cortical interneuron types, the generated responses reproduce electrophysiological feature distributions, spike timing, and excitability, and match experimental recordings from human cortical neurons. The model recovers both firing and non-firing responses near threshold and both low- and high-firing modes near depolarization block, whereas a neural operator baseline suppresses spiking near threshold and blurs those modes.

SOL: Measuring Gaps between Text Distributions by Double Sliced Wasserstein Metrics

In briefSOL: Measuring Text Distribution Gaps with Double Sliced Wasserstein Distance

SummaryThe paper proposes SOL, a distance between text distributions that represents each sequence by the empirical measure of its hidden states from a fixed transformer, then compares these measures with the double sliced Wasserstein distance. The authors prove SOL is a metric when the transformer is injective. Experiments show it detects distributional failures, recovers expected model trends, and gives stable sample-based estimates, and it is used to re-evaluate several models trained on OpenWebText.

Xaurora: Generative Weather Forecasting with Denoising Stochastic Interpolants from a Foundation Model Prior

In briefXaurora: a generative weather forecasting model built from a pretrained deterministic foundation model

SummaryMost atmospheric foundation models for weather forecasting are deterministic, which limits their ability to produce large ensembles for uncertainty and extreme-risk assessment. This paper converts the pretrained Aurora model into a generative ensemble model, Xaurora, using Denoising Stochastic Interpolants and a replay buffer for SDE rollouts. Xaurora is finetuned from the small Aurora version and approaches state-of-the-art global ensemble metrics, competing with the large Aurora. It generates 15-day forecasts in 13 minutes.

In briefNeuroCBIR: A Fast, Accurate Brain MRI Search System for Whole-Brain and Regional Scans

SummaryNeuroCBIR is a framework that searches for similar 3D T1-weighted brain MRI scans, either for the whole brain or for specific regions. FairProp: Fair Node Representation Learning via Differentiable Propagation Layers

In briefFairProp: Fair Node Representation Learning via Differentiable Propagation Layers

SummaryThis paper studies group fairness in graph neural networks for node classification, link prediction, and node regression, bounding the demographic parity gap for any number of sensitive groups. It identifies two sources of bias: differences between group means and within-group covariance of final representations. The authors propose FairProp, which embeds fairness constraints into the APPNP propagation process and provably converges linearly to a unique fair optimum. Experiments on three tasks report strong fairness-utility trade-offs compared with baselines.

Emmanouil Kariotakis, Aritra Konar

AECP: Artifact-Exclusive Communication Protocol for Multi-Agent Code Generation

In briefAECP: A protocol where AI coding agents coordinate only through structured artifacts

SummaryThis paper introduces AECP (Artifact-Exclusive Communication Protocol), which requires multiple AI agents working on code generation to communicate only through structured artifacts. The execution harness supplies relevant findings, checks implementations against recorded interface commitments, and requires affected agents to revisit revised agreements. On Doc2Repo, NL2Repo, and CodeProjectEval, AECP improved average test pass rate by 28.2% and reduced average wall time by 16.5% compared with free-form inter-agent messaging. Behavior-Preserving KV Cache Compression

In briefBehavior-Preserving KV Cache Compression: A Training-Free Method

SummaryKey-value (KV) caches consume large memory during long-context inference in language models. This paper proposes a training-free framework that decides which cache entries to evict by estimating how much removing each one would change the full-cache model's next-token distribution, measured by KL divergence. The authors report that, at matched cache budgets across several architectures, the method improves downstream task quality over attention-based heuristics, with the largest gains under aggressive compression, while still giving an end-to-end speedup over full-cache inference in their tested settings.

Doo Hwan Hwang, Junyoung Jang, Junho Na et al.

In briefOdyssey: A Long-Horizon Closed-Loop Driving Benchmark with Explicit Navigation Routes

In briefLatent Flow Matching for Molecular Graph Generation: generating whole molecular graphs in a compressed latent space

SummaryThe paper generates entire molecular graphs in the latent space of a pretrained Variational Autoencoder with high reconstruction fidelity, using flow matching and decoding only at the final step.
