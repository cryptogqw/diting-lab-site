TC-ADA: One-Shot Active Domain Adaptation for Semantic Segmentation

In briefTC-ADA: One-Shot Active Domain Adaptation for Semantic Segmentation

SummaryThis paper addresses the high cost of dense manual annotation when adapting semantic segmentation models to new driving environments. It proposes TC-ADA, a one-shot active domain adaptation method that selects and fully annotates a fixed set of target images in a single round before uninterrupted adaptation. The selection stage combines vision foundation model features with predictions from a fixed unsupervised domain adaptation model, without target labels, while adaptation jointly uses labeled source, labeled target, and unlabeled target data with calibrated supervision. The authors report consistent gains over representative baselines across five transfers and performance within 1.9 mIoU points of full target supervision using only 23 to 140 labeled target images.

Weihao Yan, Yeqiang Qian, Yueyuan Li et al.

MoGround: Measuring and Mitigating Modality Distraction in Vision-Language Models

In briefMoGround: Measuring and Reducing Modality Distraction in Vision-Language Models

SummaryThe authors release MoGround, a dataset spanning four visual domains where each question's answer is available in exactly one modality. This allows measuring modality distraction, where a model answers correctly from one modality but gets it wrong after irrelevant content from the other is added. Across seven open-source vision-language models, distraction varies by model and scales inversely with grounding strength. A weight-space robustness vector trained on one MoGround split reduces distraction by 9% to 51% across all seven models, costing only 0.1 average points on standard multimodal tasks.

Luca Zhou, Bo Zhao, Rose Yu et al.
