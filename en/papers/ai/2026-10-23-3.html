Head Dimension Matters in Language Models for Long-Context Retrieval

In briefWider attention heads improve long-context retrieval in language models

SummaryThis paper studies whether, at a fixed parameter budget, using fewer but wider attention heads improves retrieval over long contexts. The authors find that pretraining loss and short-context non-retrieval performance are barely affected by head dimension, while long-context retrieval improves with wider heads. Wider heads also contain a higher proportion of retrieval heads, and the gains persist even when attention projection weights are frozen during long-context continual pretraining, suggesting benefits extend to embeddings and MLPs.

In briefSOPO: Socratic-guided policy optimization for detecting hallucinated spans in LLM outputs

SummaryThis paper addresses hallucination in large language models, where generated content is unfaithful or inconsistent, by proposing SOPO, a multi-turn reinforcement learning framework for span-level hallucination detection. SummaryThis paper studies the internal representations of OpenVLA, a vision-language-action robot policy model. Not All Information Is Worth Asking For: Decision-Informed Information Seeking with LLMs

In briefNot All Information Is Worth Asking For: LLM Information Seeking Guided by Decision Value

SummaryThe paper observes that large language models often pay for checks that cannot change their decision or are not worth their cost, and keep asking after evidence is already sufficient. It proposes DIVA, a training-free procedure in which the LLM specifies a small decision model through short numeric questions and values each check by its decision improvement net of cost, stopping when no check is worthwhile. The variant DIVA-U also accounts for the LLM's uncertainty about how strongly each check shifts the probability of a bad outcome. Across four domains and 20 LLMs, DIVA-U achieves lower regret than every LLM baseline on real-world data.
