A Framework for Context Enrichment and Selective Robustness in LLM Social Agents

In briefA framework for adding context to AI social agents while keeping their behavior stable

SummaryResearchers use large language models as agents in social simulations, but these agents often change behavior when prompts are reworded without changing their meaning. The paper introduces controlled context enrichment, which systematically adds neutral padding, generic social background, descriptive decision grounding, or normative decision grounding to prompts. Dimension-Free Private Mean Estimation is Impossible Without the Covariance

In briefPrivate mean estimation without known covariance cannot avoid dimension dependence

SummaryThis paper studies the limits of differentially private Gaussian mean estimation when the covariance matrix is unknown. When the covariance is known, optimal sample complexity depends only on its spectrum rather than the ambient dimension. The authors prove that private estimators cannot achieve similar guarantees when the covariance is unknown. They give lower bounds showing error gaps of order √(d/n) when the sample size is below the effective dimension, and of order √d/n^{3/2} above it, even for diagonal covariances.

WhyThe authors prove dimension-dependent lower bounds for private Gaussian mean estimation with unknown covariance, contrasting the known-covariance case.
