Optimizer-dependent training dynamics converge to the same one-third optimal data scaling

In briefOptimizer-dependent training dynamics still reach the same optimal one-third data scaling

SummaryThe paper studies how neural network loss falls as a power law with dataset size, separating the per-step dynamic exponent of a training run from the optimal data exponent after tuning.
