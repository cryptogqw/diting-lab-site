Reverse-KL Policy Distillation: Statistical Guarantees and Limits

In briefReverse-KL Policy Distillation: Theory and Limits (Paper)

SummaryThis paper studies the statistical theory of training with reverse-KL policy distillation. It compares two teacher feedback types, full action distribution versus only the probability of the sampled action, and analyzes online and offline settings. Online distillation with probability-only feedback may require substantially more data, while offline distillation reaches the same minimax rate under both feedback types. The authors also show that common online objectives can fail and propose trajectory-loss and importance-ratio truncation techniques to achieve optimal guarantees.

Haolin Liu, Chen-Yu Wei, Julian Zimmert

In briefLean Finder V2: Mathlib Search That Resolves Ambiguous Short Queries

SummaryThis paper presents Lean Finder V2, a semantic search framework for Mathlib, the Lean mathematics library, that resolves ambiguous user queries through adversarial training at the model level and query rewriting at the input level. On the human-curated MathlibQR benchmark, it reports at least an 11% relative improvement over the previous best engines, and it ranks highest on unlabeled real queries. Integrated with Claude Code, it also improves proof success on a downstream theorem-proving task.

Semi-Linear Gaussian Bayesian Networks: Enhancing Flexibility in Node Dependencies via penalized B-Splines

In briefSemi-Linear Gaussian Bayesian Networks with Penalized B-Spline Dependencies

SummaryThis paper introduces Semi-Linear Gaussian Bayesian Networks (SLGBN), a class of continuous Bayesian networks that keep Gaussian conditional distributions while allowing nonlinear conditional mean functions.
