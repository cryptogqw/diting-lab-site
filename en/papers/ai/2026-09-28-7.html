In briefGradLev: Parallel Test-Time Training via Costate Prediction

SummaryThe paper introduces GradLev, a method for parallelizing test-time training (TTT), where a model updates its weights after each token during inference. Because sequential gradient updates make parallel training difficult, the authors show that online gradient descent can be computed exactly in parallel given layer inputs and activation gradients (costates). The auxiliary predictor is discarded at deployment, and the model updates token by token as usual.
