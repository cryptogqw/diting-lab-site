In briefSpeculative Sampling: Large bidirectional windows for low-latency interactive video generation

SummaryInteractive video generation usually uses small bidirectional chunks to reduce latency, but larger chunks can improve quality and use GPU parallelism better. The paper proposes Speculative Sampling, which expands the modeling window by appending noise latents as padding and caches predictions for reuse when the condition is unchanged. Predictions are delivered incrementally, while uncommitted future content stays replaceable. The authors' latency analysis indicates this removes the waiting-time lower bound set by chunk boundaries, shifting optimization toward faster per-chunk sampling.
