TALLOP: Joint Optimization of Local Training and Communication Compression for Time-Constrained Federated Learning

In briefTALLOP: Jointly tuning local training and communication compression for federated learning under time limits

SummaryThis paper addresses federated learning, where slow clients delay global synchronization, by proposing TALLOP, which jointly optimizes local training workload and communication compression under a per-round time constraint. It predicts achievable training loss under different computation budgets from early mini-batch loss trajectories (LENS) and estimates compression-induced degradation with a compression-error model (CLEN). Experiments in heterogeneous FL settings show TALLOP generally achieves competitive or improved time-to-accuracy compared with methods that optimize only local training or neither, though the advantage over training-only adaptation varies by setting.

Honoka Anada, Yafei Ou, Shinya Takamaeda-Yamazaki

In briefStudy of the tradeoff between robustness and geometric accuracy in adversarial training

SummaryThe paper studies non-parametric binary classification under an adversary, measuring geometric accuracy by the Hausdorff distance to the Bayes classifier and robustness by the adversarial budget ε. The authors prove that robust classifiers lose at most O(√ε) or O(ε) accuracy, depending on the regularity of the underlying Bayes classifier. In briefMulti-turn jailbreaks need only about half their queries to reach the same progress

SummaryThis paper studies whether multi-turn jailbreak attacks still need their full earlier query history once an effective attack path is found. The authors remove early attacker queries, rerun the remaining ones in a new conversation, and define the effective horizon as the shortest query sequence that recovers the same attack progress.
