FACE: Flow-Guided Angular Channel Estimation Framework for mmWave MIMO Systems

In briefFACE: A flow-based method for estimating millimeter-wave MIMO wireless channels

SummaryThis paper proposes FACE, a framework for estimating channel state information in millimeter-wave MIMO systems using flow matching, a generative modeling approach. It exploits the angular-domain sparsity of mmWave channels and uses the learned flow matching model as a generative channel prior. Numerical results indicate FACE achieves higher estimation accuracy than conventional and diffusion-based estimators across diverse configurations, with fewer pilots and far fewer sampling steps than diffusion methods.

Samitha Gunarathne, Eslam Eldeeb, Nurul Huda Mahmood et al.
