In briefEvoAlloc: A self-evolving agent that allocates limited compute to promising programs during LLM-driven program search

SummaryThis paper proposes EvoAlloc, a resource-allocation agent for LLM-based program evolution that learns from search experience to revise how it assigns evaluation resources to candidate programs. In briefPolicy design for finite agent groups under probabilistic reach-avoid limits on group fractions

SummaryThe paper studies how to design policies for a finite population of Markov decision agents so that, with high probability, a required fraction reaches a target while the unsafe fraction stays below a threshold. It tracks the variance of the empirical density with a discrete-time Lyapunov recursion and uses the Cantelli inequality to turn chance constraints into tractable moment conditions. These conditions feed a gradient-based sequential convex approximation, and additional bounds give a finite-population certificate. The method is tested on a gridworld and an EV-charging aggregation problem and compared with a deterministic population-level linear programming baseline.
