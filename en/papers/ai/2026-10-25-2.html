Constrained Gradient Equilibrium: Approachability under Locally Constrained Adversaries

In briefConstrained Gradient Equilibrium: theory on online learning under locally constrained adversaries

SummaryThis paper studies gradient equilibrium (GEQ), an online learning framework where time-averaged gradients along the learner's trajectory vanish. It shows that constrained GEQ (CGEQ) can be impossible even under simple, slowly varying constraints, so it imposes local temporal structure on the adversary. The authors give necessary and sufficient conditions in the style of Blackwell approachability and an algorithmic method that turns window-wise no-regret learners into CGEQ algorithms. Experiments on synthetic and real data show strong performance in both achieving equilibrium and respecting constraints.

Kaicheng Zhang, Lujing Zhang, Yifei Pang et al.

Hierarchical Cooperative GFlowNets

In briefHierarchical Cooperative GFlowNets: several samplers divide up the search so they cover more high-reward regions

SummaryGFlowNets learn to sample objects with probability proportional to a reward, but weak exploration can make them concentrate on a few high-reward regions. The paper proposes Hierarchical Cooperative GFlowNets (HCG), which uses a shared classifier to dynamically partition the search space so higher-ranked models explore broadly while lower-ranked ones are pushed toward uncovered regions. Experiments on multimodal distributions, sequence generation, combinatorial optimization, and biological sequence design report improved mode coverage and target approximation.
