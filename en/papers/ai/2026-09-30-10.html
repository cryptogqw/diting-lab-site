Counterfactual Probing for Parallel Unmasking with Hidden Forest Structure

In briefCounterfactual probing for safe parallel token unmasking in hidden-forest distributions

SummaryThe paper studies parallel sampling from masked generative models, where token dependencies are unknown and must be discovered at extra model-evaluation cost. It proposes a sampler for discrete distributions with hidden forest structure that shares hypothetical-reveal evaluations across dependence tests to find safe parallel batches without fully recovering the forest. Under stated regularity conditions, the authors show total evaluations and sequential depth are sublinear in sequence length, and that comparable irreversible product-commit samplers require more counterfactual submissions or commit rounds in the worst case.

Ryotaro Kawata, Satoshi Hayakawa, Taiji Suzuki

Can Vision-Language Models Stay Helpful When Facing Implicit Risks? Intent-Privilege OPSD for Efficient Safety-Helpfulness Alignment

In briefCan vision-language models stay helpful when facing hidden risks? A cheaper training method that teaches safe and useful answers

SummaryThe paper introduces Intent-Privilege On-Policy Self-Distillation (OPSD), a training method that uses evidence-grounded intent as extra supervision so vision-language models can spot implicit cross-modal risks and still give safe, useful answers instead of refusing. It uses only 1,447 safety examples, cutting training time by 5x compared with multi-rollout GRPO-style training and reducing average inference length by 7%. On pooled SIUO and HoliSafe evaluations, the joint safety-helpfulness success ratio rises from 43.9% to 53.5%.

Haotian Deng, Wenbin Xing, Gang Xu et al.

Extending common training to 50,000 updates did not remove this difference, even though the accuracy gap was only 0.18 percentage points.

In briefCan a Cacheable Decision Model Follow Rules? A study of a small non-generative decision model

SummaryThe paper studies Certo, a small non-generative decision model built on Qwen3-4B, which scores candidate actions from their text and returns a probability rather than generating an answer.
