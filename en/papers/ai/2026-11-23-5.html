In briefUniDR: Generating Anatomical Deformations from Images with Unified Diffusion and Registration

SummaryThis paper introduces UniDR, a framework that generates arbitrary anatomical deformations from images while preserving topology. It uses a conditional diffusion model over deformation fields that is jointly optimized with the registration process, guided by a registration-based score-matching objective. Learning-Augmented QUBO for Time-Expanded Multi-Commodity Flow

In briefLearning-augmented QUBO model for time-expanded multi-commodity flow

SummaryThis paper studies energy-aware multi-commodity flow on time-expanded graphs, a problem modeling dynamic routing in layered networks that is NP-hard in general. The authors formulate it as a QUBO problem with an explicit penalty coefficient that guarantees feasibility and is smaller than the standard bound. They also present structural results, including a treewidth-based exact algorithm, a small-bond-dimension matrix-product-operator representation, and a positive annealing gap for the associated Ising Hamiltonian. For learning-augmented routing, they prove a consistency guarantee linking the cost gap to the prediction error.

Aikaterini Nikolidaki

In briefEvolutionary AI Control: Keeping Population-Level Safety Intact Through Reproduction and Selection

SummaryThis paper studies open multi-agent systems in which agents copy themselves, modify their successors, and change membership during a run. It shows that every agent can pass its individual safety check while the population as a whole violates a collective safety limit. The authors prove conditions under which safety guarantees persist through reproduction, inheritance, and selection, and give four counterexamples showing how they fail. Loop-Count Schedules in Looped Transformers: An Empirical Study

In briefLoop-count schedules in looped transformers: an empirical study

SummaryThis study examines how the schedule of loop iteration counts R during training determines whether each extra pass in a looped transformer acts as a full layer or a small correction. Models of 46M to 110M parameters were trained on 2B tokens under three schedules: fixed R=2, R sampled from {1,...,6}, and R sampled from {2,3,4}. The lower bound of the sampling range is decisive: including R=1 drops the residual gates to 0.1-0.5, while starting at R=2 keeps them at 0.6-0.9.

Konstantin Kolokolov
