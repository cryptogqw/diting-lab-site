Reinforcing Diffusion Models by Instantaneous Trajectory Distribution Matching

In briefStabilizing Diffusion Model Reinforcement Learning via Instantaneous Trajectory Distribution Matching

SummaryThis paper proposes a method for improving reinforcement learning of diffusion models that use deterministic ODE sampling. The authors argue that existing approaches rely on reward-weighted regression, which can cause unstable training when the behavior distribution differs from the trainable policy, as under classifier-free guidance or offline updates. They introduce Instantaneous Trajectory Distribution Matching (iTDM), which derives a sample-level gradient anchored at the behavior velocity and tracks it with an online auxiliary denoiser to suppress baseline-induced drift. Experiments on compositional generation, text rendering, and preference alignment report state-of-the-art performance, with the method converging nearly three times faster than competing methods on the GenEval2 benchmark.
