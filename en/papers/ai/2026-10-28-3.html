Finite-Step Geometry of Marginal-Preserving Velocity Fields in Generative Flows

In briefFinite-step geometry of velocity fields that preserve marginals in generative flows

SummaryThis paper studies why different velocity fields can produce the same continuous marginal evolution in generative flows yet behave differently after numerical discretization. Keeping the marginal path and numerical method fixed, the authors use projected modified equations to characterize the local integration error that changes the marginal law, and give sufficient conditions for higher marginal convergence order.
