Integral Probability Boundaries

In briefIntegral Probability Boundaries: Tighter Non-Linear Bounds Than Integral Probability Metrics

SummaryThis paper introduces Integral Probability Boundaries (IPBs), a non-linear generalization of Integral Probability Metrics (IPMs). IPMs reduce the gap between two distributions to a single worst-case difference in expectations, which can yield loose or vacuous bounds for rare events, safety failures, or under-represented groups. IPBs are designed to remain tight at these extremes. The authors derive their basic properties, present estimation algorithms, and demonstrate use in alignment, judge-free performance estimation, and fairness, including more precise fairness-gap estimates for rare groups in a generative modeling task than prior methods.
