In briefTesting Whether AI Reasoning Adapts, Beyond Answers and Efficiency

SummaryThe paper argues that current AI evaluations, based on final-answer accuracy, reasoning traces, process supervision, and efficiency, do not show whether reasoning adapts when tasks or evidence change.
