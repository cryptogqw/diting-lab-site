RankBuffer: Efficient Ranking-Based Rewards for Open-Ended Generation

In briefRankBuffer: Efficient Ranking-Based Rewards for Open-Ended Generation

SummaryThis paper introduces RankBuffer, a method for building rewards in reinforcement learning for open-ended text generation, where no single correct answer exists. It keeps an ordered, per-query buffer of previously judged responses, first placing each new response into an anchor interval through a coarse judgment and then ranking only responses within the same interval. The full order is converted into bounded rank rewards. Across four open-ended benchmarks, RankBuffer outperforms all pointwise baselines and nearly matches the strongest ranking-based baseline while substantially reducing judging cost.

Zixuan Yang, Yiqun Chen, Qi Liu et al.
