In briefStatistically optimal, near-linear-time method for choosing the best candidate distribution

SummaryThe paper studies hypothesis selection: given n candidate distributions and samples from an unknown distribution p, it seeks a distribution q whose total variation distance to p is at most α times the best candidate's distance plus ε.
