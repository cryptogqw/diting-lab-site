In briefOrthogonal Boundary Unlearning for Removing Dual-Use Capabilities from LLMs

SummaryThis paper introduces Orthogonal Boundary Unlearning (OBU), a method for removing hazardous capabilities such as cyberwarfare exploitation and pathogen enhancement from large language models while preserving benign scientific ability.
