CAVA: Complementary-Label-Aware Data Valuation

In briefCAVA: A Data Valuation Method for Complementary-Label Learning

SummaryComplementary-label learning trains classifiers using labels that say what an instance is not, which are cheap but can be wrong. The paper shows that directly transferring optimal-transport data valuation to this setting fails because complementary labels have different semantics. WhyAdapts optimal-transport data valuation to complementary-label learning, identifying a semantic mismatch and fixing it with a label margin, backed by analysis and experiments.

In briefCross-Fitting for Neural Posterior Estimation: A Fix for Overconfident Amortized Inference

SummaryNeural Posterior Estimation trains a conditional density estimator on simulated data, but its log-likelihood objective encourages overconfidence on training points, which hurts performance on held-out data. Deep Pontryagin Learning for Continuous-Time Stochastic Control with Jumps

In briefDeep Pontryagin learning for continuous-time stochastic control with jumps

SummaryThis paper proposes a deep Pontryagin learning framework for high-dimensional continuous-time stochastic control problems that include jump-diffusions. It separates adjoint learning from policy improvement: adjoint labels are computed along simulated trajectories and projected onto the Markov state by regression, then the control is updated via Hamiltonian stationarity. The authors prove the projected adjoint satisfies a discrete-time Pontryagin principle and converges to the continuous-time adjoint BSDE solution as the time step vanishes. Numerical experiments show accuracy, stability, and scalability.
