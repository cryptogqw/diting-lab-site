Density-Matrix Reinforcement Learning

In briefDensity-Matrix RL: Adding Action Relations to Policies

SummaryStandard reinforcement learning policies store only one probability per action at each state, leaving out how actions relate to each other. This paper stores each policy as a density matrix, where diagonal entries are the usual action probabilities and off-diagonal coherences are new parameters, a framework called density-matrix reinforcement learning (DMRL). The authors prove that coherences change outcomes in two settings: regularizing toward a coherent reference policy, and rewards that are quadratic functions of a unit-vector action. This is a purely theoretical paper with no empirical evaluation.
