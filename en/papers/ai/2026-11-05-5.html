ToroFit: Fitting Wedge Sums of Tori to High-Dimensional Data

In briefToroFit: Fitting Shapes Made of Joined Tori to High-Dimensional Data

SummaryThis paper presents methods for detecting and parameterizing Euclidean point cloud data that lies near a wedge sum of tori, a shape formed by joining several toroidal components. The approach uses persistent cohomology and cup products to identify the distinct toroidal parts, then optimizes low-energy multivariate trigonometric polynomials to best fit the data on each part. The resulting problem reduces to generalized ridge regression, which can be solved efficiently. The authors demonstrate the framework on synthetic and real datasets, including video interpolation, attractor recovery in quasiperiodic dynamical systems, and parameterizing gene-space cycle data for parasites and humans.

Tanishq Bhatia, Jose A. Perea, Tarek M. Zikry
