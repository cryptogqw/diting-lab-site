LatentIndex: Cross-Layer Sharing with Layer-Specific Selection for Sparse Attention

In briefLatentIndex: cross-layer sharing with layer-specific selection for sparse attention

SummarySparse attention cuts core attention computation, but its indexers still repeat selection work and store keys for each layer. LatentIndex extends Multi-head Latent Attention's latent-sharing idea across indexer layers: each layer group builds one shared latent cache from its first layer, while each layer scores tokens independently. With four-layer sharing, it reduces logical indexer-cache storage by 61.1% on DeepSeek-V3.2 and gives decode indexer speedups of 2.30-2.72 times over DSA in the hierarchical variant.

Zhaohui Wang, Zhixin Pan, Fanxu Meng et al.

DistScene: Object-to-Scene Distillation for 3D Scene Generation

In briefDistScene: Object-to-Scene Distillation for 3D Scene Generation

SummaryDistScene is a framework that generates compositional 3D scenes from a single image by jointly modeling the environment and individual objects. It generates scene and object components in a shared coordinate frame, then refines each object using scene context. It also transfers pretrained object-generation priors to scene generation through automatically composed and rendered synthetic scenes. Evaluations on indoor and outdoor benchmarks report improved scene-level spatial coherence over the compared baselines.

Kunming Luo, Hongyu Yan, Ken Deng et al.

Backward-Consistent Diffusion Sampling for Sparsely Observed PDE Inverse Problems

In briefA new diffusion sampling method that better reconstructs PDE coefficients from sparse data

SummaryThis paper addresses recovering PDE coefficient fields from extremely sparse observations, an ill-posed inverse problem. The authors prove that output-space diffusion methods can fail to minimize unobserved error in discontinuous PDE settings. They propose FunBCS, an input-space optimization approach that seeks inputs whose denoiser reconstructions are physics-consistent, and prove it minimizes unobserved error. Across four PDE inverse problems, including discontinuous Darcy flow, FunBCS reduces reconstruction error by 27-64% and runs 1.4-2.1 times faster than the current state of the art.

Stance Drift: How AI-mediated Communication Distorts Our Message

In briefStance Drift: How AI Tools Can Distort the Positions in Our Messages

SummaryThis paper examines whether large language models keep a speaker's position intact when they draft or summarize text. The authors model the process as a two-step pipeline, where one model writes an argument from a given stance and another extracts the stance back, measured by a stance preservation rate. Across 112 debate propositions, none of nine tested models exceeded 0.7 under default settings. The most common drift patterns were polarization, deviation from neutrality, and flipping.

In briefForeAct3D: Policy-Grounded Future World Modeling for VLA Policies

SummaryThis paper presents ForeAct3D, a framework that trains Vision-Language-Action robot policies to predict future semantic 3D scene states, with future predictions conditioned on the policy's planned action chunk. Physical-consistency constraints based on background staticity, instance-level rigidity, and end-effector kinematics shape the shared representation during training, and no future prediction is needed at inference. The authors report 98.3% average success on LIBERO, an average task length of 3.73 on CALVIN, and real-world average success rising from 6.7% to 37.8% over the base policy.

From Probe Scores to Alarm Policies: Operational Validity of Activation Monitors for Language-Model Agents

In briefFrom Probe Scores to Alarm Policies: Checking Whether Activation Monitors for Language-Model Agents Work in Practice

SummaryThe paper argues that activation probes can predict safety-relevant properties of language models with high AUROC, but deployed monitors make thresholded alarm decisions under tight false-alarm limits, which is a different task. The authors propose an Operational Validity Contract covering target, timing, calibration and cost, and test it on several benchmarks. They find that high AUROC does not guarantee good detection rates at fixed false-alarm levels, and that thresholds can realize false-positive rates different from their targets. When support is insufficient, the policy abstains rather than reporting success.

WhyProposes an operational validity contract separating AUROC from alarm risk, validated across benchmarks with preregistered protocols showing low detection at tight false-alarm budgets.

From Transformers to Weighted Automata: Towards the Verification of Large Language Models

In briefFrom Transformers to Weighted Automata: A Formal Bridge for Verifying Large Language Models

SummaryLarge language models are increasingly used in safety-critical settings, but their black-box nature makes formal guarantees about their behavior difficult. This paper links transformers to weighted automata, a classical model from formal language theory, so that verification tools from automata theory can be applied to LLMs. It establishes a formal correspondence between transformer architectures and weighted automata over the reals, and introduces an identity testing algorithm that statistically decides whether two stochastic models define the same distribution up to a tolerance threshold.

Gradient-Free Sampling from Generative Models via Stochastic Bounded Extremum Seeking

In briefGradient-Free Sampling: Drawing Samples from Generative Models via Stochastic Bounded Extremum Seeking

SummaryThis paper proposes a sampling method for energy- and score-based generative models that requires no gradient evaluations of the model. It replaces the score-containing drift term with a high-frequency dithered cosine of the model's value, which in the high-frequency averaging limit yields Langevin MCMC or the reverse-time SDE of diffusion models. The authors prove convergence of the dithered trajectories to the target SDE trajectories and give an O(ω^-1/2) mean-square rate under global bounds. Experiments on CelebA-HQ and CIFAR-10 demonstrate latent-space tracking and latent-space EBM sampling.

Alexander Scheinker
