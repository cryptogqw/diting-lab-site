Shared Geometry Is Not Shared Physics: A Layerwise Test of the Platonic Representation Hypothesis in Astronomy

In briefShared Geometry Is Not Shared Physics: Testing the Platonic Representation Hypothesis by Layer in Astronomy

SummaryThe paper analyzes representations from every layer of 36 pretrained models, using cross-matched optical images, infrared images, and spectra. Representational Control over Self-Report & Behavior Coherence in LLM Risk-Taking

In briefControlling whether LLMs' self-reported risk matches their behavior, by steering internal representations

SummaryThis paper examines whether large language models' self-reports about risk-taking match how they actually behave. Using activation steering, the authors compare nine methods for extracting steering vectors, evaluated on two behavioral tasks and two psychometric instruments across four open-weight models. In briefConvergence and sample complexity of policy mirror descent for average-reward MDPs
