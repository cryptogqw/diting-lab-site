In briefPhysical Muon: Orthogonalization as Equilibrium Computation

SummaryThis paper introduces Physical Muon, which computes the orthogonalization step of the Muon optimizer as the equilibrium of a continuous-time flow instead of using dense Newton–Schulz matrix products.
