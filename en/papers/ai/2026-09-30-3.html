PCB-MC: Missing Component Analysis in Printed Circuit Boards

In briefPCB-MC: Analyzing Missing Components on Circuit Boards

SummaryThis paper introduces PCB-MC, a dataset for detecting components missing from printed circuit boards, built on RF100 with footprint-level annotations across 197 board types. The authors benchmark several supervised and unsupervised methods and propose board-type-aware cross-validation to prevent layout leakage. Supervised models show high false negative rates on unseen board designs, while unsupervised anomaly detection fails without spatial alignment to a board-specific reference. The authors conclude that detecting missing components across diverse layouts remains an open challenge.

Awakening of the Buddha: Subspace Learning During Population-Loss Plateaus

In briefAwakening of the Buddha: Subspace Learning During Population-Loss Plateaus explains how networks learn during flat-loss phases.

SummaryThe paper studies how a neural network can learn a more predictive representation while its population loss stays nearly constant. It gives theoretical conditions for two-layer ReLU and leaky-ReLU networks on Gaussian inputs, plus results for SwiGLU networks and population-moment experiments. The main result is that during a high-loss plateau, alignment between the teacher subspace and the leading AGOP eigenspace increases and refit error decreases, and training later drops below all plateau values.

SummaryThis paper introduces R²NO, a framework for adapting neural operators pretrained on numerical simulations to real-world data while keeping useful pretrained structure.
