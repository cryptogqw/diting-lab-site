Robust-by-Design Distributional Learning from Contaminated Samples

In briefRobust Distribution Learning from Contaminated Data by Design

SummaryThis paper studies learning data distributions when the observed sample mixes a target distribution with an unknown contaminant. The authors propose contamination-corrected maximum mean discrepancy (CC-MMD), which combines noisy proxy membership scores with residual corrections from a small audited subset to recover the oracle MMD. They prove finite-population design-unbiasedness of the corrected kernel sums, asymptotic normality of the audit correction, and consistency for the target discrepancy. Experiments show CC-MMD tracks the oracle as contamination increases, and generative models trained on heavily corrupted mixtures can recover the clean target distribution.
