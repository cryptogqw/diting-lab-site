Event Cameras versus Frame Cameras for Synthetic-Aperture Imaging: A Simulation Study

In briefEvent vs. Frame Cameras for Seeing Through Occluders: A Simulation Study

SummarySynthetic-aperture imaging removes occluders like foliage by combining many views along a camera path, and has mainly used frame cameras. This simulation compares event and frame cameras viewing the same scene and motion, with a new method that projects each event onto the focal plane. In bright high-speed scenes, the frame camera records only three images and cannot remove the occluder, while the event camera reconstructs the target. At 1 m/s with matched data budgets, the event camera shows no reconstruction advantage, and its data rate depends on the scene.
