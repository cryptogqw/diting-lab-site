Weighting Schedules Govern What and When Score-Based Generative Models Learn from Multimodal Data

In briefHow weighting schedules control what score-based generative models learn from multimodal data, and when

SummaryThis paper studies how score-based generative models learn multimodal distributions during training. The authors split the time-integrated loss into single-time contributions and analyze each at a fixed signal-to-noise ratio, finding that this ratio sets how fast mode directions and relative weights are learned. At high signal-to-noise ratio, all mode directions are learned together on one timescale while relative weights are not learned. Near the speciation time, where the ratio is of order one, every feature becomes learnable on its own timescale. The results rest on an exact high-dimensional analysis of unbalanced and hierarchical Gaussian mixtures and are checked on image and human genome haplotype generation.
