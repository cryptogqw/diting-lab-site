Online Interval Selection with Binary Predictions: Optimal Consistency and a Continuous Robustness Tradeoff

In briefOnline interval selection with binary predictions: optimal consistency and robustness tradeoff

SummaryThis paper studies online interval selection where each request comes with a binary prediction of whether it belongs to a final optimal solution. Requests arrive in arbitrary order, and accepted requests may be revoked, but rejected or revoked requests cannot be recovered. The authors show that the best consistency under finite strict robustness is (3+√5)/2, and give a deterministic algorithm achieving this consistency with exact robustness φ^5.
