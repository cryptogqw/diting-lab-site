Is Sycophancy a Form of In-Context Updating?

In briefIs Sycophancy a Form of In-Context Updating?

SummaryThis paper examines factual sycophancy, where language models defer to users' false beliefs. The authors argue it is better understood as a failure of in-context updating, the process by which models revise answers based on information provided during an interaction, rather than only a bid for user approval. The authors note that reducing sycophancy may also reduce useful updating, so evaluations and mitigations should measure and separate both behaviors.
