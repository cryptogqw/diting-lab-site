Generative Diffusion Models for Secure UAV-ISAC with Real-Time Target Tracking

In briefDiffusion-based secure UAV sensing and communication that hides alert traffic patterns

SummaryThe paper studies a drone that both tracks a mobile target and sends alerts to a ground station when the target enters a protected area. Because these alerts change the communication traffic, an eavesdropper could infer the target's status without decoding messages. The authors propose a keyless scheme in which a conditional diffusion model generates a message-independent safeguard waveform superposed on the data, making alert and non-alert periods statistically indistinguishable. Numerical results with five dB eavesdropper SNR show a learned eavesdropper stays near the 0.5 blind-guess accuracy while the average position PCRB remains between 0.59 and 0.70 square meters.

Homa Nikbakht, Yonina C.Eldar, and H. Vincent Poor

Nonequivalence of $\mathbb{Z}_p\mathbb{Z}_{p^2}$-linear and $\mathbb{Z}_{p^s}$-linear generalized Hadamard codes for odd primes

In briefNon-equivalence of mixed-additive and Z_{p^s}-linear generalized Hadamard codes for odd primes

SummaryThis paper studies generalized Hadamard (GH) codes that are Gray map images of Z_pZ_{p^2}-additive codes, comparing them with Z_{p^s}-linear GH codes. The authors show that the kernel blocks of a nonlinear code H_p^{t_1,t_2} yield equivalent linear punctured codes, and they determine the ranks of these codes. For odd primes p, no code H_p^{t_1,t_2} is equivalent to any of the Z_{p^s}-linear GH codes.

Timing and Reliability Limits of Position-Assisted UWB Multicast Audio

In briefTiming and reliability limits of position-assisted UWB multicast audio

SummaryThis paper presents a unified feasibility analysis for one-to-many ultra-wideband audio transport that separates clock error, age-dependent acoustic propagation compensation, correlated block erasures, and reserved communication resources. It derives bounds on single-listener arrival spread and a stationary expression for all-receiver block delivery, validated through 4,590 Monte Carlo runs. Position assistance markedly reduces acoustic arrival spread, yet most compensated events still exceed the 30-microsecond criterion, and the study identifies failure mechanisms such as position age and shared fades.

Subjective Valuations Make It Hard to be Fair: The Complexity of Resource Allocation to Groups of Agents

In briefSubjective Valuations Make Fair Group Allocation Harder: Complexity of Allocating Resources to Agent Groups

SummaryThis paper studies the parameterized complexity of finding EF1 (envy-free up to one item) allocations of indivisible items when items are assigned to groups of agents who share them. It considers both fixed group assignments (EF1-Fix) and variable ones (EF1-Var). The authors show both problems are NP-hard even with a constant number of agents. They also establish fixed-parameter tractability for EF1-Var with respect to the number of subjective items and for EF1-Fix with respect to the number of item types, while linking an analogous result for EF1-Var to an open question in Integer Linear Programming.

SummaryThis paper proposes an information-theoretic method for lower bounding the prior-predictive conditional value-at-risk of loss in interactive statistical decision making. In briefBinomial distribution maximises entropy among negatively related indicator sums

SummaryThe paper shows that the binomial distribution on {0,1,…,n} with mean np (0<p<1) maximises entropy among sums of negatively related indicator random variables with the same mean and support.
