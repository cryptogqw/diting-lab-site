Exact Sampling Under Campbell Cost via R\'enyi Channel Simulation Divergence

In briefExact sampling cost under Campbell coding, with a new Rényi-based lower bound

SummaryThis paper studies how many bits are needed for exact sampling when the cost is measured by Campbell's exponential coding criterion. The sender and receiver share i.i.d. samples from Q, and the sender sends an index so the chosen sample exactly follows P. The authors introduce a Rényi channel simulation divergence R_α(P‖Q), show it lower-bounds the Campbell cost of any exact sampler, and achieve it up to an additive constant using a new layered sampling scheme. For i.i.d. product distributions under suitable conditions, the gap beyond the leading Rényi-divergence term is (1/2)log n + O(1).]

In briefUsing Bistatic Delay and Doppler to Locate 3D Targets in Distributed Wi-Fi Sensing

In briefBinary codes that fix multiple edits, using polynomial hashing and entropy recycling

SummaryThis paper presents new explicit binary codes of length n that can correct t edits, including deletions, insertions, and substitutions, which is relevant to DNA-based storage and racetrack memories. The authors first build a systematic code with a polynomial hash, using (4t+2)log n + O_t(log log n) bits of redundancy. In briefProof that computing Wyner's common information and common entropy is NP-hard

SummaryThis paper studies two information measures for a pair of random variables with finite ranges: Wyner's common information J(p) and the common entropy G(p). Both are defined as infima over auxiliary variables that make X and Y conditionally independent. The authors prove that computing either quantity is NP-hard, and that deciding whether J(p) is strictly below a given integer is NP-complete on certain tables.
