The Complexity of Exact and Approximate Nash Equilibria in Anonymous Games

In briefComputational Complexity of Exact and Approximate Nash Equilibria in Anonymous Games

SummaryThe paper proves that, for any fixed rational c>0, computing a 2^{-n^c}-approximate Nash equilibrium in an n-player two-strategy anonymous game is PPAD-complete, even under restricted game structures. It simulates circuit constraints approximately using coefficient estimates. For exact computation, a three-strategy construction uses polynomial roots to implement circuit gates exactly. This establishes FIXP-completeness for exact equilibrium computation and FIXP_a-completeness for approximation in distance to an exact equilibrium.

In briefInformation-Theoretic Limits of Spiked Matrix Models with Rotationally Invariant Noise

SummaryThis paper studies the information-theoretic limits of inference in rectangular rank-one spiked matrix models with additive rotationally invariant noise, relaxing the usual assumption of independent noise entries.
