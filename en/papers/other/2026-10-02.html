In briefMultiple Access in Pinching-Antenna Systems: Survey and Impairment Evaluation

In briefWhether OFDM is optimal for aperiodic ranging signals

SummaryThis paper studies the expected aperiodic autocorrelation sidelobes of random communication symbols sent through a unitary modulation matrix, for constellations such as uniform QAM and PSK.
