Generalized Prophet Inequalities for Online Auctions

In briefGeneralized Prophet Inequalities for Online Auctions: Agents with Budgets and Mixed Objectives

SummaryThis paper studies prophet inequalities for online auctions, a setting that classically assumes agents maximize quasi-linear utility. The authors model agents' budgets and varying objectives, such as value maximization in autobidding, as stochastic inputs alongside valuations. They analyze anonymous posted-price mechanisms and generalize the balanced prices framework to incorporate budgets and mixed objectives. The paper shows constant competitive ratios in several central settings, including agents with subadditive valuations, and identifies trade-offs between competitive ratio and price sensitivity. It also gives a general reduction from budgeted settings to budget-free value maximizers.

Twan Kroll, Rebecca Reiffenh\"auser

In briefFrom Pilots to Production: Lessons on Multi-Site Federated AI for Science

In briefDarpan: A Digital Twin Framework for Next-Generation Computing Continuum Decisions

SummaryThis paper proposes Darpan, a digital twin framework for applications spanning devices, edge, fog, and cloud. A physical side runs real applications and observes their state, while a digital side keeps an executable virtual copy updated to evaluate candidate decisions from the same starting point. The authors report a mean absolute error of 0.485 s for predicted response time and 93.1% scale-out efficiency at 40 nodes on real DAG workloads.

Distribution of Age of Information in the Erlang Loss System

In briefAge of information distributions in an Erlang loss system with shared servers

SummaryThis paper derives exact distributions of age of information (AoI) and peak AoI (PAoI) in a bufferless M/M/c/c Erlang loss system, where Poisson-arriving updates from one or more sources share c servers. Three policies handle arrivals when all servers are busy: blocking, preempting a random same-source update, or preempting the stalest one. Using an absorbing Markov chain method, the authors obtain matrix-exponential distributions, which extend to multiple sources affected only by aggregate update rates.

In briefInnovation-Based Sampling: Conveying Information Through the Direction and Timing of New Information

SummaryThis paper introduces innovation-based sampling of continuous stochastic processes, generating a sample only when the process reveals sufficiently new information relative to earlier samples.
