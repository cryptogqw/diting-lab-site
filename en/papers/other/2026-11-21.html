Simplified Plotkin-type Bounds for Function-Correcting Codes with b-Symbol and Sum-Rank Distances

In briefSimplified Plotkin-type bounds for function-correcting codes under b-symbol and sum-rank distances

SummaryThis paper studies function-correcting codes, which allow recovery of specific functions of transmitted messages with minimal redundancy, under the b-symbol and sum-rank distances. Existing general Plotkin-type bounds on optimal redundancy require computing pairwise distances between vectors mapped to different function values, which is often computationally difficult. The authors derive simplified bounds that depend only on the number of message vectors per function value and the sum of pairwise distances among vectors mapped to the same value, and these apply to arbitrary functions. The known simplified bounds for linear functions are included as special cases, and a tighter bound for linear functions under the sum-rank distance is obtained.

Sachin Sampath H., N.T. Rashid Ummer, B. Sundar Rajan

A Proof of Blackburn's Conjecture on Non-Overlapping Codes

In briefProof of a Conjecture on Codes Without Self-Overlap

SummaryThis paper studies non-overlapping codes, in which no nonempty proper prefix of a codeword is also a suffix of a codeword. It proves Blackburn's conjecture that, for every length n and all sufficiently large alphabet sizes q, the best code of the form I^{n-1}J is a maximum non-overlapping code. The proof uses generating functions for the nested partitions that describe maximal non-overlapping codes. The paper also shows that only these codes with |I|=(n-1)q/n, and their reversals, meet Levenshtein's bound.

Yeow Meng Chee

R2WM: Recursive Reasoning Wireless Model for Long-Horizon mmWave Beam Prediction

In briefR2WM: A recursive reasoning wireless model for predicting millimeter-wave beams far into the future

SummaryThis paper proposes R2WM, a recursive reasoning wireless model for long-horizon beam prediction in mobile millimeter-wave systems. It takes a fixed history of beam indices and angles of departure, repeatedly updates coupled latent and prediction states with a shared causal-attention backbone, and then jointly decodes future beam indices. In the standard 10-step DeepMIMO setting, it reaches an average normalized beamforming gain of 0.9597, versus 0.8629 for a prompt-as-prefix large language model, with 99.1% fewer parameters.

Yunxiao Tian, Li Qiao, Ziwei Wan et al.

The Defect Is in the Arithmetic Between the Files: Static Detection of Exactly-Once Violations in Payment Pipelines

In briefStatic detection of exactly-once violations in payment pipelines

SummaryThe paper argues that whether a streaming payment pipeline double-charges a customer after a restart depends on comparing how long a provider remembers an idempotency key with how stale a record can be when reprocessed, and these figures are scattered across separate files. The authors present a checker that gathers these facts and derives their relations, and a synthesiser that turns 335 of 515 findings into runnable replay scenarios. Predictive Self-Entropy in Dirichlet Universal Coding: A Factor-Two Central Limit Theorem and a Markov Extension

In briefPredictive Self-Entropy in Dirichlet Coding: A Factor-Two Theorem

SummaryThis paper studies the Shannon entropy of the predictive distributions issued by sequential universal coders before each symbol is observed, comparing their cumulative sum with the realized arithmetic code length. For finite-alphabet memoryless sources and Dirichlet (add-α) predictors, the authors prove a joint central limit theorem for the two normalized quantities. Both share the same first-order entropy limit, but the cumulative predictive self-entropy has exactly twice the leading asymptotic variance of the code length. The result is extended to finite-order Markov sources via a context/innovation covariance decomposition.

Thomas Sch\"urmann

One Release for All: Private Simple Linear Regression Via Improved Simplex Mapping

In briefOne-Release Private Simple Linear Regression Using an Improved Simplex Mapping

SummaryThis paper extends DP-RSS to private simple linear regression for inputs and outputs bounded in [0,1]. Instead of releasing several related sufficient-statistic estimates separately, it encodes the needed information in a single unit-sensitivity vector and applies the Laplace mechanism once using the full privacy budget. Making Repeated Variational Circuit Optimization Efficient on GPUs

In briefSpeeding Up Repeated Variational Circuit Optimization on GPUs

SummaryThis paper addresses classical simulation of parameterized quantum circuits, noting that training steps change gate parameters while keeping circuit connectivity and reusing a few gate-layer patterns. The authors propose a GPU framework that combines execution primitives for common circuit layers with adjoint differentiation to speed up gradient evaluation. It selects execution plans by narrowing options under structural and hardware constraints and measuring representative layers, then reuses those plans while the circuit structure stays fixed. CCAMoE: Cache-Conditioned Adaptive Inference for Offloaded MoE

In briefCCAMoE: Cache-Conditioned Adaptive Inference for Offloaded Mixture-of-Experts Models

SummaryThis paper presents CCAMoE, a runtime that speeds up decoding for Mixture-of-Experts models whose experts are offloaded on memory-limited GPUs. It drafts tokens using already-resident target experts instead of separate draft weights, and updates the expert cache in step with drafting and verification so transfers overlap computation. It also adjusts draft length based on expected acceptance versus execution cost. The authors report the lowest decoding latency across various models, GPUs, and workloads, with up to 2.33× speedup over state-of-the-art baselines.

Xuan Zhang, Haisheng Tan, Han Tian et al.

In briefAsynchronous uplink rate-splitting multiple access: sending two users' signals in layers with timing offsets

SummaryThis paper studies a two-user uplink system in which each user splits its message into layers sent with deliberately chosen timing offsets, a scheme called asynchronous rate splitting (A-RS). It shows that the offsets make the layers distinguishable only in the excess band of the pulse and gives a closed-form rate expression. By using excess bandwidth that synchronous transmission leaves unused, A-RS achieves rate pairs outside the regions of both synchronous and asynchronous non-orthogonal multiple access.
