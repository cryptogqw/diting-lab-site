Exploring the Tractability Frontier of Hylland-Zeckhauser Models

In briefMapping the Computability Limits of Hylland-Zeckhauser Market Models

SummaryThis paper studies how hard it is to compute equilibria in the Hylland-Zeckhauser one-sided matching mechanism. It shows that allowing three utility values over {0,a,1} already makes approximate equilibrium computation PPAD-hard, while two values were known to be tractable. For separable concave utilities, two shared marginal slopes admit a polynomial-time exact algorithm, whereas three slopes lead to PPAD-hardness.

Ioannis Panageas, Avi Shein, Shayan Taherijam et al.

World Model for Energy Efficiency Maximization in Dynamic Metasurface Antennas

In briefWorld Model for Energy-Efficient Beamforming in Dynamic Metasurface Antennas

SummaryThis paper addresses jointly optimizing digital beamforming and dynamic metasurface antenna (DMA) element weights under time-varying channels to maximize time-averaged energy efficiency, subject to power, phase, and minimum rate constraints. The authors propose a world model framework that learns latent system dynamics from environment interactions and optimizes both variables through imagined trajectories, with feasibility-preserving action mappings and safety cost signals. Simulations show about a 20% improvement in time-averaged energy efficiency over a deep Q-network baseline, with higher sample efficiency and more reliable constraint satisfaction.

Abdolrasoul Sakhaei Gharagezlou, Mehdi Monemi, Matti Latva-aho et al.

Making the Telemetry Pipeline Measurable: An Edge IoT Testbed and Dataset for End-to-End Latency, Provenance, and Energy-Aware Benchmarking

In briefMaking the telemetry pipeline measurable: an edge IoT testbed and dataset for latency, provenance, and energy

SummaryThis paper presents a measurement-first edge IoT testbed and dataset that treat timeliness, provenance, and energy as core telemetry dimensions. The system uses a role-based event-driven architecture, Avro-encoded telemetry messages, and a Mosquitto MQTT broker plugin that records ingress and egress timestamps. The dataset is released as replayable raw Avro streams and analysis-ready Parquet tables. The paper demonstrates one deployment through latency, measurement-context, and energy summaries, but does not introduce a new attribution method or a controlled performance benchmark.

Dimitris Gkoulis

A Unified Queueing-Based Framework for Comparative Evaluation of Serverless Autoscaling Strategies under Bursty Workloads

In briefA unified queueing model for comparing serverless autoscaling under bursty workloads

SummaryThis paper compares three serverless autoscaling strategies: scale-per-request, concurrency-level scaling, and metric-based scaling, under bursty workloads. The authors combine analytical queueing network models with queueing Petri nets built in Java Modeling Tools, using two-phase hyper-exponential arrivals. They evaluate throughput, mean response time, system population, and system power. The study finds that scale-per-request has the lowest delay at moderate load but is fragile near capacity, concurrency-level scaling is sensitive to configuration, and metric-based scaling gives the most stable QoS across the workload range.

Zahra Zafarzade, Ehsan Ataie
