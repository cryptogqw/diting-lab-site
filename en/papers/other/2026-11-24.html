In briefJoule is FLOP: a framework for tensor-core kernel optimization under GPU power limits

SummaryThe paper presents Joule, a framework showing that when a tensor-core kernel's power stays near the GPU's cap, runtime roughly equals energy per call divided by that cap, so reducing data movement can raise clock speed and shorten execution. In briefSequence design for flat-top beams: controlling passband ripple and stopband leakage together

SummaryThis paper studies sequences for flat-top beams, which are used in radar and communication systems.
