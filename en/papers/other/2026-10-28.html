In briefDimension-free exponential entropy quotients over the field F₂

SummaryThis paper studies entropy quotients for random vectors over F₂. Exact Second-Order Asymptotics for the Gaussian Gray--Wyner Problem

In briefExact second-order asymptotics for the Gaussian Gray-Wyner problem

SummaryThis paper revisits the Gray-Wyner problem of lossy compression for bivariate Gaussian sources under quadratic distortion and derives exact second-order asymptotics. It first identifies boundary rate tuples using the explicit first-order results of Yu (TIT 2023), then characterizes the tradeoff among three second-order coding rates under a joint excess-distortion probability constraint. The main contribution is an achievability proof based on a covariance shell covering lemma, analogous to the type covering lemma for discrete sources, and the asymptotic analysis is completed using the Berry-Esseen theorem.
