Distributional statistics of nonequilibrium

In briefDistributional statistics of nonequilibrium: a reference framework for random probability distributions under constraints

SummaryThe paper establishes a reference modeling framework for random probability distributions subject to constraints, generalizing Crooks' hyperensemble approach with Jaynesian priors of maximal uncertainty. It focuses on constraints on average energy and Shannon entropy, examining predictions such as mean and modal microstate distributions and the expected free energy of random distributions. The framework reduces to Boltzmann-Gibbs statistics when Shannon entropy is maximized, and is illustrated through comparisons with superstatistics, generation of random nonequilibrium steady states, and Bayesian inference.

Harrison Hartle, Aanjaneya Kumar, David H. Wolpert

In briefSecrecy analysis and antenna placement for pinching-antenna systems against a mobile eavesdropper

Nemo: Efficient Caching with Index Pushdown for Disaggregated OLTP Databases

In briefNemo: Faster caching and index pushdown for storage-separated OLTP databases

SummaryStorage-disaggregated databases separate compute from storage but suffer network round-trip and page-transfer bottlenecks. This paper presents Nemo, which pushes the index down to storage, caches only hot records at compute, uses a tree-structured record buffer for both caching and buffering, and caches negative search results. It also provides phantom protection that does not depend on the storage-side data layout. Experiments report up to 11.42x and 2.97x speedups over page-based B+Tree and 2-Tree on positive point queries.

Wenjie Hu, Xinjing Zhou, Zhihan Guo et al.

In briefTargeting Interventions to Reduce Volatility in Network Systems (Part I: Idiosyncratic Shocks)

SummaryThe paper studies how to design optimal interventions that minimize aggregate volatility in linear network equilibrium models, such as those arising in network games, production networks, and opinion dynamics. It identifies a general network centrality measure that determines how each node's shock variance amplifies system-wide volatility, reducing to Katz-Bonacich, cycle, and concentration centralities in special cases. Under a budget constraint, the authors solve analytically for the optimal shock attenuation, which has a water-filling structure that targets more nodes as the budget grows. A robust version against adversarially chosen shock variances is also solved and shows the same water-filling structure.

AFRA: Age-Threshold Frame-Based Random Access for Timely Communication in Multi-Agent Systems

In briefAFRA: Age-threshold frame-based random access protocol for timely communication among multiple agents

SummaryThis paper proposes AFRA, a distributed medium access protocol that helps multi-agent systems sharing a wireless channel keep information fresh. When an agent's information age reaches a threshold, it starts a local frame with a mandatory transmission and makes one further random attempt if that fails. The authors prove that, under stated conditions on frame length and threshold, operation converges almost surely to collision-free periodic behavior, and they derive closed-form expressions for throughput, average AoI, and average peak AoI. Simulations indicate fast convergence at small thresholds and lower window-averaged AoI than the other age-aware schemes evaluated.
