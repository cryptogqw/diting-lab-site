Pipelining vs. Combining in Aggregation Tasks

In briefPipelining vs. Combining: Two Strategies for Aggregation in Networks

Sum-rank analogues of Reed--Muller codes over Abelian Galois extensions

In briefSum-rank versions of Reed–Muller codes over Abelian Galois extensions

SummaryThis paper studies sum-rank metric codes over finite Abelian Galois extensions. The authors show that a quotient of a multivariate skew polynomial ring is isomorphic to a product of copies of End_K(L), so one skew polynomial can represent all blocks of a sum-rank space. They define the sum-rank weight via the rank of Dickson and generalized Moore matrices. They then define sum-rank Reed–Muller-type codes and give formulas for their dimension and minimum distance, which include linearized Reed–Solomon, Gabidulin, and rank-metric Reed–Muller codes as special cases.

Alessandro Neri, Giuseppe Zampella

In briefConstructing Non-GRS MDS Codes Using Imaginary Secant Lines
