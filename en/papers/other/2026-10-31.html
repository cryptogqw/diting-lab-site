A density theorem for non-standard irreducible cyclic codes

In briefA density theorem for non-standard irreducible cyclic codes

SummaryThis paper proves a fixed-characteristic density version of the Berger-Charpin conjecture for non-degenerate irreducible cyclic codes. For each prime p, the set of lengths admitting such a non-standard code over some finite field of characteristic p has natural density zero. Consequently, for almost all lengths coprime to p, such codes have only permutation automorphisms generated by the cyclic shift and the Frobenius map. The proof relies on the classification by Feng, Hollmann, Li and Xiang, a normal form for their derivation constructions, and a theorem of Wiertelak on multiplicative orders.

Tao Feng, Henk D. L. Hollmann, Weicong Li et al.

Closed-Form Gaussian Mixture Representations of d-Dimensional Triangulated Domains

In briefClosed-form Gaussian mixture representations for triangulated domains in d dimensions

SummaryThe paper derives a closed-form Gaussian mixture (GM) representation of a finite-volume region in Euclidean space from a simplicial triangulation. On each simplex, the uniform density is replaced by the Gaussian density minimizing the KL divergence, via information projection onto Gaussian densities. Using affine isomorphisms, KL properties, and a partition-of-unity argument, the authors establish a sharp, dimension-dependent but geometry-independent upper bound on the global KL divergence to the uniform density. Numerical examples illustrate the approach and its practical potential.

Ali Darijani, Benedikt Stratmann, Uwe D. Hanebeck et al.

In briefNoise stability on the hypercube at order 7/8: a study of how balanced Boolean functions keep their values steady under random noise.

SummaryThe paper studies the alpha-stability of balanced Boolean functions on the discrete hypercube, defined via the Boolean noise operator for exponents between 0 and 1. Using asymmetric Hellinger kernel estimates recently established by OpenAI, it proves that dictator functions minimize the 7/8-stability in every dimension and at every correlation, and are the only minimizers at interior correlations. Batch Universal Prediction via Conditional Additive Smoothing

In briefBatch universal prediction via conditional additive smoothing

SummaryThe paper studies batch regret, a measure of how well a predictor performs on a fresh test sequence after observing a batch of training sequences from the same unknown source. It analyzes conditional additive smoothing estimators for binary memoryless and first-order Markov sources, deriving their asymptotic batch regret.
