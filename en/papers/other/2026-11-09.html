Resetting-based designated information distribution

In briefResetting-based designated information distribution

SummaryThis paper studies a network of n nodes under random gossip dynamics, where a resetting mechanism with rate β is applied to a designated subset of r nodes. In the small-β and long-time limit, the expected sum of all node values asymptotically equals n/r times the sum of the initial values of those r nodes. The authors also analyze the speed of convergence and provide an approximate high-probability confidence interval for the expected sum at finite time and finite β.

Xiaojuan Ma, Yan Wang, Indranil Gupta

Reference-Dependent Utility Optimization for Goal-Oriented Communication Systems

In briefReference-dependent utility optimization for goal-oriented communication systems

SummaryThis paper proposes a general resource allocation framework that transforms operational rewards through reference-dependent user utilities and evaluates them with expected utility or cumulative prospect theory. Weighted sum-reward maximization, proportional fairness, and α-fairness appear as exact special cases. The paper gives optimality conditions for deterministic orthogonal systems and convergence guarantees for successive convex approximation in nonorthogonal and stochastic systems. Numerical examples show how reference points, utility slopes, channel quality, and budgets shape the active users and allocation policies.

Symeon Vaidanis, Photios A. Stavrou, Marios Kountouris

In briefOutage and diversity limits of fluid antenna multiple access under correlated Nakagami-m fading

SummaryThis paper analyzes fluid antenna multiple access (FAMA) systems under spatially correlated Nakagami-m fading, providing the first exact and asymptotically tight outage analysis. It models the joint statistics of desired and interference powers across antenna ports and derives an explicit integral expression for outage probability. On the Concavity of Rényi Entropy and Rényi Entropy Power along Heat Flows on Manifolds

In briefConcavity of Rényi entropy and entropy power along heat flows on manifolds

SummaryThis paper proves that Rényi entropy and Rényi entropy power are concave along heat flow on Euclidean space of any dimension, for Rényi orders between 1 and 2. The result is extended to complete Riemannian manifolds satisfying a suitable curvature-dimension condition, with the heat flow generated by the Witten Laplacian. The argument relies on functional inequalities recently developed for Tsallis entropy concavity, which the authors extend to the Riemannian setting. The paper also establishes complete monotonicity of order-2 Tsallis entropy along heat flow and derives its spectral decomposition.

Hao Wu, Lei Yu

Mitigating the BICM Loss in MIMO Receivers with Correlation-Informed Logits

In briefCorrelation-informed soft outputs to reduce BICM information loss in MIMO receivers

SummaryThe paper shows that in MIMO bit-interleaved coded modulation, passing only per-bit soft information between detector and decoder discards correlations among bits within each transmit vector, limiting one-shot receiver performance. The authors propose COIL, a soft-output format that adds pairwise coupling logits for correlated bits, and show how to incorporate them into a belief-propagation channel decoder. In a 4x4 MIMO-OFDM evaluation, a one-shot LMMSE receiver with COIL outperforms a conventional unbiased LMMSE receiver by 1.3 dB and comes within 0.8 dB of four-iteration IDD with a single detection pass.

Optimal Proximity Black Hole Search with Programmable Matter

In briefOptimal Black Hole Search Using Programmable Matter Particles

SummaryThis paper studies the Black Hole Search problem in the context of programmable matter, which consists of tiny mobile particles that can self-arrange. The black hole destroys any particle that visits it without leaving a trace. Particles move in a convex area on a triangular grid, and the goal is to identify a restricted set of vertices containing the black hole while keeping at least one particle alive. The authors present an algorithm using three two-colored particles and show it is Pareto optimal in particle and color count, and asymptotically optimal in number of epochs.

Giuseppe Antonio Di Luna, Alfredo Navarra, Francesco Piselli

In briefFixed points of binary belief propagation: finiteness, rigidity, and topological complexity

SummaryThe paper studies belief propagation fixed points of binary Ising models under fixed external fields. In briefHow hard is finding competitive prices when agents are paid to take on chores?

SummaryThis paper studies how computationally hard it is to find competitive equilibria in Fisher markets where divisible chores carry disutility. Compression of Sources with Memory and Private Random Access

In briefCompressing memory-based data while keeping individual items private

SummaryThe paper studies compression of data sources under a privacy constraint: decoding one source symbol must reveal nothing about other symbols beyond that symbol itself. The authors show that for any stationary ergodic source over a finite alphabet, every rate above the entropy rate can be achieved with exact privacy and error-free local decoding on source sequences whose probability tends to one. They also present non-ergodic sources for which private local decoding carries a strictly positive rate penalty.

WhyThe authors extend compatibility of private local decoding with entropy-rate compression from memoryless to stationary ergodic sources and show a strict rate penalty for non-ergodic sources.

Sharp Rates for Optimal Recovery of Bandlimited Functions from Localized Oversampling

In briefSharp Rates for Recovering Bandlimited Functions from Localized Oversampling

SummaryBandlimited functions, whose frequencies lie in the interval [-δ, δ], can be approximated from a finite set of sample values. The paper shows that the optimal worst-case recovery algorithm has an error decaying exponentially in the number of samples n, and it gives a sharp rate depending on n and δ. It also finds that one-sided sample data can reconstruct such functions with exponentially decaying error, and identifies the optimal rate for this case.
