In briefMapping the Computability Limits of Hylland-Zeckhauser Market Models

SummaryThis paper studies how hard it is to compute equilibria in the Hylland-Zeckhauser one-sided matching mechanism. It shows that allowing three utility values over {0,a,1} already makes approximate equilibrium computation PPAD-hard, while two values were known to be tractable.
