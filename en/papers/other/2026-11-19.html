In briefLive migration of streaming DeltaNet requests and the cost of log backlog

SummaryThe paper studies how to live-migrate a streaming Gated DeltaNet (GDN) request by recovering its recurrent state at the correct token boundary on a worker with the same model weights. It combines snapshots, batched replay, and token catch-up, and proposes a compact update-log migration path. In 1,350 pure-GDN trials on two GPU deployments, compact logs gave the shortest exact handoff gap in all 90 configurations, while exact catch-up always released the source earliest and 32-step replay gave the highest output rate when arithmetic differences were allowed.

Relative Entropy under Ordered Chi-Squared Constraints: Sharp Bounds and Extremizers

In briefRelative Entropy Bounds Under Ordered Chi-Squared Constraints

SummaryThis paper derives a sharp upper bound on the relative entropy D(P‖Q) in terms of the two ordered chi-squared divergences χ²(P‖Q) and χ²(Q‖P).
