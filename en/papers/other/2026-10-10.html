In briefIndoor radio channel measurements at upper 6 GHz with directional and quasi-omnidirectional antennas

MSE Minimization for Movable Antenna Enhanced Wireless Sensing via Array Geometry Optimization

In briefMinimizing estimation error in wireless sensing by optimizing movable antenna array geometry

SummaryThis paper studies movable antenna (MA) arrays that adapt their geometry to reduce angle-of-arrival (AoA) estimation mean squared error (MSE) across arbitrary SNR levels. The authors derive a deterministic MSE approximation that captures both local mainlobe error and outlier error caused by sidelobes, and propose three MA structures suited to different hardware constraints. Numerical results show the approximation closely matches the true MSE and the proposed structures outperform CRB-optimized designs by orders of magnitude in the tested low-to-medium SNR range.

Wenrong Chen, Lipeng Zhu, Wenyan Ma et al.

In briefAgentWeave: Efficient Distributed Agent Serving via Flow Decomposition

SummaryThis paper presents AgentWeave, a distributed serving system for agent applications built from LLM calls and tool invocations. Sequential Change Point Detection for PSK Modulation Switching

In briefSequential detection of when a transmitter switches between PSK modulation schemes, with controlled false alarms and delay

SummaryAdaptive communication transmitters may switch modulation schemes at an unknown time, and receivers must detect the switch quickly without frequent false alarms. The paper studies sequential detection of switches between phase shift keying schemes over an additive white Gaussian noise channel, using CUSUM and MOSUM detectors built from the log-likelihood ratio and its low-SNR approximation. For a switch from QPSK to BPSK, the authors derive the moment-generating and probability density functions of the approximate statistic to relate the threshold to the average run length to false alarm and worst-case detection delay. Monte Carlo simulations show the derived expressions closely match empirical results across thresholds.</summary>

Complexity of Autobidding Equilibria in GSP Sponsored Shopping

In briefComputational Complexity of Autobidding Equilibria in GSP Sponsored Shopping Auctions

SummaryThis paper revisits autobidding equilibria in GSP sponsored shopping auctions, following Dütting et al. It shows that equilibria exist under both budget constraints (ABE-B) and return-on-investment constraints (ABE-R), and that they always admit polynomial-size support over the allocation space.
