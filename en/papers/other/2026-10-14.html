Exact Cap Response and Aperture Selection for Molecular Communication under Aligned Drift

In briefExact Cap Response and Aperture Selection for Molecular Communication under Aligned Drift

SummaryThis paper studies spatial reception in molecular communication, where directed molecule transport requires balancing useful capture against intersymbol interference. It analyzes a fully absorbing spherical receiver that counts absorptions only within a prescribed cap, deriving exact impulse, cumulative, and eventual responses from the joint first-hitting law under aligned drift. The responses are checked with particle simulations and time-step comparisons. For a 0.15 s symbol duration, the signal-to-interference difference (SID)-maximizing aperture grows from 29.13° at zero drift to 61.86° at 40 μm/s drift.

Yen-Chi Lee, Chun-An Yang, Nan Yang
