A probabilistic construction of maximum $h$-scattered subspaces

In briefProbabilistic construction of maximum h-scattered subspaces (a combinatorial structure in linear spaces over finite fields)

SummaryThis paper studies maximum h-scattered subspaces, linear subspaces over finite fields that are connected to coding-theory notions such as MRD codes. The authors build such subspaces from random matrices over a suitable algebra. They prove that they exist when the dimension r satisfies a stated inequality and (h+1) divides rn. A Resource Allocation Tournament

In briefA Resource Allocation Tournament: a contest model where players split limited resources across head-to-head matches

SummaryThe paper introduces a round-robin simultaneous resource allocation contest in which players allocate limited resources in advance across matches against all opponents, with outcomes set by relative allocations. It compares a total points payoff with a winner-takes-all payoff. For three players, complete equilibrium characterizations are provided under both schemes. For four or more players, total points admits Blotto-type equilibria, while winner-takes-all is studied numerically, using a best-response dynamics algorithm with stochastic inertia to approximate equilibria for four and five players.
