IRAS Warns Against False, Incorrect or Unsupported Employment Expense Claims

In briefIRAS warns against false or unsupported employment expense claims

SummaryThe Inland Revenue Authority of Singapore (IRAS) has found misleading information circulating that encourages taxpayers to claim employment expenses not incurred or not tax deductible. Taxpayers are told to ensure their claims are valid, accurate and supported by records. IRAS has identified over 3,000 taxpayers making similar claims, unsupported claims will be disallowed, and deliberate false claims may face penalties and enforcement action.

Taxpayers won’t be penalised because of inaccurate details submitted by intermediaries

In briefTaxpayers Not Penalised for Inaccurate Intermediary-Submitted Income Data

SummaryIRAS states that taxpayers will not be penalised if tax assessments must be revised due to incomplete or inaccurate income information submitted by intermediaries, who are responsible for accuracy. Following Grab's correction of income records, IRAS is revising assessments for affected private-hire car drivers and will issue revised tax bills, requiring no action from drivers before receipt.
