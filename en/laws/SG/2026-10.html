IRAS Warns Against False, Incorrect or Unsupported Employment Expense Claims

In briefIRAS Warns Against False or Unsupported Employment Expense Claims

SummaryThe Inland Revenue Authority of Singapore warns that misleading online messages are encouraging taxpayers to claim employment expenses that were not incurred or are not tax deductible. Taxpayers must ensure claims are valid, accurate and supported by records, and non-allowable items such as private car costs and personal meals are listed. Incorrect claims can be amended through the 'Amend Tax Bill' service, while deliberate false claims may lead to penalties and prosecution.

Taxpayers won’t be penalised because of inaccurate details submitted by intermediaries

In briefTaxpayers won't be penalised for inaccurate income data from intermediaries

SummaryThe Inland Revenue Authority of Singapore (IRAS) responded to a reader letter, stating that under its pre-filling of income initiative for platform workers, taxpayers will not be penalised if assessments must be revised due to incomplete or inaccurate income information submitted by intermediaries, who are responsible for accuracy. For the Grab case, Grab has submitted corrected income records, and IRAS is revising affected private-hire car drivers' assessments and will issue revised tax bills, requiring no action from drivers beforehand.
