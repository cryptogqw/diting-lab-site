IRAS Warns Against False, Incorrect or Unsupported Employment Expense Claims

In briefIRAS Warns Against False or Unsupported Employment Expense Claims

SummaryThe Inland Revenue Authority of Singapore (IRAS) has found misleading online information urging taxpayers to claim employment expenses that were not incurred or are not tax deductible. Taxpayers are advised not to rely on such information and to ensure their claims are accurate and supported by records. Incorrect claims should be amended or withdrawn immediately through the 'Amend Tax Bill' service.

IRAS Oct 5, 2026 Guidance#

Taxpayers won’t be penalised because of inaccurate details submitted by intermediaries

In briefTaxpayers won't be penalised for inaccurate intermediary-submitted income details

SummaryThe Inland Revenue Authority of Singapore says taxpayers will not be penalised if tax assessments must be revised because intermediaries submitted incomplete or inaccurate income information, as intermediaries are responsible for its accuracy. In the Grab case, Grab submitted corrected income records, and IRAS will revise the assessments of affected private-hire car drivers and issue revised tax bills.
