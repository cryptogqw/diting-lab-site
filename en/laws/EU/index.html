ESMA specifies requirements to facilitate safe use of post-trade risk reduction services

In briefESMA sets requirements for safe use of post-trade risk reduction services

SummaryESMA, the EU financial markets regulator, has set out the conditions providers and users of post-trade risk reduction (PTRR) services must meet for certain OTC derivative transactions to be exempt from the clearing obligation under EMIR 3. The final report also sets safeguards for providing these services and monitoring use of the exemption. The rules aim to facilitate PTRR services while ensuring the exemption is used under EMIR 3 conditions and the clearing obligation is not misused. The draft Regulatory Technical Standards will now go to the European Commission for endorsement.

In briefESMA's third sanctions report calls for more consistent enforcement across the EU

SummaryESMA has published its consolidated report on sanctions and measures imposed across the EEA in 2025. ESMA promotes clarity in claims on impact and transition

In briefESMA issues thematic note on clear, fair and not misleading impact claims

SummaryThe European Securities and Markets Authority published a third thematic note on impact claims made through funds and bonds. It explains that market participants understand positive environmental or social impact differently, and encourages them to clearly state what difference they expect to make, how they will generate impact, and how results will be measured. The note creates no new regulatory requirements; it offers observed market practices and examples to support understanding of existing expectations on clear, fair and not misleading communication.
