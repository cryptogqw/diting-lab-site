ESMA Oct 7, 2026 Guidance#

ESMA clarifies access for EU market participants to the services of third-country CSDs

In briefESMA clarifies EU market access to third-country CSD services

SummaryThe European Securities and Markets Authority (ESMA) issued a statement saying EU market participants should not be blocked from using third-country central securities depositories (CSDs) after 17 January 2027, until the EU finalises legislation extending the current transitional regime. The Market Integration and Supervision Package proposes a three-year extension, which the Council and European Parliament both support. ESMA expects national competent authorities not to prioritise supervisory or enforcement action against unrecognised third-country CSDs until the extension takes effect.
