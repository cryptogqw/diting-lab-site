ESMA Oct 16, 2026

ESMA finalises the review of guidelines on the delay in disclosure of inside information

In briefESMA finalises revised guidance on delaying disclosure of inside information

SummaryThe European Securities and Markets Authority (ESMA) has updated and streamlined its MAR guidelines on delaying the public disclosure of inside information. The guidelines add five further legitimate interests that may justify a delay, such as a public authority's request for confidentiality. Legitimate interests tied to protracted processes were removed because issuers no longer must disclose such information before those processes finish. The revised guidelines apply from their publication on ESMA's website in all 24 EU official languages.

ESMA updates its manual on transparency with new guidance for OTC derivatives and tick-size regime

In briefESMA updates transparency manual with guidance on OTC derivatives and tick-size regime

SummaryThe European Securities and Markets Authority (ESMA) has updated its manual on pre- and post-trade transparency under MiFID II/MiFIR. The update clarifies which over-the-counter derivatives are subject to transparency requirements, how their identifying reference data should be provided, and adds Q&As and recommendations on the tick-size regime. The updated manual is to be considered a draft until the related technical standards (RTS 2 and the RTS on CTP input/output data) enter into force.

In briefESMA clarifies EU market access to third-country CSD services
