中国证监会 Sep 28, 2026 Enforcement#

湖北证监局原党委书记、局长王广幼被开除党籍

In briefFormer Hubei CSRC bureau party chief Wang Guangyou expelled from the Party

SummaryThe CCDI-led discipline inspection team stationed at the CSRC and the Hubei Supervisory Commission investigated former Hubei CSRC bureau party secretary and director Wang Guangyou for serious violations. Findings include receiving gifts and cash, improperly borrowing large sums, trading stocks, interfering in securities enforcement, and accepting large amounts of illicit property, with suspected bribery. The CSRC Party committee expelled him from the Party, confiscated illegal gains, and referred the suspected criminal matter to prosecutors for review.
