中国证监会就《证券市场先行赔付指导意见》公开征求意见

In briefCSRC seeks public comment on guidelines for advance compensation in securities markets

SummaryThe China Securities Regulatory Commission has drafted guidelines on advance compensation in securities markets to make the process under Article 93 of the Securities Law more standardized, transparent and predictable. The draft sets out who may apply advance compensation and its basic positioning, the main implementation procedures, the scope of services entrusted to investor protection institutions, and coordination among relevant parties. It also states that advance compensation may be considered in administrative licensing reviews and penalty decisions. The public comment period is now open.
