SummaryThe UK Financial Conduct Authority advises investors not to judge a fund only by whether its headline return beats a benchmark. Instead, investors should check the fund's stated objective, strategy and style, and whether it still fits their wider portfolio. United States CFTC Nov 23, 2026 GuidanceCrypto#

CFTC Staff Issues Advisory Concerning the Use of Crypto Assets to Satisfy Financial Resources Requirements

In briefCFTC Staff Advisory on Using Crypto Assets to Meet Financial Resources Requirements

Topic evidencecertain crypto assets, including bitcoin, ether, and payment stablecoins, may be counted toward the requirement to hold financial resources covering at least 12 months of operating costs

SummaryThe CFTC's Division of Market Oversight and Division of Clearing and Risk issued a staff advisory on how swap execution facilities, designated contract markets and derivatives clearing organizations may use crypto assets to meet their financial resources requirements. It says bitcoin, ether and payment stablecoins may count toward holding resources that cover at least 12 months of operating costs, subject to appropriate haircuts. Because crypto assets are not "cash" or "highly liquid securities" under GAAP, they are not eligible for the separate liquidity requirement. The advisory reflects staff views only and does not create new rules or enforceable rights.
