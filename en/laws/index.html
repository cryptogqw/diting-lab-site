Self-Regulatory Organizations; Cboe EDGX Exchange, Inc.; Notice of Filing and Immediate Effectiveness of a Proposed Rule Change To Amend Its Fee Schedule To Update the Definition of Trading Platform

In briefSEC notice: Cboe EDGX proposal to update the definition of "Trading Platform" in its fee schedule

SummaryThe SEC published a proposed rule change filed by Cboe EDGX Exchange to amend the definition of "Trading Platform" in its fee schedule. The current definition covers only registered national securities exchanges, alternative trading systems, and electronic communications networks. The proposal adds a functional, use-based category for any other system or platform that systematically executes orders of persons other than its operator by electronic means. Non-exhaustive examples include broker crossing systems not filed as ATSs, systematic internalization systems, and decentralized blockchain-based or tokenized environments.
