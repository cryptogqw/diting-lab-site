FCA opens the gateway to regulated crypto

In briefFCA opens crypto firm authorisation applications under new UK regime

Topic evidenceFrom today, crypto firms can apply for authorisation from the FCA, marking a landmark moment as the UK takes a step closer towards becoming one of the most trusted places in the world to build and inv

SummaryThe FCA announced that crypto firms can now apply for authorisation under the UK's new regime. Firms operating in the UK will be brought into full regulation, assessed against standards on consumer protection, safeguarding of customer assets, market integrity and financial resilience. Applications should be submitted by 28 February 2027, ahead of the regime's entry into force on 25 October 2027. Authorisation is not automatic, and firms that cannot meet the standards will not be permitted to operate in the UK market.
