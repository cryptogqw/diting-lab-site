ADGM Issues Alert Concerning Misuse of the ADGM Academy Name in Investment and Trading Activities 09 Nov 2026

In briefADGM Warns of Unauthorised Use of Its Academy Name in Investment and Trading Schemes

SummaryThe ADGM Registration Authority has issued an alert that third-party websites and individuals are using the ADGM Academy name, logo and branding without authorisation to offer investment opportunities and trading services. The ADGM Academy is not licensed to offer investment opportunities, provide trading services or collect funds from the public. The public is advised to independently verify any claimed association, especially where payments or personal or financial information are requested, and particularly where funds are sought to be transferred to bank accounts outside the UAE.
