FSRA Alerts Public to Suspected Fraudulent Scheme by E-Community

In briefFSRA Warns Public About Suspected Fraud by E-Community

SummaryThe Financial Services Regulatory Authority (FSRA) of ADGM warns the public about a suspected fraudulent scheme run through the website e-community.co by an entity named E-Community, which has made false and misleading claims. The FSRA states that only FSRA-authorised firms may undertake Regulated Activities in ADGM and advises against dealing with any firm until its authorisation is verified.
