ADGM Becomes the Region's First International Financial Centre to Publish Dedicated Crypto Mining Guidance

In briefADGM publishes guidance on crypto mining, the region's first such guidance from an international financial centre

Evidenceproviding clear governance and commercial licensing direction for crypto mining operators as well as businesses conducting crypto mining activities within or from ADGM

SummaryThe ADGM Registration Authority has published guidance on crypto mining activities in and from ADGM, issued under section 23 of the Commercial Licensing Regulations 2025. The guidance treats crypto mining as a licensable commercial activity overseen by the RA rather than a regulated financial service. It takes a technology-neutral approach covering Proof-of-Work, Proof-of-Stake and future consensus mechanisms. This is the first phase, with a regulatory framework to be developed and consulted on later.

SummaryThe ADGM Registration Authority has imposed financial penalties totalling USD 100,000 on PKF Accountants and Business Advisers LLP and its registered audit principal, Ms Saranga Omprakash Lalwani, for audit failings identified during an inspection.
