In briefFCA: What 'fair value' looks like in practice three years into the Consumer Duty

SummaryThe FCA reviewed how consumer finance firms assess, evidence and monitor fair value three years into the Consumer Duty, and carried out focused work on fees and charges across credit products and supervisory work on home energy consumer hire agreements.
