FCA Nov 5, 2026 Enforcement#

FCA acts to protect consumers facing demands for disguised remuneration scheme loan repayments

In briefFCA acts to protect consumers facing repayment demands on disguised remuneration scheme loans

SummaryAfter FCA intervention, Ethica Resolve Limited, an FCA-authorised debt collection firm, agreed to restrictions. It had demanded repayment of disguised remuneration loans, or purported loans, on behalf of West 28th Street Limited, which bought them from a third party. Under the agreed requirements, Ethica will tell affected consumers they have no enforceable payment obligation for now, will make no further payment demands in the proposed court claim, and will not seek default judgments.

Act now to be resilient in the quantum future

In briefPreparing for the quantum future: UK FCA urges financial firms to start post-quantum cryptography migration now

SummaryThe UK Financial Conduct Authority says a viable quantum computer could break widely used cryptography, so firms should prepare now for post-quantum cryptography (PQC). It cites National Cyber Security Centre milestones: a migration plan by 2028, high-priority migration by 2031, and all systems migrated by 2035. The notice also attaches a G7 Cyber Expert Group statement offering a non-binding roadmap for the transition.

FCA sets expectations for firms making car finance settlement offers

In briefFCA sets rules for car finance settlement offers during legal challenges

SummaryThe UK Financial Conduct Authority sets expectations for lenders making settlement offers to customers owed compensation under its motor finance scheme while legal challenges continue. Offers must be no less than what the scheme would pay. Accepting an offer is full and final, and cannot be referred to the Financial Ombudsman Service.
