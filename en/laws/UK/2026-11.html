FCA Nov 25, 2026 Guidance#

How should investors evaluate performance?

In briefHow should investors evaluate fund performance?

SummaryThe UK Financial Conduct Authority advises investors not to judge a fund only by whether its headline return beats a benchmark. Instead, investors should check the fund's stated objective, strategy and style, and whether it still fits their wider portfolio. The document notes that when a few large companies dominate markets, a more diversified fund may lag a concentrated index without the manager having made poor decisions. It also says style biases should be clearly disclosed and that managers must assess fund value annually.

In briefBereaved families need support rather than a struggle with paperwork

SummaryThe FCA reviewed how financial advisers, wealth managers and investment platforms support bereaved families and those handling estates. It found confusing communications, delays and gaps in support, and some firms still charged advice fees until an original death certificate was provided.
