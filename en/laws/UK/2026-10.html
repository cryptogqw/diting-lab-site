FCA Oct 23, 2026 SpeechSummary of opening part#

Beyond borders: Regulating the risks we share

In briefBeyond borders: how regulators can address shared risks that cross firms and jurisdictions

SummaryFCA Chair Ashley Alder gave a speech on international financial regulatory cooperation at the Michael D. Gill Lecture. He argues that risks now spread horizontally across firms, sectors, supply chains and borders, so regulation must look at shared dependencies that can transmit shocks. He calls for simplifying regulation while sharpening oversight of critical services and risks outside the traditional perimeter. He also urges regulators to share intelligence, test cross-border scenarios and prepare to act together.
