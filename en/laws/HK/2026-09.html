SFC reprimands and fines Zheng Da International Financial Holding Limited $7 million and suspends its responsible officer for regulatory breaches

In briefHong Kong SFC reprimands and fines Zheng Da $7 million, suspends its responsible officer for seven months

SummaryThe Securities and Futures Commission reprimanded and fined Zheng Da International Financial Holding Limited $7 million for anti-money laundering and other breaches between 1 December 2021 and 30 September 2023. Its responsible officer, Zhong Hao, was suspended for seven months from 28 September 2026 to 27 April 2027. The SFC found the firm conducted no due diligence on customer-supplied trading systems and failed to properly monitor deposits and suspicious trading in client accounts.

SFC and AFRC extend regulatory cooperation to financial reporting of licensed firms and funds under new MoU

In briefSFC and AFRC extend cooperation to licensed firms and funds under new MoU

Topic evidenceLicensed Firm means a licensed corporation as defined in section 1 of Part 1 of Schedule 1 to the SFO or a licensed VAS provider

SummaryThe Securities and Futures Commission and the Accounting and Financial Reporting Council signed a new Memorandum of Understanding expanding their cooperation beyond listed entities. It covers financial and compliance reporting by licensed corporations, licensed virtual asset service providers, authorised funds and registered open-ended fund companies, along with related audit work. The MoU sets out a framework for information sharing, case referrals, mutual assistance and coordinated inspections, and supersedes the 2021 MoU.
