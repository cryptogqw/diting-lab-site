SFC reprimands and fines Zheng Da International Financial Holding Limited $7 million and suspends its responsible officer for regulatory breaches

In briefSFC reprimands and fines Zheng Da $7 million, suspends its responsible officer for seven months

SummaryThe Hong Kong Securities and Futures Commission reprimanded and fined Zheng Da International Financial Holding Limited $7 million for anti-money laundering and other regulatory failures between 1 December 2021 and 30 September 2023. The SFC found the firm conducted no due diligence or testing on customer supplied systems used by 160 clients and failed to properly monitor deposits and suspicious trading in eight client accounts. The SFC also suspended the licence of responsible officer Zhong Hao for seven months from 28 September 2026 to 27 April 2027.

New speech by Kelvin Wong: Remarks at SFC and Accounting and Financial Reporting Council’s MoU Signing Ceremony

In briefSpeech by SFC Chairman Kelvin Wong at the SFC–AFRC MoU signing ceremony

SummarySFC Chairman Kelvin Wong gave remarks at the signing of a modernised memorandum of understanding between the SFC and the Accounting and Financial Reporting Council. SFC Sep 28, 2026 Crypto#

SFC and AFRC extend regulatory cooperation to financial reporting of licensed firms and funds under new MoU

In briefSFC and AFRC sign new MoU extending financial reporting oversight to licensed firms and funds

SummaryThe Securities and Futures Commission and the Accounting and Financial Reporting Council signed a new Memorandum of Understanding expanding their cooperation beyond listed entities. It covers financial and compliance reporting by licensed corporations, licensed virtual asset service providers, SFC-authorised funds and registered open-ended fund companies, along with related audit and assurance work. The MoU also sets out a framework for information sharing, case referrals, mutual assistance and coordinated inspections, and supersedes the 2021 MoU.
