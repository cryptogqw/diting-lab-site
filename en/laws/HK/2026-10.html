SFC proceeds to strengthen targeted tools to tackle IPO-related and other corporate misconduct

In briefHK regulator strengthens tools for IPO and other corporate misconduct cases

SummaryThe Securities and Futures Commission has published its consultation conclusions and will proceed with amendments to the Securities and Futures (Stock Market Listing) Rules. The changes allow conditions on listing applicants that continue after listing, and post-listing conditions as an alternative to immediate trading suspension, with streamlined resumption procedures. A new independent merits review mechanism through the Securities and Futures Appeals Tribunal will also be introduced. The SFC expects the amendments to take effect in the first quarter of 2027, subject to the legislative process.
