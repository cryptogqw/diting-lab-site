SFC Oct 8, 2026 Guidance#

SFC to proceed with enhancements to Hong Kong’s retail fund regime code

In briefSFC confirms upgrades to Hong Kong's retail fund rules

SummaryThe Securities and Futures Commission has published its consultation conclusions on amendments to the Code on Unit Trusts and Mutual Funds, taking effect in November 2026. The changes add a Value-at-Risk approach for managing retail funds' derivative exposure, streamline requirements for management companies and feeder funds, and strengthen liquidity risk management and money market fund safeguards. Related codes for pooled retirement funds, MPF products, investment-linked assurance schemes and REITs will be amended in parallel, with a 12-month transition for existing funds.
