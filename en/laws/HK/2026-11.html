SummaryThe Hong Kong SFC published its first Large Firm AI Readiness Survey, covering 20 large financial firms with 78 affiliated licensed corporations, conducted in July and August 2026. SFC Nov 2, 2026 Guidance#

SFC welcomes expanded use of China Government Bonds as margin collateral by HKEX

In briefSFC welcomes HKEX expansion of China bonds as margin collateral

SummaryHKEX's two clearing houses now accept China Government Bonds and China policy bank bonds as eligible non-cash margin collateral, effective today. They have also cut accommodation charges on non-cash collateral to 0.15% per annum. The SFC says the move lowers market participants' funding costs and supports Hong Kong's fixed income and currency ecosystem.
