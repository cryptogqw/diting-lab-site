SFC proceeds to strengthen targeted tools to tackle IPO-related and other corporate misconduct

In briefHong Kong SFC strengthens targeted tools for IPO-related and other corporate misconduct

SummaryThe Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) has published its consultation conclusions and will amend the Securities and Futures (Stock Market Listing) Rules to strengthen tools for handling IPO cases and post-IPO matters. The amendments allow conditions on listing applicants and post-listing conditions on listed issuers as an alternative to immediate trading suspension, and streamline resumption procedures. A new independent merits review mechanism through the Securities and Futures Appeals Tribunal will also be introduced. The SFC expects the amendments to take effect in the first quarter of 2027, subject to the legislative process.
