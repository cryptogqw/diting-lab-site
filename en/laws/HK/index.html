SFC Oct 13, 2026 Guidance#

SFC proceeds to strengthen targeted tools to tackle IPO-related and other corporate misconduct

In briefSFC Strengthens Targeted Tools for IPO-Related and Other Corporate Misconduct

SummaryThe Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) has published its consultation conclusions on strengthening tools under the Securities and Futures (Stock Market Listing) Rules (SMLR) for handling IPO cases and post-IPO matters. The amendments would let the SFC impose conditions on listing applicants that continue after listing, and post-listing conditions on issuers as an alternative to immediate trading suspension, while streamlining resumption. A new independent merits review through the Securities and Futures Appeals Tribunal is also proposed. The SFC expects the changes to take effect in the first quarter of 2027, subject to the legislative process.
