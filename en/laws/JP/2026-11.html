In briefFSA Urges Financial Users to Stay Alert Against Cyber Threats

SummaryThe Financial Services Agency of Japan issued a notice asking financial institution customers to take precautions after recent unauthorized access incidents led to leaks of customer data. It advises not clicking links in suspicious emails, avoiding password reuse, using phishing-resistant multi-factor authentication, and promptly contacting the financial institution about unrecognized transaction notices. An attached National Police Agency letter also outlines basic security measures for individuals and organizations.
