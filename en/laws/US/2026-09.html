CFTC Secures Court Order Directing Louisiana Man and Arkansas Woman to Pay Over $31 Million for Digital Assets, Precious Metals Fraud

In briefCFTC Wins Court Order Requiring Louisiana Man and Arkansas Woman to Pay Over $31 Million in Crypto and Precious Metals Fraud

SummaryThe CFTC announced that the U.S. District Court for the Middle District of Florida entered a default judgment against Brian Early and Alisha Ann Kingrey for a digital assets and precious metals fraud involving the Fundsz entity and its website. Treasury, IRS issue proposed regulations on installment payments for tax on certain farmland sales

In briefTreasury and IRS propose rules for paying tax on certain farmland sales in installments

SummaryThe Treasury and IRS issued proposed regulations for taxpayers who sell or exchange qualifying farmland to qualified farmers and elect to pay the resulting tax in four equal annual installments. Under Section 1062 of the Internal Revenue Code, the election applies to qualified sales made in taxable years beginning after July 4, 2025. National Emission Standards for Hazardous Air Pollutants for Gasoline Distribution Facilities and Standards of Performance for Bulk Gasoline Terminals Reconsideration

In briefEPA proposal to reconsider air pollution standards for gasoline distribution facilities and bulk gasoline terminals

SummaryThe EPA proposes to reconsider parts of its 2024 final rule on hazardous air pollutant and performance standards for gasoline facilities. Topics include operating limits and continuous compliance for emission control devices, vapor tightness for gasoline cargo tanks, and modification criteria in the NSPS. The agency states the changes do not affect the stringency of the standards and are not expected to change VOC or HAP emissions. Public comments and a possible virtual hearing are provided for.
