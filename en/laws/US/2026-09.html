SEC Charges Registered Investment Adviser Zoe Financial for Failure to Disclose Conflict of Interest

In briefSEC Charges Zoe Financial for Undisclosed Conflicts of Interest in Adviser Referrals

SummaryThe SEC settled charges against New York-based registered investment adviser Zoe Financial for failing to fully and fairly disclose material conflicts of interest to clients. The order finds that from about January 2023 to December 2024, the firm did not disclose its financial incentive to refer clients to third-party advisers using its Zoe Wealth platform, and misleadingly described how it mitigated other referral conflicts. Without admitting the findings, Zoe Financial agreed to a cease-and-desist order, a censure, and a $450,000 civil penalty.
