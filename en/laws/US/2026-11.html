Improving Transparency Into Pharmacy Benefit Manager Fee Disclosure

In briefFinal rule requiring pharmacy benefit managers to disclose their fees

SummaryThe Department of Labor issues a final rule requiring pharmacy benefit managers and affiliated brokerage and consulting providers to disclose their compensation to fiduciaries of large, self-insured group health plans under ERISA section 408(b)(2). The disclosures let fiduciaries assess whether service contracts and the providers' compensation are reasonable. The rule takes effect January 4, 2027 and applies to plan years beginning on or after July 1, 2027.

Federal Register Nov 2, 2026 Draft / consultationComments close Jan 4, 2027Summary of opening part#

Unleashing the Power of Unlicensed Ultra-Wideband Devices; Petition of Robert Bosch LLC for Amendment of the Part 15 Subpart F Ultra-Wideband Rules; and Petition of Alliance for Automative Innovation et al. To Amend Part 15 of the FCC's Rules To Permit the Operation of Certain Ultra-Wideband Devices

In briefProposed FCC update to ultra-wideband device rules for modern uses

SummaryThe Federal Communications Commission proposes updating its rules for ultra-wideband (UWB) devices, which were first adopted in 2002. UWB is now widely used for tracking, automation, precision location, health monitoring, and public safety. Because the existing rules have not kept pace, the FCC has received a growing number of waiver requests for modern devices. The proposal aims to add flexibility and protect incumbent federal and non-federal spectrum users.
