SEC Adopts Amendments to Update Definitions of Small Entity for Investment Companies and Investment Advisers

In briefSEC Updates Small Entity Definitions for Investment Companies and Advisers

SummaryThe SEC adopted rule amendments changing which registered investment companies, investment advisers, and business development companies count as small entities under the Regulatory Flexibility Act. The changes raise asset-based thresholds, update how related funds' assets are aggregated, and remove the adviser total assets threshold. The thresholds will also receive inflation adjustments by order every 10 years.

IRS Nov 4, 2026 Guidance#

IRS reminder: Tax professionals should renew their PTINs for 2027

In briefIRS reminds tax preparers to renew 2027 PTINs before Dec. 31, 2026 expiration

SummaryThe IRS reminds paid tax return preparers to renew their Preparer Tax Identification Numbers (PTINs) for 2027, as current PTINs expire Dec. 31, 2026. Online renewal through the IRS PTIN System takes about 15 minutes and costs $18.75, while Form W-12 can be used for mailed applications. The release also describes other PTIN account features, the voluntary Annual Filing Season Program, and the separate enrolled agent renewal cycle running through Jan. 31, 2027.

CFTC Nov 3, 2026 Enforcement#

CFTC Secures Court Order Directing Texas Man and His Company to Pay Over $13 Million in Commodities Fraud

In briefCFTC Wins Court Order Requiring Texas Man and Firm to Pay Over $13 Million in Commodities Fraud Case

SummaryThe Commodity Futures Trading Commission announced a default judgment from the U.S. District Court for the Northern District of Texas against Walter Dunning Larrick III and Cambridge Financial Advisors LLC for a commodities fraud scheme. The court found they solicited and misappropriated over $3 million from participants through false trading claims, Ponzi-like payments, and personal use of funds. The defendants were ordered to pay a $9,767,625 civil penalty and $3,255,875 in restitution, and were permanently enjoined and banned from trading and registration.

CFTC Nov 3, 2026 Report#

CFTC Chairman Selig Releases Statement on Equity Perpetual Futures

In briefCFTC Chairman Selig statement on perpetual futures for individual stocks

SummaryCFTC Chairman Michael S. Selig announced that perpetual futures on individual equity securities can begin trading for the first time on designated contract markets dually registered with the SEC. He said the change lets U.S. participants use these products within the CFTC's regulatory framework instead of offshore venues. The statement cites the Commodity Futures Modernization Act of 2000 and the CFTC-SEC Memorandum of Understanding as its basis.

Topic evidencepermit up to 15% of the net asset value of the Trust’s holdings to consist of certain digital commodities and securities that do not otherwise satisfy the applicable eligibility criteria

Improving Transparency Into Pharmacy Benefit Manager Fee Disclosure

In briefFinal rule requiring pharmacy benefit managers to disclose their fees

SummaryThe Department of Labor issues a final rule requiring pharmacy benefit managers and affiliated brokerage and consulting providers to disclose their compensation to fiduciaries of large, self-insured group health plans under ERISA section 408(b)(2). The disclosures let fiduciaries assess whether service contracts and the providers' compensation are reasonable. The rule takes effect January 4, 2027 and applies to plan years beginning on or after July 1, 2027.
